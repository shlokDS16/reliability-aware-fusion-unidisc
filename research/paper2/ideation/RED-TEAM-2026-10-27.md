# Red-team report: five candidate ideas (2026-10-27)

Two conventions. Severity is `fatal` / `major` / `minor`. Sections headed "Conditions for GO" are included because the orchestrator asked for the minimal strengthening change; they list what the verdict hinges on, not a method design. Everything about UniDisc below is from files on disk (E4); everything about other models and prior work is E0/E1.

## 0. Cross-cutting findings (apply to every idea)

**X1 (major). The only installed backbone is a poor generator, and four of five ideas lean on generation quality.** The one UniDisc image on disk (`smoke_unidisc/joint_image_1024px.png`) is a red bicycle with two saddles and blue fragments grafted onto the wheel; its text is `photoshiko shui copyright 1 2 3 / 02 a / 12 3 / 15 a`. EXP-003 control tails read "the image of the car car on the yaha 91s 93t 95gb springs max car car model". The confirmatory JSON puts debris share of completions at 25-38% and sibling-noun Jaccard at 0.02-0.08. Any CLIPScore, CIDEr or judge number computed on that text will be dominated by junk tails. An Information Fusion reviewer looking at a figure with that caption row will stop reading. Only likelihood-based uses of UniDisc (Ideas 1 scoring half, 3) are partly insulated, and even that rests on the UniDisc paper's retrieval claim (E1, via ideas file A5), which has not been reproduced here.

**X2 (major). Coupling is conditional on the guided sampler.** D0016/D0017 and `confirmatory.json`: plain sampling gives Lambda_b 0.051 (P64) against 0.180 under the official guidance (G), and PC-1 failed under plain sampling. Correction to the brief's wording: the judge's positive control (PC-2, guided T2I checker, matched 0.77 vs cross 0.17) PASSED; what failed (PC-1) is that plain-sampled images do not depend on the completion. So the Qwen3-VL-2B checker is usable on guided output, and the weakness is the generator. Every idea that reads "cross-modal effect" off plain sampling inherits a floor-level signal. Ideas 1 and 3 must therefore state which sampler they use; if guided, matched-call comparisons double-count the unconditional pass.

**X3 (major). One model.** Ideas 1, 3 and 4 as scoped are single-backbone (UniDisc) with optional "second model if time". A 17+ IF reviewer will ask for a second family; an 8B second model costs a 16 GB download (about 2.5 h at the measured 1-2 MB/s, `setup.md` surprise 2), a bitsandbytes install, and unverified Windows/sm_120 behaviour. In 3.5 days the second model is a coin flip.

**X4 (major). Machine-level instability.** `setup.md` documents that CUDA allocations are charged to system commit, that "CPU allocations above about 5 GB crash Python with an access violation", and that free commit swung between 1 and 17 GB because of stale processes. Loading a 16 GB safetensors checkpoint for 4-bit quantisation (which streams shards through CPU) lands exactly in that failure regime. This is a standing risk for every 8B path (2, 4-speech, 5) and not for UniDisc (5.6 GB fp32; peak 8.2 GB measured).

**X5 (minor). Information Fusion scope.** The style guide (section 5) already rates a masked-diffusion generator as "not an obvious fit" with desk-rejection risk. The guide's own list says fusion must be an operational, measured result (any-subset conditioning, missing/degraded modality, cross-modal consistency). Ideas 2, 4 and 5 are sampler/decoding changes; "fusion" is a label there. Ideas 1 and 3 have a genuine fusion analogue.

## Idea 1. GATE (reliability-gated conditioning)

**Feasibility.** Engineering 12-20 h; GPU 8-14 h realistic (ideas file says 12-18, driven by 30k decodes; cut by severities). Requires: encoding real COCO images with the LlamaGen VQ-16 encoder (checkpoint `vq_ds16_t2i.pt` is on disk; the encode path has not been used in EXP-001/003, which consumed generated tokens; E3-unverified), an image-to-text (captioning) mode that was never run here, and a purification-arm VQ decode. **Most likely blocker: UniDisc cannot caption a real image in a way CLIPScore/CIDEr can resolve** (X1, X2). Second: the VQ round trip. LlamaGen VQ-16 reconstructions of COCO photos are lossy, so the "clean" baseline is already degraded.

**Validity.**
- **F1.1 (fatal as written).** The score is r_i = log p(x_i | rest, other modality) - log p(x_i | rest, other modality masked). For a swapped or implausible image token, both terms are low and the subtraction cancels exactly the evidence of corruption; r then measures how much the text helps predict token i, not whether the token is plausible. In the captioning use case the "other modality" is the caption being generated, so both terms are identical and r = 0 by construction. As specified, H1.1 should fail, or pass only through noise. The "PMI removes the frequency prior" rationale conflates an image-context prior with a text-context one.
- **F1.2 (major). The kill probe does not test the core claim.** Token-swap with uniform random codes from a 16384 codebook is the easiest possible corruption: random codes are incompatible with any 2D neighbourhood, so a local n-gram model would give AUROC near 1. Passing "AUROC >= 0.75 on token swap" is uninformative about robust fusion. The realistic corruptions (Gaussian noise, JPEG) tokenise to plausible codes, which the proposal itself predicts will fail. The core claim is the downstream recovery (H1.2), which the probe tests only through a CLIPScore comparison on 100 junk-tail captions.
- **F1.3 (major). Headline metric.** "Recovers >= 30% of the corruption-induced drop in CLIPScore" requires a measurable drop. With captions that are 25-38% debris and a corruption that barely changes the object nouns, the denominator (the drop) may be statistically zero, making a ratio meaningless (a ratio of two noisy near-zero numbers). No power analysis. With 300-500 images and a single stochastic caption per image, seed variance can exceed the effect.
- **F1.4 (major). Garden of forking paths:** K, tau quantile, three fusion arms, four corruptions x two or three severities, two metrics, text-side and image-side variants. The ideas file does not say which is primary or how many tests are in the family.
- **F1.5 (minor). Masking as regularisation.** M2/M4 are listed, and the random-gate and oracle-gate controls are good. But 15% masked conditioning on a weak captioner can change text length and style, which CLIPScore rewards or penalises independently of corruption. The ideas file's own M4 control (CIDEr) needs reference captions on a model whose output is mostly debris.

**Novelty/scope.** Prior art for "mask implausible tokens, then resample" in masked generators exists (Token-Critic, ECCV 2022, arXiv 2209.04439, E1; Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion-Image 2606.29814 edits tokens above a confidence threshold with a corruption-trained model, E1; self-correction 2602.02927, E1). The delta claimed (gate external evidence, not own outputs) is real but narrow; pseudo-likelihood anomaly scoring with masked models is a standard idea (E0, not searched). Fusion fit is good in framing; evidence is weak if only the image side is gated and only one model is used.

**Figures.** Reliability heatmaps, gated mask, purified image strips, ROC curves would all be real and attractive. The caption rows will be poor (X1).

**Falsifier honesty.** H1.1 falsifier too easy to pass (F1.2). H1.2 falsifier (random gate control) is honest.
**Reject the claim if:** the random gate matches or beats GATE-M in paired bootstrap on any realistic corruption; or gating a clean image changes the metric as much as gating a corrupted one.
**Verdict: GO-WITH-CHANGES, confidence 0.55.**
Conditions for GO: (a) r redefined so the corruption signal survives (the specification is incoherent now); (b) the primary downstream test on a task where UniDisc is not asked to write fluent text (the scoring direction, A5), with the generative caption arm demoted to qualitative; (c) the realistic corruptions (noise, JPEG, occlusion) designated primary and token-swap labelled a sanity check; (d) one pre-declared primary contrast and family size.

## Idea 2. Inherited boundary prior (PPC)

**Feasibility.** Engineering 25-40 h; GPU realistically 12-30 h, not the 8-20 h in the ideas file. Reason: the 5-20 s per 4-bit 8B image is unmeasured and optimistic. An 8B 4-bit model at roughly 300-token canvases with CFG doubling costs on the order of 0.3-0.6 s per step (E0 estimate from FLOPs and bitsandbytes dequant overhead); 50 steps is 15-30 s, 256 steps 1.3-2.5 min. 4.5k images is therefore 20-35 h at 50 steps. **Most likely blocker: getting Omni-Diffusion's custom Dream-7B code and MAGVIT-v2 tokenizer running under bitsandbytes on sm_120, Windows, no triton, with X4 commit problems on a 16 GB load.** Fallback model MMaDA has the same install risk plus an unverified discrete-understanding path.

**Validity.**
- **F2.1 (major). The kill probe is nearly guaranteed to pass for a reason other than the claimed mechanism.** Natural-image statistics make border rows (sky, ground, uniform background) genuinely easy to predict, and boundary positions neighbour known `<soi>/<eoi>` delimiters. An early-step boundary-row excess >= 1.5x is expected in any model, including UniDisc. H2.1's discriminator (UniDisc lacks it) therefore tests UniDisc's architecture (2D RoPE), not text inheritance, and the two models differ in everything else (size, tokenizer, data, training). No controlled pair (same image tokenizer and data, text-initialised vs scratch) exists, so M1 versus M2 is not identifiable.
- **F2.2 (major). Calibration removes real signal.** The null-prompt confidence map c-bar_i contains the legitimate dataset prior (sky rows are predictable). Subtracting it penalises true easy tokens and cannot separate "inherited text bias" from "image prior". The 8-bit control on 30 prompts does not address this.
- **F2.3 (major). The harm is rare and the metric is weak.** The authors themselves say "occasionally" (ideas file risk). A DINOv2 top-vs-bottom similarity index on 256-px images, with a left-right control, is an unvalidated metric with no positive control; at a low base rate, 150 prompts x 2 seeds will not separate samplers. 4-bit quantisation alters confidence calibration, which is the quantity being studied.
- **F2.4 (minor). GenEval detector** (Mask2Former, 1-2 GB) is not downloaded and not checked.

**Novelty/scope.** Boundary/position bias in masked-diffusion sampling is published (Uncode/PC-Sampler 2508.13021, E1); Halton order exists; Omni-Diffusion ships its own fix. The delta (images, with a scratch control) is thin and a concurrent paper is likely (ideas file says "high"). For IF it reads as a sampler heuristic with a decorative fusion label (X5).

**Figures.** Commit-step heatmaps and unmasking film strips are the best "real output" figures of the five, if the models run.
**Falsifier honesty.** H2.1 falsifier is not discriminative (F2.1); H2.2/2.3 falsifiers are honest.
**Reject the claim if:** boundary excess is equal in UniDisc and the text-initialised model, or the position-transposed raster does not move the duplication.
**Verdict: KILL as the lead idea, confidence 0.7.** The commit-map figure alone could survive as an analysis panel inside another paper, only if an 8B model installs; it does not carry a paper.

## Idea 3. Two-way evidence fusion (PMI*)

**Feasibility.** Engineering 8-14 h; GPU 6-12 h (ideas file 10-14; scoring passes are about 0.1 s so SugarCrepe is cheap). **Most likely blocker: obtaining the conditional ELBO with the image fully clean and text masked, and the reverse, from the UniDisc API, and the memory of 1024 image tokens at K=16 with batch > 1.** Lower engineering risk than any other idea; no new download except SugarCrepe (small) and COCO.

**Validity.**
- **F3.1 (major). The fusion premise is violated.** Inverse-variance weighting is optimal only for unbiased, independent estimates. Both directions come from the same network and the same joint; their errors are correlated and their biases (ELBO gaps of different size for 1024 image tokens versus about 20 text tokens) are large and unequal. Per-mask sample variance captures neither. Fusing by sampling variance will give nearly all weight to whichever direction has smaller MC variance (the short text side), i.e. reduce to the single direction with a variance-reduction trick.
- **F3.2 (major). Cheapest explanation for any gain:** averaging more masks plus the null-term debiasing (the proposal's own M2), already standard (VisualGPTScore-style debiasing, E0 from memory, id unverified). "Equal network calls" does not rule it out because the fused estimator simply uses both directions' passes. The decisive control is single-direction PMI at equal total calls; its absence makes H3.1 uninterpretable. Also text-only priors still leak through SugarCrepe replace/swap categories (blind baselines are above chance on parts of it, E0).
- **F3.3 (major). Self-verification circularity.** Best-of-4 selected by UniDisc likelihood and then scored by CLIPScore or a 2B VLM judge: any likelihood that correlates with image fluency raises both. N=4 on junk-prone plain samples yields small, noisy gains; with the guided sampler the pool is better but the cost doubles (X2).
- **F3.4 (minor). Sample size.** SugarCrepe 1.5k pairs gives about +/-2.5 points at 95% for a single accuracy; the hypothesised +2 point gain is at the edge. Per-category analysis would have 3-5 times less power. Winoground is gated on Shlok's approval and has only 400 items.

**Novelty/scope.** Delta over UniDisc retrieval plus DiffusionITM-style scoring is thin; likely read as engineering of an evaluator, not a fusion method. IF fit for "optimal combination of two estimates" is nominally good and the compute-accuracy curve is a clean figure.

**Figures.** Accuracy-vs-calls curves and per-word attribution overlays are real but are not the generative unmasking figures the user wants (nothing unmasks).
**Falsifier honesty.** H3.1 falsifier honest; the kill probe ("PMI* <= best single") lacks the equal-call single-direction control.
**Reject the claim if:** equal-call single-direction PMI matches PMI*, or the direction gap (bias) exceeds the per-direction variance.
**Verdict: GO-WITH-CHANGES as a component only, confidence 0.5; KILL as a standalone paper (0.7).**
Conditions: the equal-total-calls single-direction control in the primary contrast; bias reported next to variance; the best-of-N generation arm dropped or made explicitly secondary.

## Idea 4. Equivalence-class confidence

**Feasibility.** Engineering 15-25 h; GPU 12-25 h (ideas file 10-25). The kernel W is 16384 x 16384 for UniDisc's codebook (about 1 GB fp32, fine) but built from decoded patch similarity: each code decoded in several contexts through the LlamaGen decoder, and the result is context-dependent (the decoder has a receptive field beyond one patch), so "equivalence" is not a property of a code. **Most likely blocker: no defensible, pre-freezable definition of W**, then evaluation at 8-16 steps on a generator that produces texture-like images under plain sampling (D0016).

**Validity.**
- **F4.1 (major).** The temperature-smoothing control is the likely explanation (the proposal lists it); a kernel product W p is a smoothing of the distribution, and at 8-16 steps rank changes between the two rules are probably small. Null result probability is high (ideas file concedes this).
- **F4.2 (major). Metric floor.** CLIPScore differences of order 0.005 between rules at 8 steps on poor images; 50 prompts in the probe cannot detect that.
- **F4.3 (major). Forking paths.** Number of W constructions, kernel widths, context sets, step budgets, rules, models.
- **F4.4 (minor).** The speech arm needs Omni-Diffusion (X4, Idea 5 blockers).

**Novelty/scope.** Close to LANTERN / Grouped Speculative Decoding (E1) in spirit; position selection instead of acceptance is a small delta. IF fit weak (a sampler detail). Figures: confidence maps and 8-step unmasking are real but the images at 8 steps will be bad.
**Reject the claim if:** the matched-entropy temperature control reproduces the gain.
**Verdict: KILL, confidence 0.75.**

## Idea 5. Text leads speech (Omni-Diffusion)

**Feasibility.** Engineering 30-50 h with a high chance of exceeding the 3.5 days; GPU 15-40 h if the stack runs. A single canvas with 12.5 Hz speech tokens for several seconds plus text is on the order of 500-1000 tokens; an 8B 4-bit forward costs about 1 s at that length (E0), so 100-256 steps is 2-4 minutes per sample; 200 prompts x 3 arms is 20-40 h. **Most likely blocker (near certain to bite): the dependency stack.** Dream-7B-based custom code under bitsandbytes on sm_120 without triton or flash-attn, the GLM-4-Voice flow-matching decoder plus vocoder (CosyVoice lineage, Windows/Linux-only packages unverified), SenseVoiceSmall (FunASR), plus a 16 GB download at 1-2 MB/s with the commit problem (X4). The brief says "work unattended": a failure in hour 3 of an unattended night wastes the night.

**Validity.**
- **F5.1 (fatal).** Feasibility, not statistics: one model, one stack, unverified Windows path, and the kill probe ("one spoken answer in 2 h") is itself unlikely to be met given the download time alone. A paper whose entire evidence is a single 8B model that may not run by Thursday cannot be planned.
- **F5.2 (major). The core measurement is ill-defined.** "Speech token committed before its aligned text token" needs a token-level alignment. The fallback is proportional alignment, which is wrong whenever speaking rate varies; misalignment noise alone will produce a large "speech-first" share and can inflate H5.1 above 30% with no real phenomenon. The LLaDA-TTS alignment heads belong to a different model.
- **F5.3 (major). H5.2 is near-tautological.** Forcing text to commit first makes the speech condition on committed text, so text-speech WER falls by construction; Whisper-transcript-versus-text disagreement is also confounded by Whisper errors and by the decoder's acoustic quality (the proposal's control "re-synthesise ground-truth tokens" helps only partly).
- **F5.4 (minor).** Dynin-Omni and Omni-Diffusion already use block-wise or fixed-ratio layouts (the 0.25L/0.75L split is part of the sampler), which may mean text is already effectively first.

**Novelty/scope.** Text-speech joint scheduling is the same crowded genre as SC-CMJP/learned-order (E1); delta small. IF fit (audio-text fusion) is the best of the five on paper.
**Figures.** The text-plus-mel-spectrogram film strip would be the best figure of the whole set, but only if it runs.
**Reject the claim if:** default decoding is already text-first under the real alignment, or WER is unchanged.
**Verdict: KILL for this deadline, confidence 0.85.** Starting its 16 GB download in the background costs no GPU time, but nothing should depend on it.

## Overall assessment

1. **Fusion-scope and significance ranking.** None of the five yields a clearly 17+ IF contribution as scoped. Ideas 1 and 3 are the only ones that can be written as fusion. Ideas 2, 4 and 5 read as sampler tweaks with a fusion label.
2. **Realism.** Every plan assumes the UniDisc text side is usable as a task output. The repository's own evidence says it is not (X1). Any plan that survives must treat UniDisc as a likelihood scorer, not a captioner, or be rebuilt around the guided sampler.
3. **Single-model risk.** A UniDisc-only paper invites "one 1.4B model with no licence statement, weak output" as the first review sentence (the checkpoint has no licence, `setup.md`). That is a reputational as well as a scientific risk for a journal submission.
4. **Reviewer's one missing experiment (NeurIPS-style, for the best candidate, Idea 1):** the same gating on a second, independently trained unified model (any family) with a task where its own captions/answers are fluent. Without it the score is "weak accept at best for a workshop".

## Overall recommendation

**Best chance of an honest accepted paper by 1 Nov: Idea 1 as the main thread, with Idea 3's variance-controlled masked-pass estimator used as the scoring machinery (not as a separate claim), analysed on the likelihood-scoring direction rather than free-form captioning.** Report the window as narrow: if probe 1 or 2 below fails, there is no viable idea in this set for 1 Nov and the honest outputs are a negative-result/feasibility note or a later deadline. I give the combined path about 0.35 chance of a clean, defensible result set by Thursday, and a lower chance that IF accepts it (it is a modest result on one small model). Ideas 2, 4, 5 should not receive engineering time before probes 1-3 resolve. Start the 8B checkpoint download (Omni-Diffusion or MMaDA) in the background tonight only as a free option, resumable across the 23:00/00:15 network changes.

### First three probes tonight (each under 1 GPU-hour, in order; stop at the first failure)

1. **Does UniDisc carry real image-text signal on real COCO images?** (about 0.3 GPU-h). Encode 300 COCO val images with the on-disk LlamaGen VQ-16, score true-caption vs a mismatched-caption likelihood in both directions (K=8 anti-masked passes, text masked / image masked), report paired accuracy. Kill rule: both directions <= 55% (then every idea that uses UniDisc as a fusion substrate is dead). This also tests the encode path, which has never been run.
2. **Does the proposed reliability score r detect anything?** (about 0.5 GPU-h). On 200 images, compute r exactly as specified in Idea 1 and plain masked log-likelihood of the observed token, with token swap and a realistic corruption (JPEG q=10 or noise, tokenised). Report AUROC for both scores. Kill rule: r (as written) at chance on token swap while plain log-likelihood is high confirms F1.1 and sends the work back for redefinition; both at chance on the realistic corruption kills the gating story (the paper becomes a negative result). The test on token swap alone must not be treated as a pass (F1.2).
3. **Is there a measurable corruption-induced drop and a noise floor?** (about 0.7 GPU-h). 100 images, one downstream score (the probe-1 pair accuracy under corrupted images; plus CLIPScore of any generated caption as secondary), 3 seeds, clean vs corrupted vs random-gate-of-equal-size. Report the paired SD per image. Kill rule: corruption-induced drop smaller than 2 paired SEs at n=100 on the primary score, or random gate indistinguishable from GATE-M with the oracle gate also indistinguishable (no headroom).

These three cost about 1.5 GPU-hours total, are resumable in 15-minute chunks, and need only the installed UniDisc stack (peak 8.2 GB measured), so none is exposed to X4.
