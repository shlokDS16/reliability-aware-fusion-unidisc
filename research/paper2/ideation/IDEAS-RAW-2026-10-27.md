## Idea 1. GATE: reliability-gated conditioning — unified MDMs can turn untrustworthy evidence back into [MASK]

**Problem and fusion relevance.** Real multimodal inputs are degraded: noisy or occluded images, compression
artefacts, typo-ridden prompts, mismatched modalities. A unified masked-diffusion model has a property no
encoder-based VLM has: it was trained with [MASK] meaning "unknown", over a learned joint distribution of the
*conditioning* tokens themselves. So it can (a) score each conditioning token by how plausible it is given the rest,
and (b) withdraw an implausible token by masking it, which approximately marginalises over it instead of fusing a
corrupted value. This is innovation gating from Kalman-filter sensor fusion (reject measurements whose residual fails
a chi-square test) carried over to token fusion, and it matches the missing-data distinction between imputation and
marginalisation (Rubin).

**Method (training-free).**
1. Reliability score per conditioning token i (image token, or prompt word): with K complementary random masks
   S_1..S_K partitioning the conditioning span (each token masked exactly once; anti-masking as in 2602.21472 Sec. 7.4),
   r_i = log p_θ(x_i | x_{−S_k(i)}, other modality) − log p_θ(x_i | x_{−S_k(i)}, other modality masked).
   The second term (the same pass with the other modality masked) removes the token-frequency prior, giving a
   pointwise mutual information with context. Cost: 2K forward passes (K = 4-8).
2. Gate: G = {i : r_i < τ}, with τ set as the q-quantile of r on clean calibration images (a false-alarm rate, as in
   CFAR radar detection), not tuned on test data.
3. Fuse: three arms — (M) marginalise: keep G masked during answer/caption decoding; (I) impute: re-infill G with the
   model (purification), then decode; (M+I) re-infill only gated tokens whose re-infilled confidence exceeds a threshold.
4. Task heads: image captioning / short VQA with corrupted image; T2I with corrupted prompt (typos, inserted
   distractor words) using the same gate on text tokens.

Key prediction from theory: if the MDM's conditionals are coherent, (M) approximates p(y | x_observed); (I) adds a
single-sample plug-in bias, so (M) ≥ (I) in expected accuracy, but (I) produces a purified image that can be shown.

**Hypotheses and falsifiers.**
- H1.1: r separates corrupted from clean tokens (AUROC ≥ 0.80 for token-swap and patch occlusion; ≥ 0.65 for Gaussian
  noise/JPEG). Falsifier: AUROC < 0.75 on token-swap.
- H1.2: gating recovers ≥ 30% of the corruption-induced drop in caption CLIPScore / VQA accuracy at matched forward
  passes versus (i) no gate, (ii) random gate of equal size, (iii) gating by plain max-probability without the PMI
  term. Falsifier: recovery < 10% or not above the random-gate control (paired bootstrap 95% CI includes 0).

**Competing mechanisms for any gain and the prediction table.**

| Mechanism | Gate on clean images | Random gate of equal size | Oracle gate (true corrupted set) | (M) vs (I) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 genuine detection + marginalisation (theory) | ~no change | much smaller gain | ≥ GATE gain | M ≥ I |
| M2 masking just regularises / reduces CFG-like over-commitment (systems) | gain also on clean | similar gain | similar to GATE | no ordering |
| M3 corruption detector is really a "high-frequency texture" detector (data) | removes clean texture tokens; small loss | — | oracle ≫ GATE | — |
| M4 evaluation artefact: CLIPScore rewards blander captions after masking | gain on clean too, CIDEr flat | similar | — | — |
Mechanisms M2 and M4 both predict gains on clean images; they are told apart by the CIDEr/ref-based metric (M4 predicts
CIDEr flat or down, M2 predicts up). Kept.

**Closest prior work (all E1).**
- Dropout Decoding (NeurIPS 2025): masks uncertain *continuous* visual tokens in AR LVLMs, uncertainty from projecting
  visual tokens to text space, ensemble vote. Delta: our score is the model's own masked likelihood of the discrete
  conditioning token under a joint it was trained on, [MASK] is an in-distribution state, and we compare
  marginalise vs impute; also applies to text-side corruption and to generation tasks.
- Epistemic uncertainty of visual tokens (NeurIPS 2025, AR LVLMs, PGD-based). Delta as above.
- T2M remasking 2604.18738, ReMDM 2503.00307, PRISM 2510.01384, FreeCorrection 2602.02927: remask the model's *own
  outputs*. Delta: we gate *inputs/evidence* from another modality; the theory question (marginalise vs impute) does
  not arise for self-correction.
- DiffPure-style purification (continuous diffusion, adversarial). Delta: discrete, token-level, unified model,
  evidence gating rather than whole-input purification.
- Needs a direct novelty search for "masked diffusion" + "corrupted conditioning" / "robust multimodal fusion"; none
  found in this session (absence is weak evidence).

**Models and data.** UniDisc 1.4B (installed; discrete tokens for both directions, so the method applies directly);
MMaDA-8B-MixCoT in 4-bit for understanding if MMaDA's understanding path is discrete (believed, unverified). Data:

## Idea 2. Inherited boundary prior: why text-initialised unified MDMs paint mirrored images, and a calibrated fix

**Problem.** Anomaly A1: Omni-Diffusion repeats patterns at the top and bottom of images and patches it with a
hand-set logit penalty on the last N_t tokens. Text MDMs are known to decode sequence boundaries first (U-shaped
trajectory, 2508.13021). Most unified MDMs (Omni-Diffusion on Dream-7B; MMaDA, Lumina-DiMOO, LaViDa-O, Dynin-Omni on
LLaDA) are initialised from text MDMs and flatten the image grid into the 1D sequence. Hypothesis: the text model's
boundary prior is inherited by the image span, so the first and last raster rows are committed together early;
because they are far apart and decoded in parallel from similar marginals, they duplicate content. This is a fusion
failure between a pretrained text prior and a new visual modality.

**Method (training-free): positional prior calibration (PPC) + 2D dispersion.**
1. Calibrate once per model and canvas length: run the sampler's first-step forward pass on M null prompts (empty or
   masked text) and record per-position confidence c̄_i (mean max-prob or negative entropy). This is "calibrate before
   use" (contextual calibration for LLM few-shot) applied to positions, not labels.
2. Score for commitment: s_i,t = c_i,t − λ_t · c̄_i, with λ_t decaying to 0 over the first fraction of steps.
3. Optional dispersion term: penalise selecting positions whose 2D grid distance to already-selected positions in the
   same step is small *or* whose mirrored row index matches (cheap set selection, greedy).
4. Compare against: Omni-Diffusion's position penalty, Halton order (2503.17076), plain confidence, random order,
   Uncode/PC-Sampler's positional prior (text), at matched steps.

**Hypotheses and falsifiers.**
- H2.1 (mechanism): early-step commit maps of text-initialised unified MDMs show elevated commitment in the first and
  last image rows; UniDisc (trained from scratch with 2D RoPE) does not. Falsifier: the boundary-row excess (first and
  last 2 rows vs middle, first 10% of steps) is < 1.5× in every text-initialised model, or equally present in UniDisc.
- H2.2 (harm): a top-bottom duplication index (DINOv2 patch-feature similarity of top vs bottom quarter, minus that of
  left vs right quarter as control) is higher under plain confidence than under random order. Falsifier: no difference
  (95% CI includes 0).
- H2.3 (fix): PPC lowers the duplication index and does not lower CLIPScore / GenEval-subset accuracy at matched steps,
  and matches or beats the hand-set position penalty without its two tuned constants.

**Competing mechanisms and predictions.**

| Mechanism | UniDisc shows it? | Null-prompt maps show it? | Effect of transposing the raster (column-major) | Effect of PPC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 inherited text boundary/EOS prior (theory) | no | yes | duplication follows the new sequence ends (left/right) | removes it |
| M2 image-span delimiter tokens <eoi>/<soi> anchor the ends (data/format) | yes if it has delimiters | yes | follows sequence ends | removes it |
| M3 parallel commit of far tokens with similar marginals (factorisation error) | yes | weak | follows similar-marginal positions, not ends | partial; dispersion term needed |
| M4 CFG amplifies boundary confidence (systems) | maybe | no (null prompt has no CFG gap) | — | partial; vanishes at CFG=0 |
M1 and M2 share predictions on raster transposition; they are told apart by UniDisc (has image delimiters but no text
pretraining) — M2 predicts it shows the effect, M1 that it does not. Kept.

**Closest prior work (E1).** 2508.13021 Uncode/PC-Sampler: boundary bias and trivial-token bias in text MDMs; positional
exponential weight and frequency calibration. Delta: images inside unified models, inherited-from-text mechanism with
a from-scratch control, null-prompt calibration instead of a hand-set decay, 2D dispersion, and visual duplication as
the harm. Halton 2503.17076: fixed low-discrepancy order for class-conditional MaskGIT. Delta: content-adaptive and
targets an inherited bias. LADR 2603.13450: locality-aware rescue for T2I speed in dLLMs (opposite aim). Omni-Diffusion
position penalty (E2): the heuristic we explain and replace. Risk of a concurrent paper: high (boundary bias is a known
theme; the multimodal transfer is the delta).

**Models and data.** Omni-Diffusion (T2I uses a 256-token canvas per Table 3 text, E2) in 4-bit; MMaDA-8B in 4-bit;
Lumina-DiMOO in 4-bit if time; UniDisc 1.4B as from-scratch control (installed). Prompts: 300 MS-COCO val captions +
GenEval subset (~200 prompts, object counting/position, which are sensitive to duplication). Metrics: duplication
index (DINOv2), CLIPScore, GenEval detector-based accuracy (needs the GenEval detector, ~1-2 GB; download not checked).

**GPU budget (E0).** Commit-map extraction: 50 prompts × 4 models ≈ 1-2 h. Main: 300 prompts × 6 samplers × 2 seeds
× 3 models ≈ 10.8k images; at an unmeasured ~5-20 s per 4-bit 8B image ≈ 15-60 h — too much; plan 150 prompts × 5
samplers × 2 seeds on two 8B models + UniDisc ≈ 4.5k images ≈ 8-20 h. Peak VRAM for 8B 4-bit unknown (expect 6-9 GB).

**Figures.** F1 commit-step heatmaps (step at which each token committed) on the 2D grid for 4 models — the core
visual evidence (U-shape in text-initialised models, absent in UniDisc). F2 step-by-step unmasking of one prompt under

## Idea 3. Two-way evidence fusion: a unified MDM as its own cross-modal verifier

**Problem.** A unified MDM can score p(text | image), p(image | text), p(text) and p(image) with one set of weights,
so it holds two independent-ish estimates of the same pointwise mutual information PMI(t, i). Current use of such
scores (UniDisc retrieval, A5) takes one direction, a fixed number of random masks, and no variance accounting; Monte
Carlo mask estimates are noisy, and the two directions have very different variance (1024 image tokens vs ~20 text
tokens).

**Method.**
1. Estimators: PMI_t←i = ELBO(t | i) − ELBO(t | ∅) and PMI_i←t = ELBO(i | t) − ELBO(i | ∅), each by K complementary
   mask pairs (variance reduction by anti-masking; same mask used for conditional and null passes as common random
   numbers).
2. Fusion: inverse-variance weighting, PMI* = (PMI_a/σ_a² + PMI_b/σ_b²)/(1/σ_a² + 1/σ_b²), σ² from the per-mask
   sample variance (the sensor-fusion optimal linear combiner for independent unbiased estimates; bias check via
   the gap between directions).
3. Sequential allocation: for pairwise decisions (Winoground/SugarCrepe-style), keep drawing masks from the direction
   with the larger expected variance reduction per pass until the decision's z-score exceeds a threshold (a sequential
   test; racing-bandit style); report accuracy vs network calls.
4. Use as a self-verifier: best-of-N text-to-image selection by PMI*, and as a caption reranker.

**Hypotheses and falsifiers.** H3.1: at equal network calls, PMI* beats either single direction on SugarCrepe
(COCO-based, ~7.5k pairs; 1-2k subset) by ≥ 2 accuracy points. Falsifier: CI includes 0. H3.2: sequential allocation
reaches the fixed-budget accuracy with ≤ 50% of calls. H3.3: best-of-4 by PMI* raises CLIPScore and a held-out
judge (Qwen3-VL-2B yes/no presence, used in EXP-003) over random-of-4 at the same N.

**Competing mechanisms.** (M1) variance reduction from fusion (predicts gain grows as K falls); (M2) the null-term
debiasing alone does the work (predicts single-direction PMI equals PMI*; test by ablation); (M3) the image direction
encodes a "fluency of image" bias (predicts gain disappears on hard negatives that keep the image fixed); (M4) CLIP-like
shortcut of text length (control: SugarCrepe is built to remove it).

**Closest prior work (E1).** UniDisc 2503.20853 (retrieval with single-direction likelihoods); VisualGPTScore
(2306.01879, AR generative score with prior debiasing; id from memory, verify); DiffusionITM 2305.16397 (Stable
Diffusion as matcher); Diffusion Classifier (Li et al. 2023); DUEL 2603.01367 (exact MDM likelihood under deterministic
unmasking — a possible stronger estimator). Delta: two-direction PMI fusion with variance accounting and adaptive
allocation inside one unified discrete model; self-verification for generation.

**Models/data.** UniDisc 1.4B (installed), Muddit 1B or MMaDA-8B 4-bit as second model. SugarCrepe subset; Winoground
(HF gated: accepting its terms needs Shlok's approval); COCO prompts for best-of-N.

**GPU (E0).** UniDisc ≈ 0.1 s per pass (from 6.6 s / 64 calls); SugarCrepe 1.5k pairs × 2 captions × 2 directions × 2

## Idea 4. Equivalence-class confidence for visual and speech tokens

**Problem.** Confidence rules (max-prob, margin, entropy) treat codebook entries as unrelated symbols. Visual and speech
codebooks contain many near-interchangeable codes, so probability spread over several equivalent codes looks like
uncertainty and delays or misorders commitment; a token that is "certain up to an equivalent code" is ranked low.
This is a fusion-of-token-space-geometry-into-the-sampler problem, and it matters more at few steps (many tokens per
step), the regime unified models advertise.

**Method.** Precompute a code-equivalence kernel W (8192×8192 for MAGVIT-v2) once from *decoded* patch similarity
(decode each code in a few fixed contexts; GSD found raw embedding distances near-uniform, so embeddings alone are not
used). Confidence c_i = max_k Σ_j W_kj p_i(j) (mass of the best soft class), entropy on the class-smoothed
distribution; the value rule is unchanged (sample the code itself). Analogy: semantic entropy (meaning-level
uncertainty) for language outputs.

**Hypotheses/falsifiers.** H4.1: class-confidence ranks tokens differently from max-prob (Kendall τ < 0.8 in early
steps). H4.2: at 8-16 steps it raises CLIPScore/GenEval-subset at matched steps; at 50+ steps no difference. Falsifier:
no gain at 8-16 steps (CI includes 0). Competing mechanisms: genuine geometry use; mere temperature-like flattening of
confidence (control: rank by max-prob of a temperature-smoothed distribution with matched entropy); spatial effect
(class-confident tokens happen to be background; control: report commit maps).

**Closest prior work (E1).** LANTERN 2410.03355 and Grouped Speculative Decoding (ICCV 2025): latent-proximity sets for
*acceptance* in AR speculative decoding; IAR 2501.00880 and coarse-to-fine 2503.16194: codebook clusters in AR
*training*; Chatterbox-Flash 2605.30748: PMI-calibrated ordering for speech MDM; MaskFocus (CVPR 2026): entropy routing
in RL. Delta: codebook-equivalence confidence for *position selection* in unified masked diffusion, image and speech.

**Models/data.** UniDisc, MMaDA-8B 4-bit (MAGVIT-v2), Muddit; 300 COCO prompts, GenEval subset. Optional speech arm on
Omni-Diffusion TTS (LibriTTS subset, Whisper WER).

**GPU (E0).** Kernel precompute ≈ 1 h; 300 prompts × 3 step budgets × 3 rules × 2 seeds × 2 models ≈ 10.8k images ≈
10-25 h. **Figures.** Confidence maps (max-prob vs class) at step 1; step-by-step unmasking at 8 steps under both rules;
quality-vs-steps curves; T2I grids at 8/16 steps with metrics. **Risks.** Gain may equal the temperature control (then
null result); W construction choices are many (garden of forking paths: freeze before outcomes).
**IF appeal.** Moderate (geometry-aware decision fusion); cleanest "method" story.
**Kill probe.** 50 prompts at 8 steps on UniDisc: kill if rank correlation between rules > 0.9 or no visible CLIPScore
difference versus the matched-temperature control.

## Idea 5. Text leads speech: modality-calibrated commitment in one-canvas spoken responses (Omni-Diffusion)

**Problem (anomaly A4).** Omni-Diffusion writes a text answer and its spoken version in one masked canvas (special
token pre-infilling at 0.25L) and decodes both with one global entropy ranking. Text (Dream vocabulary ~150k) and
speech (16384 codes at 12.5 Hz) have different entropy scales, so the ranking decides which modality leads without
anyone choosing it. If speech commits before the text that it should voice, the two can disagree. LLaDA-TTS
(2603.26364) shows emergent text-speech alignment heads, suggesting a usable alignment signal.

**Method.** (a) Measure: commit-time distributions per modality and per aligned text-speech segment. (b) Calibrated
selection: rank within modality by quantile of its own confidence history (rank-normalisation) and set a lead δ —
speech position j is eligible only after its aligned text span (from the alignment heads, or proportional alignment
as fallback) is committed (a soft monotone gate). Sweep δ ∈ {speech-first, none, text-first}.

**Hypotheses/falsifiers.** H5.1: under the default sampler, the share of speech tokens committed before their aligned
text tokens is > 30%. H5.2: text-first gating lowers text-speech disagreement, measured as WER between Whisper's
transcript of the speech and the generated text, at matched steps. Falsifier: disagreement unchanged (CI includes 0) or
default already text-first (then the anomaly is not real; kill).
Competing mechanisms: alignment (predicts gain concentrated in later segments); global text quality (control: text-only
answer quality unchanged); speech decoder artefacts (control: re-synthesise ground-truth tokens).

**Closest prior work (E1).** SC-CMJP 2607.13188 (coupled rates for text-image), learned order 2607.08056 (text-image),
Dynin-Omni 2604.00007 (block-wise decoding for speech), LLaDA-TTS 2603.26364, Chatterbox-Flash 2605.30748, "Decoding
order matters in AR speech synthesis" 2601.08450. Delta: text-speech in one canvas, alignment-gated cross-modal lead.

**Models/data.** Omni-Diffusion 8B in 4-bit + GLM-4-Voice decoder (size not checked) + SenseVoiceSmall; Whisper
large-v3 (local). Spoken QA prompts: text questions from a VQA/QA set (speech input needs CosyVoice2 synthesis — avoid
by text-in, text+speech-out). 200 prompts.

**GPU (E0).** Unknown; likely 8-15 h if the stack runs. **Figures.** Joint text+spectrogram unmasking film strip
(text tokens and mel-spectrogram filling in over steps) — the most striking figure of all ideas; commit-time scatter
(text vs aligned speech); disagreement vs δ. **Risks.** Highest engineering risk (8B 4-bit + speech decoder + Windows +
custom code; the GLM-4-Voice decoder may need Linux-only dependencies); one model only.
**IF appeal.** Audio-text fusion, temporal alignment, strong figures. **Kill probe (2 h).** Get one spoken answer out of
Omni-Diffusion in 4-bit on this laptop; if not running within 2 h, kill.

## Idea 6. Who wins when modalities disagree? Step-resolved modality dominance in unified MDMs

**Problem.** Fusion under conflicting evidence is a core Information Fusion topic. In AR VLMs the language prior often
overrides vision. Unified MDMs commit tokens in a data-dependent order, so the "winner" of a conflict may be decided at
a measurable step. Construct controlled conflicts: an image of a red car plus a text prefix "a blue car"; an inpainting
canvas whose visible half contradicts the prompt.

**Method/analysis.** For each conflict pair, run the sampler and record, per step, the log-odds of the image-consistent
vs text-consistent attribute token(s) (and, for image generation, a detector's colour/object read-out on decoded
partial images). Define the decision step (first step after which the winning attribute's probability stays > 0.9).
Then a control: per-modality guidance weights (w_img, w_txt) to steer dominance, and a "conflict alarm" from the
divergence between the two single-modality-masked predictions (a cheap fusion conflict measure, analogous to
Dempster-Shafer conflict mass K).

**Hypotheses/falsifiers.** H6.1: decisions are made in the first 20% of steps for > 70% of conflicts. H6.2: the conflict
alarm predicts which modality wins (AUROC ≥ 0.7). Falsifiers: decision steps uniform; AUROC < 0.6.
Competing mechanisms: early layout commitment; token-frequency prior (colour words frequent); CFG direction.
**Prior work (E1).** AR VLM modality-conflict studies (not searched this session, likely numerous); UVC; 2610.15227
(attention probing of CFG branches in unified VLMs incl. a masked-diffusion one). **Delta:** step-resolved decision
timing and a conflict alarm in masked diffusion. **Models/data.** UniDisc, MMaDA 4-bit; 300 synthetic conflict pairs
from COCO (colour/object/count swaps). **GPU (E0).** 6-12 h. **Figures.** Log-odds trajectories over steps; partial
image strips showing the attribute flip; dominance maps vs (w_img, w_txt). **Risks.** Mostly analysis; method weak;
novelty search not done. **Kill probe.** 50 conflicts on UniDisc: kill if the attribute log-odds are flat (model
ignores one modality entirely).

## Idea 7. Fusion maps and information-triggered guidance

**Problem.** CFG doubles cost and, per A3, trades WER against FAD in TTS. UniDisc's appendix (E1) shows the
conditional-unconditional gap shrinks as tokens are decoded. If cross-modal information per position and step can be
predicted cheaply, the unconditional pass can be skipped where it carries no information.

**Method.** Measure I_i,t = KL(p(·|x_t, c) || p(·|x_t, ∅)) (a per-token cross-modal information map). Trigger guidance
for a step only when a running estimate of Σ_i I_i,t over still-masked tokens exceeds a threshold; reuse the last
unconditional logits otherwise. **Hypothesis.** Same CLIPScore/WER at ≤ 60% of network calls; in TTS, reduced FAD
penalty at matched WER. **Falsifier.** Quality drops before 80% of calls are saved.
**Prior work (E1).** A-CFG 2505.20199, CFG-schedule theory 2507.08965, guidance-interval work for continuous diffusion,
KLASS 2511.05664 (KL-based unmasking). **Delta** thin: information-triggered skipping in unified multimodal MDMs.
**GPU.** 6-10 h on UniDisc + one 8B model. **Figures.** Per-step fusion maps over the image grid; calls-vs-quality
Pareto. **Risks.** Low novelty; likely judged incremental. **Kill probe.** If I_i,t is not concentrated (top 20% of
steps hold < 50% of total information), kill.

## Cross-cutting notes

- **Structural hole found.** Cluster A: robustness/uncertainty of visual evidence in AR LVLMs (Dropout Decoding, VCD,
  epistemic visual-token uncertainty). Cluster B: remasking/self-correction of *outputs* in MDMs (ReMDM, T2M, PRISM,
  FreeCorrection). No work found connecting B's machinery to A's problem — remasking *inputs* from another modality.
  This is Idea 1's bridge question: "Can a unified MDM's [MASK] state serve as a principled missing-evidence state for
  robust fusion?"
- **Assumption inversion used.** Idea 1 inverts "conditioning tokens are given and trusted"; Idea 2 inverts "a text
  prior is neutral for images"; Idea 3 inverts "one direction of the likelihood is enough"; Idea 4 inverts "codes are
  unrelated symbols"; Idea 5 inverts "one global confidence scale fits all modalities".
- **Shared infrastructure.** Ideas 1, 3 and 6 run on the installed UniDisc stack; ideas 2 and 4 share a sampler hook
  (score override + commit-map logging). A practical package: Idea 1 as the main method, Idea 2's commit maps as a
  secondary analysis figure, if the orchestrator wants a fallback inside the same paper.
- **Evaluation hygiene for all.** Preregister arms, matched network calls, seeds, paired bootstrap; CLIPScore alone is
  not enough (pair with a reference metric or a judge whose positive control passes — EXP-003's checker positive
  control failed, D0017).
