## Decisions I made that the SAP does not spell out (for the gate-auditor)
- D1. `location_supported` is kept as a field but now means eligibility. Callers that read it get the stricter meaning.
- D2. "Partial prefix" applies when 120 <= completed n < 0.8 x `--n` and the planned fold is not complete (a completed list of 298 is not a partial prefix); `--n` is the nominal plan (300, or 150 if Shlok declines).
- D3. The sensitivity's "rejection" for the sensitive-to-n statement: favourable direction and p x m < 0.05 (S2 m = 2, i.e. the Holm-m = 2 threshold of the A3.1 table; B m = 1). The SAP says "opposite sides of rejection" without defining the sensitivity's threshold. It enters no Holm set and no claim.
- D4. B sensitivity "covers fewer than 150 positions" is read as max pool position + 1 < 150 among the completed finite rows.
- D5. In the refuted-by-draw-level-but-kept-draw-rejects case the reading is "refuted" and the kept-draw sentence is not emitted (A3b.1 item 3 reads "the statement 'repairing the flagged set beats repairing random sets' is not available").
