# TNNLS design A: training-free scoring for unified masked discrete diffusion (2026-10-29)

Tags: **OURS** number from a run in this repo (path given), **SF** source fact, **INF** inference, **HYP** hypothesis,
**E0** my derivation or arithmetic. Nothing here is a result of the proposed method. Planned is not done.

## 0. Verdict in ten lines

1. **The seed idea does not survive as the headline.** Under a Bayes-coherent joint, the image-direction ratio
   log p(v|c+) - log p(v|c-) *equals* the text PMI ratio; the two directions estimate one quantity, so fusion can only
   average estimation errors (Bates-Granger), never add information. On FOIL fit pairs the image direction is weak
   (0.661 vs 0.887), incoherent in scale (5x) and LDA gives it zero weight (OURS, sec. 1). Keep it as an ablation arm.
2. **Rank 1, SCER (shared-context exact ratio):** for an edited caption pair, the log-likelihood ratio equals the ratio
   of the edit-span conditionals given the shared context; an MDM reads both candidates of a one-token edit from **one
   softmax** (one pass), with zero Monte-Carlo variance, plus an in-distribution blind null (image fully masked, trained
   via `mask_entire_modality`). Replaces 16-1024 ELBO passes or an N-step chain (MDC 2510.26799).
3. **Rank 2, coherence-weighted bidirectional fusion** (the seed, reformulated): only as an arm inside the SCER runs (no
   extra GPU); demoted unless it gains >= 1 point on two dev sets.
4. **Rank 3, GATE-H** (conformal reliability gating on hard negatives under corruption): the existing method, judged by
   family H tonight; robustness section at most, never the headline (SAP AB.1 rules apply).
5. **Theory (P1-P5):** exactness and variance of SCER; Bayes rule for a two-alternative test with an editor (when PMI
   with alpha = 1 is optimal, why raw likelihood wins on add_*); full-mask scoring is a positional bag of words, at chance
   on marginal-preserving swaps; flip-invariance impossibility for left/right; fusion gain condition. All elementary;
   P3 is known in substance (VisualGPTScore 2306.01879), P5's algebra is classical. Novelty is the estimator for this
   model class plus the testable predictions, not deep theory.
6. **Datasets:** SugarCrepe (MIT, COCO val2017, local images) primary; FOIL (fresh pairs, not family B's test pairs);
   What'sUp COCO-spatial (verify); Winoground optional (gated; Shlok must accept terms) as the only non-COCO test.
7. **Baselines on 12 GB:** CLIP ViT-L/14, SigLIP so400m, NegCLIP, BLIP-2 ITM, VQAScore (CLIP-FlanT5-XL), Janus-1.3B
   likelihood + text-only prior, and on UniDisc itself: full-mask PMI, MC-ELBO with CRN, MDC confidence chain.
8. **Pilot:** 1.6 GPU-h, kill rules K1-K6 (sec. 7). **Full plan incl. pilot:** about 12-16 attended GPU-h without a second

## 2. Notation

v image tokens (1,024 at 512 px, vocab 16,384); c = (c_1..c_n) caption tokens in the padded text block; c+, c- the
positive and the edited negative; S the edit set: positions where the token-aligned c+ and c- differ, defined only when
both have equal token length (else the row is "unaligned"); c_{-S} the shared context; empty-set symbol: image fully
masked; p_theta the model's masked conditionals; t the mask fraction.

## 3. Candidates (ranked)

### 3.1 Rank 1: SCER, shared-context exact ratio

**Score.** For an aligned pair with |S| = k and an order sigma of S,

  R(v; sigma) = sum_{i=1..k} [ log p_theta(c+_{s_i} | c_{-S}, c+_{s_<i}, masks at s_>i, v) - log p_theta(c-_{s_i} | c_{-S}, c-_{s_<i}, masks at s_>i, v) ]

  SCER_alpha(v) = mean_sigma R(v; sigma) - alpha * mean_sigma R(empty; sigma),   decide c+ iff SCER_alpha > 0.

alpha = 1 is the preregistered primary (span PMI); alpha = 0 (raw ratio) is reported beside it. Orders: all k! for
k <= 3, else R = 4 random orders with a fixed seed.

**Passes.** k = 1: one pass with s masked gives both log p(c+_s|.) and log p(c-_s|.) from the same softmax; one more with
the image masked for the null: **2 passes per pair** (the current T uses 2 passes plus a cached null). k = 2 (typical swap):
1 shared pass (both masked) + 2 (one per candidate's first token) per order, so 5 passes for both orders, x2 with the
null = 10. Tree sharing across candidates and orders is part of the algorithm.

**Unaligned rows** (different token length, all add_* rows and any replace with a multi-token word): no exact identity
(positions shift). Use the order-averaged chain over the minimal differing window with each caption's own layout; label
these rows "approximate" and report them separately. add_* rows also get the length-only control.

**Algorithm (staged):** (1) tokenise both captions with the runtime tokenizer; (2) align (token-level Levenshtein with
substitution only; if lengths differ, mark unaligned); (3) build the pass tree for S and the orders; (4) one batched
forward call per tree level, with image visible, then image masked; (5) gather the candidate log-probs; (6) average over
orders; (7) SCER_1 and SCER_0. No parameters are fitted. Diagnostics stored per row: order spread (max - min of R over
orders), k, aligned flag.

**Why it should win here (and against what).** (i) Against MC-ELBO text scoring (MDC Monte Carlo, 1,024 masks there):
the shared tokens contribute variance and an ELBO-gap difference to every MC estimate; SCER has neither for k = 1 (P1,
P2). (ii) Against full-mask one-pass PMI (our T): all-masked scoring loses the binding between attribute and object
(P4); SCER conditions on two-sided context at the edit. (iii) Against CLIP/SigLIP: dual encoders pool to one vector and
are known to behave like bags of words on order and relation edits (NegCLIP/ARO 2210.01936, SugarCrepe 2306.14610); a
cross-attending generative conditional at the edit position can bind. (iv) It will not beat BLIP-2 ITM or VQAScore
because those are trained on far more data with hard negatives or instruction data; the paper says so.

**What would make me drop it:** pilot K1 or K2 fails (sec. 7).

### 3.2 Rank 2: coherence-weighted bidirectional fusion (the seed, reformulated)

Under a coherent joint, both directions estimate the same log ratio on the same nat scale:
  R_I = sum_j [log p(v_j | ..., c+) - log p(v_j | ..., c-)]  and  R_T = SCER_1.
Fused score F = w R_T + (1 - w) R_I with w = (s_I^2 - s_TI)/(s_T^2 + s_I^2 - 2 s_TI) (minimum-variance combination of two
estimators of one estimand, Bates and Granger 1969, J. Oper. Res. Soc., DOI to be verified), estimated by cross-fitting
on a fit split; per-direction reliability gating (GATE's flags) applied to R_I only. Costs no extra pass (R_I is two
passes already run). Evidence so far is against it (sec. 1). **Demote** to one ablation line unless F beats the better
single direction by >= 1 point on both dev sets (K6). Do not claim "Bayes-coherent fusion" as new: the coherence identity
is Bayes' rule and the combination rule is 1969.

## 5. Propositions (to prove; sketches)

**P1 (exactness).** If p_theta(.|v) is the conditional family of one joint q(c|v) (coherence), then for aligned c+, c-
and any order, R(v; sigma) = log q(c+|v) - log q(c-|v). *Sketch:* chain rule; q(c_{-S}|v) is common and cancels. Without
coherence, the order spread of R bounds the error to the best coherent approximation from below (if two orders differ by
delta, no joint reproduces both, so |error| >= delta/2 for at least one order); averaging orders gives the
minimum-variance mixture under exchangeable order errors. Diagnostic: report the order spread.

**P2 (variance and ELBO bias of the competitor).** For the MC-ELBO difference with common random masks over M draws,
D_M = (1/M) sum_m [h(c+; omega_m) - h(c-; omega_m)], Var D_M = sigma_CRN^2 / M with sigma_CRN^2 including the
shared-context terms, and E D_M = [ELBO(c+) - ELBO(c-)], which differs from the true log ratio by the difference of the
two ELBO gaps (sign not controlled). SCER at k = 1 has zero MC variance and no gap. Accuracy of a decision on a margin
mu with item spread tau and MC noise: Phi(mu / sqrt(tau^2 + sigma_CRN^2/M)); this gives the number of masks M needed to
match SCER, a testable curve (fig. F2).

**P3 (Bayes rule for an edited two-alternative test).** Let the benchmark draw (v, c) from the data law, then c' from an
editor e(c'|c), and present {c, c'} in random order. The posterior that c is the original is
p(v|c) p(c) e(c'|c) / [p(v|c) p(c) e(c'|c) + p(v|c') p(c') e(c|c')]. Hence (a) if p(c) e(c'|c) = p(c') e(c|c') (a text-only
observer is at chance, which is what SugarCrepe's adversarial refinement targets), the Bayes rule is the image
likelihood ratio = span PMI, alpha = 1; (b) if the editor is asymmetric (add_*: the edit produces less probable, longer
captions), the raw likelihood (alpha = 0) carries the editor term and beats PMI through text alone. Prediction: alpha* ~ 1
on replace/swap, alpha* ~ 0 on add, blind accuracy ~ 0.5 on replace/swap and >> 0.5 on add. Credit 2306.01879 for
alpha-debiasing.

**P4 (full-mask scoring is a positional bag of words).** With all caption positions masked (one pass), the score is
sum_j log m_j(c_j | v, layout), m_j the positional marginal. For a swap that transposes tokens a, b at positions i, l,
the margin is [log m_i(a) - log m_i(b)] - [log m_l(a) - log m_l(b)]; if m_i = m_l on {a, b} it is 0 (chance), whatever
the image says about which attribute binds to which object. SCER conditions on the bound context and is not subject to
this. Prediction: full-mask PMI near chance on swap_att, high on replace_obj; SCER above it on swap (fig. F4, accuracy as
the masked share of the caption goes from |S|/n to 1).

**P5 (flip-invariance impossibility).** If the training law is invariant under horizontal flip of the image with the
caption unchanged and p_theta learns it, then log p(c_left|v) - log p(c_right|v) = same quantity at flip(v); on any
left/right test set closed under flipping with labels swapped, expected accuracy of every score built from p_theta is
1/2. Audit: correlation of the margin at v and at flip(v) (+1 = invariant, -1 = equivariant). UniDisc's data code applies
RandomHorizontalFlip in several pipelines (models/third_party/unidisc/models/datasets/image_datasets.py:447,456,
webdataset_utils.py:479); whether the released model used it is unverified. Prediction: left/right near chance,
above/below not.

**P6 (fusion gain, rank 2).** For Gaussian margins with SNRs d_T, d_I and correlation rho, the LDA-fused SNR is
sqrt((d_T^2 + d_I^2 - 2 rho d_T d_I)/(1 - rho^2)), which exceeds max(d_T, d_I) iff d_I != rho d_T (for d_T > d_I);
classical, used only to state when rank 2 can help. Measured on FOIL fit: d_I = 0.415, rho d_T = 0.375 (sec. 1).

Theory budget: P1, P3, P4, P5 full proofs in an appendix (each under a page); P2 and P6 as lemmas with citations.

## 10. Limitations section seed

- One masked backbone (two if Omni runs, 4-bit); TNNLS papers in our sample never rest on one model.
- Exactness holds only for aligned edits of equal token length; add_* and multi-token word replacements are approximate.
- Exact "for the model's own conditionals", not for the data law; MDM conditionals are not guaranteed coherent and the
  order spread is reported, not removed.
- COCO-only images except Winoground; COCO captions are probably in UniDisc's training data (unmeasured), so SugarCrepe
  and FOIL positives may be memorised; blind and mismatched-image controls bound, but do not remove, this.
- Minimal-pair scoring only: SCER does not rank unrelated captions (retrieval) and does not choose between images for a
  fixed caption (Winoground image score uses the full-caption likelihood).
- Zero-shot; it ties or loses to trained cross-attention scorers (BLIP-2 ITM, VQAScore) where they are stronger, and
  those are reported.
- The bidirectional fusion and GATE results are reported with their own labels; neither is claimed as a gain.
- The theory is elementary; P3 restates known debiasing in an editor form; P5 depends on a training recipe we could not
  verify.
