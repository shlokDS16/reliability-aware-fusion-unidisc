# Paper 2 at TNNLS, design B: what only a unified masked diffusion model can verify

Tags: **SF** source fact (location), **OURS** a number from a project file (path), **INF** inference, **HYP** hypothesis,
**E0** my estimate or derivation. Planned is not done; predicted is not observed. No GPU was used for this file.

## 0. Verdict in ten lines

1. **Rank 1, CANAL (Calibrated Noisy-channel Analysis of Language with a masked diffusion model).** One frozen unified
   MDM reads a caption written by any model, computes for every word the exact two-sided slot posterior with the image
   visible and with it masked (one network pass each, full vocabulary), turns these into the Bayes posterior of a
   single-substitution channel (is the caption wrong, where, what was meant), flags words with a conformal-risk-control
   threshold so that the expected share of correct words that get edited is at most alpha, re-infills flagged words, and
   shows the image evidence for each edit by exact image-region marginalisation. Training-free.
2. **Rank 2, MARG (exact prediction-difference grounding by image-token marginalisation).** The grounding half of CANAL
   as a stand-alone method, evaluated by the pointing game on COCO boxes. Fallback if CANAL's text tasks fail.
3. **Rank 3, BICERT (Bayes-identity certificate / triple-collocation fusion of the two directions).** Killed by a CPU
   pilot today on the 239 FOIL fit pairs: the stored image direction is too weak and its gap to the text direction is
   anti-informative (section 1.2). Documented so nobody spends GPU time on it.
4. What is unified-only in CANAL: masking image tokens is in-distribution only for models trained to denoise image tokens
   (UniDisc, Omni-Diffusion), so the image-region marginal p(word | caption rest, image with region masked) is a trained
   conditional, not an occlusion artefact; the slot posterior over the whole vocabulary with two-sided caption context is
   one pass for an MDM and |candidates| suffix passes for an AR model. The blind prior is not unique (AR VLMs can drop
   the image), and that is said.
5. Theory: the channel posterior (Prop. 1, new in this form, elementary), the cost separation (Prop. 2, known in spirit),
   a regret bound from slot-level KL error (Prop. 3, elementary), the false-edit guarantee (Prop. 4, conformal risk
   control applied, known), blind-proofing (Prop. 5, elementary), marginalisation exactness (Lemma 0, known; measured by M5).
6. Validation by Saturday night: FOIL-COCO T1/T2/T3 on fresh images, SugarCrepe replace/swap, CHAIR repair of captions from
   two corporate captioners (BLIP-large, Janus-1.3B), on UniDisc 1.4B plus Omni-Diffusion 8B 4-bit if its timing passes.
   About 13-17 GPU-h plus 25 % contingency (section 3.9). Pilot 1.5 h with three kill rules.
7. Corporate baselines on 12 GB: CLIP ViT-L/14, SigLIP so400m, BLIP ITM-large (COCO-tuned), VQAScore (CLIP-FlanT5-XL),
   Janus-1.3B (AR p(t|v) and suffix-exact slot scores). Downloads exceed 5 GB in total and need Shlok's yes.
8. Honest expectation: CANAL will probably not beat VQAScore or CLIP on raw object accuracy (UniDisc is 1.4B with a
   VQ tokenizer). Its defensible wins are structural: word-level localisation and correction in one pass per word, a
   calibrated false-edit guarantee that none of the baselines carries, and measurable hallucination repair of corporate
   captioners at a stated edit risk.
9. "Best paper in TNNLS" cannot be engineered by Sunday: the Outstanding Paper Award is decided about two years after
   publication by overall quality (TNNLS-DEEP-DIVE sec. 1.10). What can be done by Sunday is a well-founded Full Paper
   with two backbones, three tasks, proofs in an appendix and preregistered tests, or an honest Brief Paper if only
   one backbone runs.

## 3. Rank 1: CANAL

### 3.1 Setting
Image tokens v (m = 1,024 for UniDisc at 512 px), an observed caption c = (c_1..c_n) grouped into words w_1..w_L (a word
is a word-initial piece and its continuations, as in method.tex). The caption may contain errors. One frozen unified MDM
p_theta. For a word slot k with token positions S_k:
- **image-visible slot posterior** P_k(y) = p_theta(c_{S_k} = y | c_{-S_k}, v), one pass with S_k masked;
- **blind slot posterior** B_k(y) = p_theta(c_{S_k} = y | c_{-S_k}, image fully masked), one pass;
- **region marginal** P_k^{-R}(y) = p_theta(c_{S_k} = y | c_{-S_k}, v with region R masked), one pass per region.
Multi-token slots: the slot joint is the chain over the |S_k| tokens in a fixed order (|S_k| - 1 extra passes for an
exact value; the mean-field product of the slot's token marginals as the cheap variant, labelled as such). Passes are
batched four to a forward.

### 3.2 The channel and the decision rules (Proposition 1)
Channel model C(rho, pi, Q): a clean caption c* ~ P(. | v); with probability rho one slot K ~ pi is substituted,
c_K = F with F ~ Q(. | c*_K, c*_{-K}), F != c*_K; otherwise c = c*.

**Proposition 1 (channel posterior from slot conditionals; new in this form, elementary).** If p_theta(. | c_{-S_k}, v)
equals the true conditional P(. | c_{-S_k}, v) for every slot, then for equal-length substitutions
(i) P(K = k | c, v, substituted) is proportional to pi_k * N_k / P_k(c_k), with N_k = sum_{y != c_k} P_k(y) Q(c_k | y, c_{-k});
(ii) P(substituted | c, v) = rho Z / (rho Z + 1 - rho) with Z = sum_k pi_k N_k / P_k(c_k);
(iii) P(c*_k = y | c, v, K = k) is proportional to P_k(y) Q(c_k | y, c_{-k}) for y != c_k.
Hence the Bayes rules for T1 (threshold on (ii)), T2 (argmax of (i)) and T3 (argmax of (iii)) are computed from one
image-visible pass per word, plus the blind pass when Q is taken from the blind conditional.

*Proof sketch.* P(c | K = k, sub, v) = P(c*_{-k} = c_{-k} | v) N_k and P(c | no sub, v) = P(c | v)
= P(c_{-k} | v) P_k(c_k). Divide: the factor P(c | v) is common to every k, so the ratio for slot k is N_k / P_k(c_k).
Bayes over (sub, K) gives (i)-(ii); conditioning on K = k and c*_{-k} = c_{-k} gives (iii). Unequal lengths shift the
layout of c_{-k}, so the common factor no longer cancels exactly; there the ratio carries a layout term (estimated with
extra passes or restricted to equal-length candidates; 53.4 % of FOIL foils have equal token length, section 1). QED.

Two named special cases, used as arms:
- **Q blind:** Q(f | y, c_{-k}) = B_k(f) / (1 - B_k(y)) (the substitute is a plausible text-only word). Then
  N_k = B_k(c_k) sum_{y != c_k} P_k(y) / (1 - B_k(y)), and log(N_k / P_k(c_k)) is, up to the bracket, minus the
  slot PMI log P_k(c_k) - log B_k(c_k). This is the hypothesis-free statistic (no candidate caption needed).
- **Q confusion:** Q estimated as a target-to-foil confusion matrix on the FOIL fit split (215 distinct pairs in the test
  file; OURS). Supervised, so labelled as such; it shows the value of knowing the channel.

### 3.3 Calibrated editing (Proposition 4: conformal risk control applied)
Suspicion s_k = log(N_k / P_k(c_k)) per word. On a calibration set of clean captions (original COCO captions, disjoint
images), the per-caption loss L_i(tau) = share of words with s_k > tau is bounded and non-increasing in tau, so the
threshold of method.tex Eq. (crc) gives E[L_test(tau_hat)] <= alpha for an exchangeable clean caption (Angelopoulos et
al., conformal risk control, 2208.02814, Theorem 1 with B = 1). **Statement made:** the expected share of words of a
correct caption that CANAL edits is at most alpha. **Not claimed:** any bound on edits of the correct words inside a wrong
caption, any bound under captioner shift (captions from BLIP or Janus are not exchangeable with COCO human captions; the
realised false-edit rate on their non-hallucinated object words is measured and reported), detection power. This reuses
the code and the wording rules of the GATE calibration (exp_p2/gate/calibrate.py, method.tex sec. "Calibration").

Repair: each flagged word is replaced by argmax of (iii) over a candidate set (the full vocabulary for single-token slots;
a noun list for multi-token slots), one word at a time in decreasing suspicion, re-scoring after each edit; the guarantee
covers the flagging step only (iteration breaks monotonicity, as for GATE).

### 3.4 Grounding each edit (the unified-only part; Lemma 0 and Rank 2)
For a flagged or corrected word, the evidence map over an 8 x 8 grid of 4 x 4-token regions R:
A_k(R) = log P_k(c_k) - log P_k^{-R}(c_k) (how much the word's probability depends on region R). For a Bayes denoiser,
masking R marginalises it (Lemma 0: method.tex Eq. marginalise; Ou et al. 2406.03736), so A_k(R) is Zintgraf et al.'s
prediction difference (1702.04595) with the conditional sampling replaced by exact marginalisation, in one pass per region,
and it is in-distribution because the model was trained to denoise masked image tokens (contrast: occlusion with grey
patches, or Chang et al.'s generative infill 1807.08024, both need a separate model or sampling). Exactness on the real
checkpoint is limited by the measured order gap (0.071 nats median, 0.25 for adjacent tokens; OURS, M5); this is stated.
A diffusion VLM with a continuous vision encoder (LLaDA-V, LaViDa) cannot do this without an occlusion artefact; an AR VLM
cannot do it at all in one pass.

Counterfactual figure (generation side, unified-only, figures only): infill the top-A regions conditioned on the foil
caption to show what the image would need for the wrong caption to be true. Not an evaluated claim.

  reported for alpha = 0.02, 0.05, 0.10).
- H4: CHAIR_i of BLIP-large and of Janus captions after CANAL at alpha = 0.05 is lower than before, with object recall

- Secondary, intervals only: FOIL T1 AUROC and pairwise accuracy; SugarCrepe per subset; Omni-Diffusion rows; cost in

### 3.11 Why frontier tri-modal and corporate efforts do not already cover this (critical reasoning, INF)
- Corporate verifiers (CLIP, SigLIP, BLIP-ITM) output one score per caption; word-level localisation needs one forward
  per deleted word and a deletion is not a marginalisation. VQAScore asks a question per hypothesis and must be told
  which word to test. None carries a false-edit guarantee.
- AR unified models (Janus, Show-o) can score a caption given an image but need suffix evaluation per candidate to get a
  two-sided slot posterior (Prop. 2) and cannot marginalise an image region without leaving the training distribution.
- Frontier tri-modal masked diffusion models (Omni-Diffusion, MMaDA, Lumina-DiMOO; Apple's tri-modal MDM) are trained and
  evaluated as generators and assistants; their papers (abstract level, E1) report benchmark scores, not verification of
  external text, and the VGR line corrects only the model's own outputs. Nothing in the model class's published use
  exploits the four conditionals jointly for auditing another model's caption.
- What this does **not** show: that a 1.4B MDM is a better visual recogniser than a CLIP-L or a 3B VQAScore model. It is
  likely not, and the paper must say so.

### 3.12 Limitations seed
- One or two backbones of one model class; UniDisc is 1.4B with no licence file found (Paper 3 design sec. 1); Omni at 4-bit.
- COCO captions are probably in UniDisc's training data (SAP U8); T3 can be memory recall. Blind and mismatched-image
  controls bound it, they do not remove it.
- The channel model is single-substitution; real hallucinations add, drop and reorder words; insertions are outside Prop. 1.
- Unequal-length substitutions break the exact cancellation; results split by length.
- The guarantee is marginal, for clean exchangeable captions, and does not transfer to captions of another captioner.
- The region maps are exact only for a coherent denoiser; the measured order gap for adjacent image tokens (0.25 nats)
  exceeds the clean mean evidence (0.199).
- FOIL is a 2017 benchmark with templated noun swaps; SugarCrepe negatives were written by a language model; CHAIR covers
  80 object classes only.
- No human evaluation of repaired captions (unless Shlok rates a sample).

## 4. Rank 2: MARG (stand-alone grounding)

**Method.** For a caption word k and an image, the region map A_k(R) of section 3.4 over an 8 x 8 grid (64 passes, batch 4),
optionally the chain-completed version (reveal regions in order and sum the increments, which equals the total word PMI by
the chain rule, completeness for free). **Props:** Lemma 0 (known); completeness of the chain version (elementary chain
rule; for an incoherent model the order dependence is measured, as in M5); equivalence to prediction difference analysis
with exact marginalisation (new in this form, elementary). **Validation:** pointing game on 500 (image, COCO-category noun)
pairs from val2017 with a single instance box of at least 2 % of the image, nouns present in a COCO caption of that image;
baselines CLIP-L grey-patch occlusion on the same 64 regions, BLIP-ITM GradCAM, the centre prior, a random region; shortcut
control: maps for a noun absent from the image (should be flat). **Pilot (30 min):** 100 pairs; kill if pointing accuracy
< centre prior + 0.05. **GPU:** 500 x 64 x 0.12 s x 1.3 = about 1.4 h on UniDisc. **Honest value:** a grounding figure
generator and a secondary result; alone it is a Brief-Paper-sized contribution at most.

## 5. Rank 3: BICERT (killed today; recorded to prevent rework)

**Idea.** For a Bayes-coherent unified model, log p(c+|v) - log p(c-|v) - [log p(c+) - log p(c-)] equals
log p(v|c+) - log p(v|c-) (Bayes). The text direction, the image direction and the joint direction are then three
readings of one latent quantity; triple collocation (one-factor analysis) would identify each direction's error variance
without labels, giving label-free fusion weights and a per-item error probability; sign disagreement would certify that at
least one direction errs relative to the true joint. **Why killed:** on the 239 fit pairs the stored image direction is a
mean-field caption-only estimator whose summed scale is about 12 times that of the text log-ratio (SD 69.95 vs 5.90 nats),
the two agree in sign on 65 % of pairs, and the gap is anti-informative for text errors (AUROC 0.262 against 0.837 for
|T|; section 1.2). Supervised LDA had already collapsed onto the text direction (fusion_frozen.json). A usable image-side
estimator (any-order chain rule over 1,024 tokens, or a multi-ratio ELBO) costs one to two orders of magnitude more passes,
and the errors of three readings from one network are unlikely to be independent, which triple collocation needs. Not
recommended for this deadline. The identity itself is a known Bayes fact; nothing new would remain without the estimator.

Also considered and dropped: understanding-guided image sampling (too close to EXP-003's question and to UniGen-style
self-verification); sequential any-order image revealing as an SPRT verifier (the image direction is weak per section 1.2);
fine-tuning methods (Paper 3's XBC; needs training and cloud).
