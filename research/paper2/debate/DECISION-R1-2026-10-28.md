## Decisions (each still needs a pre-data SAP amendment A2 with statistician and red-team sign-off before its first item)
1. **Primary family P stays frozen**: UniDisc 1.4B, JPEG / noise / splice, GATE vs no gate, exact McNemar, Holm m=3
   (statistician; red team agrees that moving patch/swap into P after the dry run would be forking paths).
2. **Claim reframed (method architect)**: from "GATE beats no gate" to a falsifiable account of WHEN input gating helps, with
   two preregistered secondary endpoints: E-dec (the corruption loss split into a detection part and a treatment part; CPU only)
   and E-pred (Prop. 4 fitted on the headroom items predicts the sign of the effect per corruption on the test fold; fitted and
   tested on disjoint folds). A null on JPEG/noise becomes a predicted, informative result, not a failed method.
3. **S2 re-infill becomes MUST** (red team: its absence is the one likely cause of rejection; the method architect agrees in a
   reduced form). True in-network re-masking and re-infill with the caption masked, on patch and splice, about 150 items, against
   the UniDisc G.1 recipe at an equal gate size; real repaired-image figures. Estimate 1.0-1.4 GPU-h (ML engineer); runs after M4.
4. **Patch and swap**: a separately labelled positive-control family PS (Holm m=2), disclosed as decided after the 21-item dry
   run; no headline claim (red team: random-code replacement is close to tautological). Cost: patch about 0, swap about 0.2
   GPU-h (estimates).
5. **Real, published benchmark: FOIL-COCO** (CC BY 4.0; overlap with val2017 to be verified, with folds re-checked for
   leakage). Used as published hard negatives for the fusion/verification endpoint, which takes the text direction off its 0.990
   ceiling: family B, m=1. It is not used as an image-token corruption, because the gate's score cannot see a fluent foil word
   (method architect, derived). Estimate 0.4-0.8 GPU-h, plus 6-8 h of code.
6. **Second model, Omni-Diffusion 8B 4-bit**: first a 20-pass timing and fit stage (audio-encoder stub; Dream's one-position
   logit shift; SenseVoice not built). Only if it fits: a gatekept family R (only corruptions where P rejects; own alpha; never
   rescues P), about 100 calibration + 120-150 test items. Estimate 0.6-1.6 GPU-h (assumed per-pass cost); first to be cut.
7. **More improvisation**: published image-corruption functions (`imagecorruptions`, Apache-2.0; ImageNet-C style) as a
   realistic exploratory corruption suite reusing M4 code (2-3 h of code). Lower priority than 3-6.
8. **Writing waits for results** (red team: drafting results before M4 invites fitting the text to the data). Only the
   outcome-independent sections exist now.
