## P1. arXiv 2605.12522 v1, Zhang et al.

**Version and venue.** PDF text says "Preprint. Under review." (28 pages). 2609.34509 cites it as "In COLM" (its bibliography), so COLM 2026 is second-hand. Code @8bafc9e.

**What it measures.**
- Sec 3.3 + App A (Tables 1-2): 10 ARMs and 10 DLMs, FineWeb prompts (1000 prompts, 20 continuations each), model-default decoding. Table 2 averages: trigram entropy 12.497 (ARM) vs 11.456 (DLM), unigram 7.316 vs 6.793, bigram 11.086 vs 10.133; semantic coherence 0.604 vs 0.649; diversity 0.589 vs 0.597. Uncontrolled: defaults differ per model.
- Sec 4: eight 120M LLaMA models trained from scratch (FineWeb, 2B tokens, ctx 512, 3 seeds), objective components ablated; the finding is that the objective barely moves entropy.
- Sec 5 (the part that matters here): final checkpoint of the DLM objective model, four remasking strategies (low-confidence, dynamic low-confidence with tau = 0.9, high-entropy, random), block lengths 2, 8, 32, N = B so one token per step (Sec 5.1). Metrics: n-gram entropy and cross-entropy H(p_con, p_seq) against sequential decoding of the same model (Eqs 8-9). Fig 3 is bars only (one seed; others in Figs 8-10). By eye from the rendered figure (approximate, not a table): trigram entropy of Confidence about 11.8, 10.7, 9.5 at block length 2, 8, 32 against about 12.1 for sequential; cross-entropy about 3.3, 2.1, 1.3 against about 4.05; bias-eliminated bars return to about 12 and about 4.2-4.4.

**Theorem 1 (Sec 5.2 informal, App C.1 full).** Statement: H(p_lcr) <= H(p_data) and H(p_dlcr) <= H(p_data).
Assumptions: (i) data distribution factorises into independent positions p_data = prod_i q^i (Eq 11); (ii) optimal predictor, p_theta(x^i | x_t) = q^i for every state (Eq 12); (iii) block-wise decoding with N = B; (iv) every step draws a token at every masked position of the block from p_theta (temperature 1 in the proof) and scores the draw by its own probability; (v) tie handling via an oracle order (Remark). Reference: H(p_data), which equals the sequential-decoding entropy under (i)-(ii). No matched-RS law.
Proof route: chain rule over position index, then majorisation of q^i by the conditional of X^i (Eq 15, Schur-concavity), proved by conditioning on the other tokens and the latent step assignment Z (Eqs 19-21). Case 1 (one token committed at step z^i): the conditional is proportional to q^i_c times w_c with w_c = prod over the other block positions j of the competitor mass sum_{r: q^j_r <= min(tau, q^i_c)} q^j_r (Eqs 22-24); w_c is nonincreasing in c, so the product majorises q^i (Eqs 25-26). Case 2 (several tokens at the step, via the threshold): weight 1[q^i_c > tau] (Eqs 27-30). Majorisation survives mixing over the conditioned variables.
My reading: sound as written for its assumptions. It is the k = 1, independent-data, state-independent-marginal version of our Props 4-5. It is not a general per-state statement because the marginals are state-independent here.

**Fix = our "sample, select, resample"?** Yes. Text: Sec 5.2 and App C.2 describe resampling the tokens at the selected positions, with the position set unchanged. Code (E3, @8bafc9e, src/models.py generate_block, approx lines 425-458): x0 is drawn by Gumbel-max from the logits, confidence is the softmax probability of that x0, top-1 (and, for dlcr, tokens above 0.9) are selected; `if resample:` redraws x0 from the same logits with fresh noise and only then writes the selected positions. Values come from the marginals at the same temperature (config.py default eval_temperature 1.0; `eval_allow_eos` False sets the EOS logit to -inf before everything, which alters the marginals slightly, same for both arms). Competitor set is the unrevealed positions of the current block (confidence outside the block set to -inf).

**Selection or bias term.** None. "Bias" is informal: the position choice depends on the sampled outcomes (Sec 5.2, Understanding of entropy reduction). No decomposition, no per-step quantity, no KL.

## P2. arXiv 2609.34509 v1, Jeon et al. (2026-09-28)

**What it proves and measures.**
- Definitions (Sec 2.1, Sec 3, boxed): LCR draws a proposal x~_i from p_i^(T) at every masked position, scores it by p_i(x~_i) under the *untempered* model, commits the argmax. TPP scores positions by c_i = max_v p_i(v), picks argmax_i c_i, then samples at that position from p_i^(T). One token per step throughout ("we focus on one-token-per-step decoding", Sec 2.1).
- Toy I (Sec 4.1, App B.1): i.i.d. positions, one strictly positive marginal, finite alphabet, unique top token, one commit per step from a fully masked block. Prop 1: P_TPP(non-top) = 1 - c_T and P_LCR(non-top) = (1 - c_T)^m with m masked positions. Prop 2: E_LCR[N_non-top / L] = (1 - c_T)(1 - (1 - c_T)^L) / (L c_T), which is O(1/L). Prop 4 (B.3): H_TPP = L H(p^(T)) and H_LCR = O(log L) (Fano plus subadditivity plus bounded E[N_non-top]), so entropy per token goes to 0. ## EXTRA. arXiv 2510.04525 v3, Hayakawa et al. (TMLR 04/2026), not in the novelty report

## Concerns

1. Hayakawa 2510.04525 (v3, TMLR 04/2026) was missing from the novelty audit. It names the sample-then-choose structure, proves the noised-SF-as-tilt equivalence in a regime, and gives an accumulated-bias explanation of the steps degradation. N1, N5 and the T1.5 taxonomy need a regime caveat; N5's mechanism claim should cite it. It deserves a full read (appendix proofs, App D-E) before any novelty statement.
2. N4 at k = 1 is not new: the heterogeneous competitor-CDF weight and the majorisation are inside 2605.12522 App C.1 (Eqs 22-26), and the iid special case is Beirami Eq 6. What remains is the stated-as-result per-state form for arbitrary predictors, ties, and k > 1, which no source has.
3. selection-term.md T2(a): the "random ties" law (F(v) + p(v))^N - F(v)^N is wrong when marginal values tie (p = (0.4, 0.4, 0.2), N = 2 gives 0.32, 0.32, 0.04, sum 0.68, against the exact 0.48, 0.48, 0.04; scratch check, re-run under `runs/`). Beirami's Assumption 2.1 excludes ties. Check which tie convention the verified check [V T2.8] used before the text is reused; the general-k formula and the T2.4-T2.6 equality cases also depend on it.
4. 2605.12522 is cited as COLM 2026 only through 2609.34509's bibliography; the arXiv PDF says "Under review", and the abs page date (4 Apr 2026) does not match the 2605 ID prefix. Cite as arXiv preprint until confirmed.
5. The SF-vs-tilt line depends on regime: Prop 2 holds for noiseless sampled-score SF (LLaDA-style per 2609.34509 A.3); released MaskGIT adds noise decaying to 0 (E3), and per Hayakawa noised SF approaches a tilt for N >> k^2 |S|^(1/tau).
