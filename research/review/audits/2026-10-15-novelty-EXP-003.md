# Novelty audit: EXP-003 (value rule and joint text-image consistency on UniDisc)

Date 2026-10-15. Evidence tags per CONTRACT. "E2 (sections)" means I read the named sections of the
paper's full text myself; it is not a reading of every page. No result of EXP-003 was looked at.
This audit says whether the question, the design and each possible outcome are already known; it
does not say how to make them new.

## 0. The candidate as one falsifiable sentence

On frozen UniDisc 1.4B, after rung-2 COCO prefixes with 32 masked tail tokens and a 512 px image
generated jointly, the official confidence-ordered sampler with CFG 3.0 at 32 steps (64 network
calls) gives a different seed-balanced noun-level link Lambda_b (own image minus sibling image VLM
presence, over nouns one seed's completion names and the sibling's does not) than plain T = 1
sampling at 64 calls; G minus O0 isolates guidance at matched steps and O0 minus P32 the rest of the
official sampler. Mechanism (card 1.2 with the D0018 erratum): guidance amplifies dependence on all
observed conditioning (template and prefix); confidence pooling may commit text earlier than image;
any effect on the completion-image link is indirect. The sentence is falsifiable (card section 5
outcome classes), so the audit proceeds.

**Single paper most likely to be cited against us:** CO2Jump, arXiv 2607.13188 (14 July 2026), because

## 2. What each closest paper does and does not measure, relative to EXP-003

Columns follow the brief: task, model, value rules compared, matched compute, statistic.

### 2.1 UniDisc, arXiv 2503.20853v1 (P0105, E2 sections)
- Source facts. Sec 4 intro: CFG enabled by dropping each modality with probability 0.1. Sec 4.1:
  "CLIP score is used for calculating image-text coherence"; conditional generation conditions on
  one whole modality ("We condition on an image to generate the corresponding language description,
  and vice versa"); the AR-UniDisc gap is "attribute[d] ... to classifier-free guidance (CFG)",
  results at scale 0 being similar. App B.2 Fig 10: unconditional multimodal generation, 115M,
  "Text - CLIP" per dataset (jointly generated pairs, no guidance). Fig 11: conditional results at
  the best CFG (UniDisc w = 1.5). App I: Fig 24 L2 distance between conditional and unconditional
  logits on masked tokens falls as sampling proceeds; Fig 25: CFG applied only on steps 1-3, 12-14,
  22-24 or all 24 gives CLIP 0.301, 0.293, 0.283, 0.312; "CLIP score scales cleanly with the guidance
  scale". App G.2: Grounded-SAM analysis, "generates all concepts at once", no positional strategy.
  Sec 3.4: MaskGIT confidence sampling found best.
- Relative to EXP-003. Task: one-modality-conditioned generation or unconditional joint generation,
  not prefix-conditioned joint completion of text and image. Model: same family (115M-340M for the
  sweeps; 1.4B only for GenEval and FID, App H.1). Value rules: CFG scale swept; plain T = 1 sampling
  without confidence ordering is not an arm; ordering and guidance are not separated. Matched
  compute: no (a guided step costs two calls; not discussed). Statistic: CLIP score of the pair; no
  same-condition baseline, so prefix-driven and co-generation-driven agreement are not separated.
- Card 1.3 check. The card's relay ("CFG windows move CLIP score"; "no same-prefix control there")
  is accurate against App I and Sec 4.1. One correction to the card's wording: UniDisc's Fig 10 does
  report a joint text-image coherence number (unconditional joint generation, no guidance), so
  "UniDisc reports joint consistency only under guidance" would be false if anyone writes it.

### 2.2 CO2Jump, arXiv 2607.13188v1 (P0107, E2 sections)
- Source facts. Sec 6: "we initialize from Lumina-DiMOO, fine-tune on the corresponding corpus, and
  swap only the inference-time sampler"; baselines MDM, ReMDM, MMaDA-Parallel; cosine alpha_t
  schedule for all. Sec 4.3: Gumbel-max draw; Sec 4.4: birth-death remasking with coupled confidence.
  Sec 5.1: target mAP against a per-sample Gemini pseudo scene graph "is the cleanest test of
  cross-modal coupling". Table 1: overall mAP 0.354 (MDM), 0.353 (ReMDM), 0.335 (MMaDA-Parallel),
  0.369 (CO2Jump). Fig 5 and text: NFE 8 to 512; "at 8 NFE the four methods sit within 0.009 mAP".
  Table 2: joint accuracy on mazes and nonograms. No guidance is mentioned anywhere in the text (text
  search for guidance, CFG); no seeds, intervals or repeated runs reported.
- Relative to EXP-003. Task: image editing with grounded text, and visual puzzles; joint output
  verified against ground truth or a judge. Model: Lumina-DiMOO, fine-tuned per task (not frozen
  released weights). Value rules compared: none; the value rule (draw, then rank) is shared; the
  arms differ in position rule, coupling and remasking. Matched compute: yes, by NFE (single pass per
  step for all). Statistic: task accuracy and mAP; no same-condition baseline; a sampler that makes
  both outputs individually better raises the joint metric without any change in their dependence.
- Inference. This is the paper a reviewer will cite against the question "does the sampler change
  joint consistency": the answer "yes, for position, coupling and remasking rules" is published.
  It does not touch guidance, temperature or any value rule, and it does not separate dependence
  from per-modality quality.

### 2.3 MMaDA-Parallel, arXiv 2511.09611v3 (P0106, E2 sections)
- Source facts. App G: Output Alignment "Evaluates the cross-modal consistency between the generated
  reasoning text and the final generated image" (GPT-4.1 judge). App H: "images are generated with
  classifier-free guidance scale of 3.5, and text with a scale of 0." App I Table 10: sequential,
  semi-parallel, fully parallel decoding give Output Align 54.1, 57.5, 59.8. Sec 4.3 Table 3:
  sequential 48.9 against parallel 51.5 (different training for the sequential arm). App E: image
  MaskGIT with sampled candidates ranked by confidence; text LLaDA semi-AR low-confidence remasking.
- Relative to EXP-003. Task: thinking-aware editing and generation (ParaBench, 300 prompts). Model:
  MMaDA-Parallel 8B. Value rules: fixed (CFG 3.5 image, 0 text); only the modality schedule varies.
  Matched compute: not stated. Statistic: LLM-judge score, one run, no intervals, no same-prompt
  baseline. Already in the project's primer (primer-multimodal 4, rows B1-B3, C1-C2).

### 2.4 MUNITE, arXiv 2610.09866v1 (P0108, E2 sections) and MUNI 2606.16408 (P0112, E1)
- Source facts. Sec 4.2: in PolyMNIST-D-Q one-to-many generation, "Conditioning on either label
  leaves the other attribute unobserved, so joint generation requires the three views to agree on
  that missing attribute"; MUNI's digit coherence 0.1074 and MUNITE's 0.9904 under quadrant
  conditioning; "MUNI's scores are near the chance agreement of independent views, 0.10 and 0.25".
  App E.1 Table 10: "Indep. is MUNITE with m1 and m2 decoded from separately drawn latents, keeping
  m0 and all decoder noise fixed", coherence 0.2500 and 0.1014, at chance. App E.1 also checks that
  agreement is not mode collapse (TV distance to the uniform conditional). Sec 4.4 Table 2:
  text-to-(image, audio) coherence by CLIP / CLAP / AIS; App D.3 Table 9: CFG fixed (1.5 for image).
- Relative to EXP-003. Task: synthetic and feature-level any-to-any generation. Model: continuous
  latent flow models; compares architectures, not samplers. Value rules: not varied. Matched compute:
  across models only. Statistic: the conceptual twin of EXP-003's question (agreement of co-generated
  outputs on content the condition does not fix, against an independent-draw baseline that keeps the
  condition), with a categorical verifier on synthetic attributes rather than a VLM on open nouns.
- Inference. The idea "measure co-generation dependence beyond the condition with a same-condition,
  independent-draw control" is published (7 October 2026, concurrent with EXP-003). EXP-003's
  sibling baseline differs in construction (the sibling is a full independent joint sample, not a
  partial re-draw), in the seed-balanced cancellation of image-level yes-bias, in the restriction to
  discordant nouns, and in applying it to natural images from a discrete unified model.

### 2.5 CoDi, arXiv 2305.11846 (P0110, E2 sections)
- Source facts. Sec 5.3: SIM(A, B) = cos(C_A(A), C_B(B)); "we compare SIM(A,B) of A and B generated
  separately vs. jointly, i.e., {P->A, P->B} vs. {P->A+B}"; Table 10, e.g. Audio->Image+Text SIM-IT
  0.251 against 0.260; "Jointly generated outputs consistently show stronger coherence".
- Relative to EXP-003. A same-prompt independent baseline exists, at the level of generation mode
  (separate against joint), not sampler; continuous latent diffusion; no intervals; similarity is not
  net of prompt-driven agreement except through that contrast.

### 2.6 CoBit, arXiv 2610.14349v1 (P0109, E2 sections; ICLR 2027 submission 117FVrY1Qv)
- Source facts. Sec 4.2, App B.2 Table 4: MNIST-Sum joint co-generation "99.46" percent mutually
  consistent, decomposed into cross-modal disagreement 0.29 and arithmetic error 0.24. App C.3
  Table 14: speech-text co-generation WER (generated text against ASR of generated speech) falls from
  367 at 4 steps to 3.5 at 512; "Unconditional generation does not use classifier-free guidance".
  Tables 18-19: guidance sweeps gs 1-5 for TTS, ASR and continuation at 256 steps, "256 function
  evaluations at gs=1; 512 for gs > 1" (not matched); "guidance improves modality alignment".
- Relative to EXP-003. Joint consistency is measured against steps but never under guidance; guidance
  is studied only in conditional directions and at unmatched NFE. Continuous bitstream, not masked.

### 2.7 Diffuse Everything, arXiv 2506.07903v2 (P0111, E2 sections)
- Source fact. App F: "CLIP score of 18.46 for text-to-image generation, 17.44 for image-to-text
  generation, and 17.57 for image-text joint generation" at the default sampler. Sec 3.4: noisy
  guidance, applicable to unconditional joint generation; evaluated on FID only (Fig 4).
- Relative to EXP-003. A joint text-image score exists; no sampler or guidance comparison on it.

### 2.8 Census-type precedent: Sampling-dependent fairness in DLMs, OpenReview Gq4yt8pxRo (P0113, E1)
- Source fact (abstract): on LLaDA, LLaDA-1.5 and Dream, "both the magnitude of measured bias and

  length, remasking strategy).
- Relative to EXP-003. Same argument shape as the census point (a metric is not attributable without
  its sampling configuration), text only, position rule and blocking rather than value rule.

### 2.9 Checker family (E1, search snippets only)

## 5. Concurrent work that changes the wording

- **MUNITE (2610.09866, 7 Oct 2026).** Any sentence presenting the sibling baseline as a new kind of
  control for co-generation must instead present it as a natural-image, discrete-model instance of a
  same-condition independent-draw control (MUNITE "Indep."), with the seed-balanced and
  discordant-noun refinements as the new parts. A null outcome (c4) should be stated beside MUNITE's
  chance-level MUNI result as an analogue, not as a first observation of conditional independence of
  co-generated outputs.
- **CO2Jump (2607.13188, 14 Jul 2026).** Paper 1 cannot say or imply that sampler choice affecting
  joint consistency in unified discrete-diffusion models is unreported. The defensible scope is
  narrower: a value-rule (guidance and selection) comparison at matched network calls on frozen
  released weights, with guidance separated from ordering (O0), on a statistic net of the prefix.
- **CoBit (2610.14349, 9 Oct 2026; ICLR 2027 submission).** Reports joint co-generation consistency
  against step count and guidance effects at unmatched NFE; strengthens the case that matched calls
