## Per-candidate evidence

### N1 (taxonomy: tilts vs value-dependent selection) - incremental, 0.70
- Source facts (E1): 2609.34509 defines LCR as sampling at every masked position and committing the sampled token with highest probability, contrasts it with TPP (rank by max-probability, then sample), and reports that LCR suppression persists across temperatures (Sec 5). 2605.12522 Sec 5.2 locates the bias in the correlation between "remasking position selection and the sampled token outcomes". 2604.09921 separates a token temperature from a position temperature and notes (footnote 1, per summariser) that stochastic confidence is the probability of the sampled token. 2608.25505 Theorem 1 holds for value-adaptive deterministic coordinate-selection policies with exact marginals; per summariser it does not treat temperature, argmax or sampled-value filtering.
- What was not found: an explicit statement that state-only tilts (temperature, top-p, argmax, CFG) stay inside the factorisation identity with a tilted predictor while value-dependent filtering does not, and the E_tilt cost term.
- Inference: the SF-vs-RS split is now known in at least three 2026 papers; the placement of CFG/top-p/argmax relative to the identity is the remaining new part, and it is a corollary.

### N2 (E_sel vs matched-RS reference; sample-select-resample) - incremental, 0.75
- Source fact (E1): 2605.12522 Sec 5.2 / App. C.2 / Fig. 3: after the remasking strategy picks positions, tokens at those positions are resampled; n-gram entropy returns close to sequential decoding. Same experiment cited by 2609.34509 App. A.1.
- What was not found: use of that procedure as the reference policy in an output-level KL definition (E_sel = KL(q||p_SF) - KL(q||p_mRS)); the KL-projection property (argmin over rho' gives rho_kappa); the self-distortion Delta and its D <= H(rho) <= log C(N,k) bound.
- Inference: the operational object is published; the formal reference and its properties are not found.

### N3 (Prop. 2, forward per-step term infinite) - novel (narrow), 0.55
- Nearest (E1): 2602.00286 Lemma C.1 (App. C.3): if the product of marginals puts mass where the joint has none, KL(p_prod || p) = +inf. That is reverse-direction, factorisation-driven, no selection. 2609.34509 Prop. 1 gives (1 - c_T)^m suppression, which is positive, not blocked.
- Not found after Q10-Q14: any statement that SF assigns zero probability to a data-supported (position, value) pair, or that a per-step forward accounting of selection error is impossible.
- Residual risk: elementary; a reviewer may call it a technicality. Scope fact (E2, Halton Sec 3.3): MaskGIT's released sampler adds Gumbel noise to the confidence for position selection, which makes every pair reachable; Prop. 2 then applies to noiseless sampled-confidence rules only.

### N4 (exact SF commit law, majorisation, best-of-n, k>1) - incremental, 0.75
- k = 1, identical marginals: our r_N(v) = (F(v) + p(v))^N - F(v)^N is Beirami et al. Lemma 2.3 Eq. 6 with reward = p(v) and ties via F vs F^- (E1). The bound log N - 1 + 1/N equals their Theorem 3.1 bound log n - (n-1)/n; the paper disproves the equality (Eq. 4) and attributes the formula to Stiennon et al. 2020, Hilton and Gao 2022, Gao et al. 2023, Coste et al. 2024, Go et al. 2023, Scheurer et al. 2023 (E1, attribution list from fetch). Mroueh 2406.05883 Theorem 1 (Eqs. 16-17) proves the same bound via the Renyi representation and data processing, the same route as our quantile-bin argument (E1). The continuous max-order-statistic KL is probably older (Ebrahimi, Soofi and Zahedi 2004, IEEE TIT 50(1):177-183); title and venue confirmed, formula NOT read (E0 for content).
- Our -log p_max bound: Beirami Corollary 4.2 / Lemma 4.1 give a tighter eps_inf-dependent bound d_n(eps_inf) (exact form not read, E1); check before claiming T2(a) monotonicity/limit as new.
- DLM-specific: 2609.34509 App. B.1 Prop. 1 gives P_LCR(non-top) = (1 - c_T)^m, which equals 1 - r_N(mode); Prop. 4 gives H_LCR = O(log L) in the iid toy (E1). 2605.12522 describes sharpening "by amplifying high probabilities" and proves output entropy reduction under independent data, including |S| > 1 (App. C.1 Case 2) (E1).
- Not found (Q1-Q4, Q20-Q24): the commit law with heterogeneous competitor marginals and tie priorities (Prop. 4 general k), the per-state majorisation of kappa_i over p^i (Prop. 5), and any k > 1 best-of-n / top-k KL formula. Beirami treats only select-1-of-n; App. B (blockwise) is sequential, not top-k (E1). Answer to the brief: the k > 1 version was not found.
- Inference: Props. 4-5 are a heterogeneous generalisation of the best-of-n law; cite Beirami Lemma 2.3 and Theorem 3.1 (and Mroueh Theorem 1) for the k = 1 pieces.

### N5 (U-shape mechanism) - incremental, 0.60
- Source fact (E2): Halton Sec 3.3: the Confidence scheduler minimises the entropy term greedily, neglects MI of selected tokens; MaskGIT code adds Gumbel noise to confidence (position selection only, "does not affect the token value itself"); result is "saturation, and even deterioration ... as the number of steps grows (see Figure 5)". Fig. 5 caption: "the Confidence scheduler's performance deteriorates"; the text layer of Fig. 5 shows the Confidence FID series falling then rising (values not re-tabulated here).
- 2605.12522: entropy reduction grows with block length (more competitors) (E1).
- Not found: the decomposition of the step-count curve into falling E_fact and rising E_sel. Competing explanation (Halton) is position-law/MI based.

### N6 (entropy counterexample) - novel (elementary), 0.60
- Nearest: 2605.12522 Theorem 1 uses entropy below the data entropy as the signature of LCR, under independent data (E1). 2608.25505 Example 2 (copy chain) separates serial depth from entropy, not output entropy from data entropy (E1). 2604.00375 Prop. 2 is an absolute cap (credited).
- Not found (Q15-Q16): an example where a state-only sampler with exact marginals has output entropy far below H(q).

### N7 (E_fact^X and requery estimator) - novel (narrow), 0.50
- The split is the TC chain rule (textbook). Halton Eq. 4-5 already writes per-step MI as KL(joint || product of marginals) and decomposes it into entropies (E2), in one modality. Hoover et al. 2104.08685 Sec 3 estimate dependence by masking/unmasking a word in an MLM (CPMI) (E1); our estimator is the expected-KL, cross-modal, sampler-state version.
- Not found (Q17-Q19, Q25): a per-step measurement of text-image dependence of co-committed tokens in a unified discrete-diffusion model. MMaDA-Parallel (2511.09611) and Muddit (2505.23606) discuss cross-modal consistency qualitatively (E1, snippets).

### N8 (census) - novel as a census, 0.55
- Not found (Q26-Q29): a systematic count, across DLM or discrete text-to-image papers, of value rules in headline results and of entropy/diversity reporting. Nearest: Generative Frontiers 2604.02718 (credited, argues for frontiers); 2606.29228 shows ranking inconsistency driven by prompt templates (no census) (E1); 2606.19475 runs its own sweeps (E1); 2604.13413 (configuration as part of protocol; snippet only, E1).
- Residual risk: surveys (e.g. 2508.10875, 2607.13431) may tabulate decoding settings without counting entropy reporting; not opened.
