Single paper most likely to be cited against us by a reviewer:
- C1: **PUNT (2510.21961)**, because its Sec 3.2-3.3 test is "KL between a token's conditional with and without the revealed anchor set", which is the same reveal-and-re-query idea on text. Close second: VRCD 2605.25820, because it reports a per-step quantity over co-committed tokens that is tied to the image.
- C2: **DAPD (2603.12996, ICML 2026)**, because it states the C2 principle (select a set with no strong dependence edges, avoid updating strongly coupled tokens in one step). Close second for the multimodal framing: VRCD.

Which parts the review may claim as new (my reading of the evidence, not a rescue): the measured quantity on a unified model and its step profile; the four-schedule comparison on a unified model. Which parts it may not: the CMI-from-reveal-and-requery estimator idea, the notion that co-committing dependent tokens costs quality, any claim of a first dependence-aware sampler.

## 3. Evidence per candidate (new items only; the known list is not re-reported)

Format: source fact (location), then inference marked [I].

### 3.1 PUNT, arXiv 2510.21961 (E1, HTML sections via summariser)
- Source fact: Sec 3.2-3.3, Alg. 1: masked tokens ranked by confidence and split recursively into anchor and test sets; each round tests every test token against the joint anchor set in one forward pass; a token is dropped if the KL between its conditional with and without the anchors revealed exceeds a threshold epsilon; O(log |M|) forward passes per step.
- Source fact: Sec 4.3, Fig 3: per-token parallel sampling error (Eq. 3) plotted against the number of previously revealed tokens; the median and 5th to 95th percentiles stay below epsilon. Sec 3.4, Fig 2: hierarchical generation pattern across steps 9 and 18, qualitative. Text and protein models only. Baselines include EB-sampler and the dilated sampler.
- [I] This is the same measurement primitive as C1's reveal-and-re-query step. It is not cross-modal and has no mixture reference, but a reviewer will see the primitive as known.

### 3.2 DAPD, arXiv 2603.12996, ICML 2026 poster (E1, HTML via summariser)
- Source fact: Sec 3.1-3.2: edge when symmetrised attention score s_ij exceeds a step-dependent threshold; step = select an independent set (relaxed to non-adjacent), Welsh-Powell-style heuristic. Sec 5-6: LLaDA-8B-Instruct and Dream-7B-Instruct, text benchmarks; baselines EB-Sampler, KLASS, Fast-dLLM; NFE not held fixed (Fig 3 plots accuracy against steps; LLaDA setups differ in block count).
- Source fact: no MI measured; Fig 6 and App B show distributions of mask-to-mask edge scores per step, used to choose the threshold; the summariser found no figure that shows when dependence is concentrated.
- [I] C2's rule is this rule restricted to cross-modal pairs. Matched-pass comparison against modality-ordered schedules is not in the paper.

### 3.3 DEMASK, arXiv 2604.02560 (E1, HTML via summariser)
- Source fact: Sec 4.3 Eq 8, 10: predictor on final hidden states, MSE to cached pairwise TV distances (about 500K examples on Tulu-3 SFT responses, 5 epochs); greedy selection with bounded cumulative dependency; Sec 5.4 Fig 4: sub-additivity check stratified by subset size |S| and random mask ratio, not by denoising step. Dream-7B only, text only. Forward passes not explicitly matched (Table 1 reports wall-clock; forward-pass comparison only in Fig 1 against KLASS).

### 3.4 Dilated, stratified and spread schedules (E1)
- DUS 2506.19037 Sec 3.5-3.6, 4.1: stride-based non-adjacent groups, NFE-matched, text only; the mutual-information argument is a Markov-chain approximation (Lemma 2, rho^(2d)), no per-step measurement.
- Halton 2503.17076 (abstract level only): Halton sequence spreads positions so tokens unmasked in the same step are far apart; the abstract refers to the sampling objective as based on mutual information between tokens. Image only (ImageNet class-to-image, COCO text-to-image where text is conditioning, not generated).
- LaViDa-O 2509.19244 App (known paper; reported through a search snippet only): stratified sampler justified by the independence assumption for adjacent image tokens. Sparse-LaViDa 2512.14008 Sec 3.2 reuses the stratified order for image generation (pre-generated 2D order, "higher generation quality", no numbers seen).

### 3.5 VIG-Sampler, arXiv 2608.26580, submitted 27 Aug 2026 (E1, HTML via summariser; PDF was binary)
- Source fact: Sec 2.1: image attention from the last layer averaged over heads. Sec 2.2 Eq 5: shared information ratio SIR = 1 - dU(S) / sum_i dU({i}), the overlap between joint and summed single-token information gain of a committed set S; Obs. 2: SIR rises with image-attention similarity for |S| in {2,4,8}, on 100 COCO images with k=1 captions (App B.1). Sec 2.3 Eqs 8-11: greedy rerank penalising pairs with similar centred image-attention vectors. Sec 3.1: LaViDa-LLaDA, MMaDA-8B-MixCoT, LLaDA-V; captioning and VQA only; no image generation. Sec 4.1 Fig 4: cumulative information gain across steps per sampler.
- Source fact: Sec 2.2 makes no claim about how the relationship varies with step (summariser).
- [I] This is the nearest published instance of "measure a per-step overlap among co-committed tokens in a model that also sees an image, then avoid it". It differs from C1/C2 in three ways: the pair is text-text (sharing visual evidence), the output is text only, and the quantity is information-gain overlap with an attention proxy, not CMI.

### 3.6 VRCD, arXiv 2605.25820, v1 25 May 2026, v2 10 Jun 2026 (E1, HTML via summariser)
- Source fact: Sec 3.2 Eq 2: VRI(S_t) = sum_u (sum_i v_i(u) - max_i v_i(u)) / (m-1), over positions committed at step t with normalised token-to-image attention v_i(u). Sec 3.3 Eqs 3-7: candidate window by confidence, pairwise saliency overlap, percentile rank, rerank s_i = c_i (r_i+1)^(-alpha). Sec 4.1: lavida-llada-reason (default), MMaDA (App A.4); M3CoT, MMBench, SQA-IMG, DocVQA, InfoVQA. Sec 4.3, Fig 3a: VRI computed at each step; text says VRCD "consistently lower VRI throughout decoding"; no stated early/late shape (Fig 3 vs Fig 4(a) citation is inconsistent in the text). Table 4: mean VRI 0.752 (LaViDa) vs 0.712 (VRCD) at L=192, FR=0.25.
- [I] A reviewer can use VRCD to argue that "per-step dependence among co-committed tokens, tied to the image, plus an avoid-it reranker" is published for dMLLMs. It does not cover generated image tokens, text-image pairs, or the schedule comparison.

### 3.7 Info-Gain sampler, arXiv 2602.18176, ICML 2026 poster (E1, first 100k chars of HTML)
- Source fact: Sec 3.2.1-3.2.2, App D.1: draw N candidate actions, apply each (reveal) to the current state, one batched re-query, score by entropy drop minus immediate cost; Sec 4.1/App B: MMaDA for GenEval and ImageNet-512 (N = 8, 50-step cosine schedule); Sec 4.3 correlation of cumulative entropy with accuracy (r = -0.70). No dependence between tokens measured. The summariser could not confirm whether MMaDA places text tokens in the same sequence for that experiment.
- [I] Same mechanical primitive (reveal candidates, re-query) used as a sampler on a unified-family model for image generation.

### 3.8 CCD, arXiv 2512.02044 (E1)
- Source fact: Sec 3.2 Prop 1 (Eq 8): H(x_i|s) = H(x_i|c,s) + I(x_i;c|s); Prop 2 bounds sampling error with this term averaged over the trajectory; Eq 6: the averaged predictive distribution is used as a proxy and the selection rule uses the entropy of that average; CMI not estimated directly; text only (Dimple appears only in related work).
- [I] Nearest prior use of an averaged-prediction reference to remove marginal inconsistency; its average is over time-history of the same position, not over draws of another modality.

### 3.9 Theory papers that name the quantity (E1)
- 2602.00286 (Sec 5.2 Eq 15-18 conditional TC; Sec 6 experiments on a synthetic Block-HMM and LLaDA arithmetic, no TC or MI on a real MDM). 2602.20126 (Sec 3.1, 3.2: schedule adaptive to TC and DTC, synthetic Reed-Solomon experiment, no per-step estimation). 2505.21400 and 2511.04647 (KL error linear in token MI; schedules in TC, DTC). 2603.22248 (per-iteration KL controlled by MI terms between newly unmasked token and tokens unmasked in the same step, per search snippet). 2607.04469 CoCommit (error as conditional TC; not estimated; text only). 2605.10518 IMDM is on the known list.
- [I] Nobody found estimates the quantity per step on a real model; the quantity is known theoretically, so the claim of "defining" it is not new, only its measurement.

### 3.10 Multimodal schedule evidence (E1)
- Omni-Diffusion 2603.06577 Sec 3.4, App B.2: modality-agnostic entropy/confidence top-k; image-only position penalty (Table 6: CLIP-T/CLIP-I 0.233/0.666 vs 0.235/0.667); speech pre-infilling puts text in the first 0.25L and speech in the remaining 0.75L, decoded simultaneously (a fixed text-first layout); no modality-order ablation, no cross-modal per-step analysis.
- LLaDA2.0-Uni 2604.20796 Sec 2.3: block-wise generation, SPRINT adaptive unmasking and modality-aware prefix pruning with separate keep ratios (cost, not ordering). No order ablation seen in the first 100k chars; last 43k chars unread.
- Tri-modal MDM 2602.21472 Sec 1 and 7.3: sampling defaults differ by modality; single predefined linear unmasking schedule; no joint text-image generation or order.
- UniCanvas 2606.04264 Sec 3.2: continuous flow matching, two sequential ODE stages (reasoning canvas then synthesis); not discrete, no dependence measure.
- Muddit, Dynin-Omni, Lumina-DiMOO: only search snippets read; no schedule ablation reported in snippets.
- TACA 2506.07986 (snippet): in MM-DiT (continuous), early denoising steps need strong text-visual interaction for layout and later steps refine details. Premise-level prior for C1's "early" finding in continuous models; it is an intervention-based design claim, not a measured MI.
- LLaDA-V pruning 2601.20168 (snippet): cross-modal aggregation concentrated in middle-to-late layers; first-denoising-step pruning. Layer depth, not step; understanding only.
