## What makes this review new (and how we keep it that way)

A catalogue of 600 to 1,500 papers would repeat what about 15 existing surveys already do (none of them states a
search protocol, synthesises numbers or audits reporting). Paper 1 earns its place with results a reader can check:

1. **The matched-comparison synthesis (new headline candidate).** From the within-paper contrasts we extract,
   how often a diffusion LM beats its own autoregressive baseline when the comparison is matched on budget and
   evaluation conditions, how often it is matched at all, and where the evidence is too weak to say. Codex
   (debate 2026-10-20, round 2) named this the result no survey can report; it needs the contrast data of
   D0042 and the K15 "matched" flag.
2. **The value-rule census**, re-coded on every include by two extractors, with a robustness check on how
   "headline result" is sampled (Codex's cheapest falsification test).
3. **The design space with its limits reported as findings** (11 partial fits of 47).
4. **Open problems with the evidence each needs**, plus EXP-003 as one short section.
Every section opens with its claim and its count; no model-by-model summaries.

## Tool guide

**Codex (GPT, your ChatGPT/Codex plan): nothing for you to do.** It is working again (the D0015 limit has
lifted). Claude runs one logged thread per discussion (`tools/debate.py`, files in `research/debates/`), so your
Codex app shows one chat per discussion, not one per message. Used at: novelty (started today), the extraction
gate, the synthesis, and the final draft.

**NotebookLM (now called Gemini Notebook, renamed in July 2026): 5 minutes of your time, once.** It gives a
second, Gemini-based review panel on the same files (FRAMEWORK.md section 4). Steps:
1. Tell Claude "set up NotebookLM". Claude starts the sign-in; a Chrome window opens on the Google login page.
   Sign in with your own Google account there (Claude never sees or types your password).
2. Open https://notebooklm.google.com in Chrome (it may forward to the Gemini Notebook page). Create a notebook
   named "DLM review panel". Claude will have put the files to upload in `research/review/panel-sources/` (the
   draft PDF, the protocol, the framework, the census table); add them with "Add source" > "Upload".
3. Click "Share", set access to "Anyone with the link" (viewer) and paste the link to Claude. Claude registers it
   and asks the panel questions through the NotebookLM tool; answers are logged next to the Codex debates.
   Free accounts allow about 50 questions a day, which is enough.

**VIT e-gateway: your Chrome, by hand (no automation this week).** Three checklists, each with a "Start here"
tab: `research/review/fulltext/egateway-manual-checklist.xlsx` (58, T1), `egateway-manual-checklist-r797.xlsx`
(118; 68 are IEEE Xplore: open the link through the e-gateway and use the PDF button),
`egateway-manual-checklist-s1243.xlsx` (3). Save each as `<ID>.pdf` in Downloads and tell Claude "import the
e-gateway PDFs".
