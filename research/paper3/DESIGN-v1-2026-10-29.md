# Paper 3 design v1: Cross-direction Bayes-Consistent self-distillation (XBC) for unified masked discrete diffusion

Tags: **SF** source fact (location given), **OURS** a number already in a project run or report (path given),
**INF** inference, **HYP** hypothesis, **E0** my derivation or estimate. Nothing in this file is a new result.
Planned is not done; every cost is arithmetic on stated assumptions until the pilot measures it.

## 0. Verdict in eight lines

1. **Design: XBC.** Keep BP-CML's core (image-direction candidate softmax over single-token span fills drawn from the
   model's own image-blind conditional), and repair it in three places: (a) the label is softened by the *same network's*
   text-direction posterior over the candidates (exact for one token, one pass), so understanding supervises generation;
   (b) a frozen **prior slot** carries the caption-only Bayes term, so the image likelihood no longer has to encode a
   machine-fill detector (red team F-A3b, now explained by Prop. 2); (c) **decoy images** with text-side targets, and
   positions chosen only from image-and-context information (fixes F-A1). Generation sampler unchanged.
2. **Structural property:** yes, the design uses it. Only a unified model holds both the understanding conditional
   p(t | i) and the generation conditional p(i | t) in one parameter set, so only it can teach one with the other
   through Bayes' rule. Masked diffusion adds exactness and cost: the teacher's full-vocabulary span posterior with
   two-sided context is one forward pass; a unified AR model needs one suffix pass per candidate; AR VLMs, CLIP and
   diffusion-ITM lack one of the two directions. This is the central claim, scoped as "cheap and exact", not "unique".
3. **Theory:** Lemmas 1-2 are known (ranking NCE, InfoNCE, Bayes). New in form, elementary in proof: Prop. 2 (where the
   detector comes from, and the slot fix), Prop. 3 (Jensen bias bound for the CRN Monte-Carlo loss: answers F-A4),
   Prop. 4 (soft target = Rao-Blackwellised label; known in substance, Menon et al. 2005.10419), Prop. 1 (consistency and
   shrinkage). Generation transfer is a conjecture.
4. **Novelty:** incremental as a method (confidence about 0.55, pending a novelty audit of XBC itself). Two upgrades
   could make it non-incremental: U1, a cross-model **Bayes-coherence benchmark** of unified models (inference only,
   about 10-15 A100-h); U2, a **shortcut-controlled hard-negative set** with an image-choice split (about 8 GPU-h plus
   2-3 h of human audit).
5. **Primary endpoint:** image-direction accuracy on length-matched SugarCrepe rows (replace_*, swap_*), image-clustered,
   against FT-pos at equal examples and at equal FLOPs; co-primary: best-scorer comparison including FT-pos text PMI;
   generation (T2I-CompBench attribute binding) confirmatory. Decision margin 3 points (MDE about 2.6-3.3, sec. 7.6).
6. **Pilot kill rule (one day, about 7.5 GPU-h):** stop if the text-side teacher is not at least 3 points above the
   image side at theta_0 (nothing to transfer), if a strong blind LM scores >= 0.70 on the training pairs, if XBC does not
   beat FT-pos by more than 2 dev points in both seeds, or if XBC's mismatched-image accuracy exceeds 0.53.
7. **Compute:** laptop about 70 attended GPU-h (pilot, caches, fidelity check, HPO on UniDisc 256 px); cloud about
   105 A100-h (finals at 5 seeds, ablations, 512-px confirmation, second masked backbone, U1), USD 125-210 at an
   unverified USD 1.2-2.0/h, cap USD 300. Without cloud there is one backbone and TNNLS Full Paper is not credible (E-2).
8. **Shlok decides:** cloud budget (cap USD 300), submission target (2026-12-23 proposed), UniDisc licence e-mail,
   pilot go-ahead, upgrades U1/U2, AI-disclosure placement, professor sign-off on XBC.

## 3. The design: XBC

### 3.1 Idea in four sentences

A unified masked model has two estimates of the same quantity, the posterior over which candidate fill is the true one:
the **text side** reads it from p(a | c_{-s}, v) directly (one pass, exact for one token), and the **image side** reads it
from p(v | c^(k)) through Bayes' rule (K+1 ELBO passes). On random negatives the project's only measurement shows the text
side far ahead (0.940 vs 0.783, probe 1, whole captions, exploratory). XBC trains the image side, which is the generator's
conditional, to agree with the true label and with the text side on hard, self-generated span candidates, while a frozen
prior slot and decoy images keep caption-only shortcuts out of the image likelihood. The text side keeps its own MDM
training; sampling and generation are unchanged.

### 3.2 Training objective (per example)

Given (v, c) from COCO train2017, after the offline cache (Algorithm 1) has fixed s, the candidates, τ, g and the decoy:

    image-side logits    z_u = β ĥ_θ(v; c^(u); ω) + g_u + log n_u,         u in U, one ω shared by all u   (1)
    soft target          y_u = (1-α) 1[u = a_0] + α softmax_u(τ_u + log n_u)                              (2)
    contrast loss        L_XBC = - Σ_u y_u log softmax_u(z)                                               (3)
    decoy branch         (with prob. ρ, replace v by v' in (1); target y'_u = softmax_u(τ'_u + log n_u),
                          τ'_u = log p_0(u | c_{-s}, v') - log r(u); no hard label)                       (4)
    total                L = L_MDM(v, c) + λ L_XBC                                                         (5)

- L_MDM is UniDisc's own training loss on the positive pair (both modalities, its own schedule and weighting), every step.
- ĥ_θ uses UniDisc's own image-token ELBO weighting w(t) and schedule with the caption fully visible; the lab-engineer
  must cite the file and line of the loss in `models/third_party/unidisc` at 3ee3e47 and reuse `exp_p2/scoring.py`
  (`masked_logprobs`, `UniScorer`) where it fits. With m draws, ĥ is the mean over m CRN draws (default m = 1; the
  pilot may set m = 2, sec. 8).
- Duplicates are merged with multiplicities (the log n_u terms), never resampled away: rejecting draws equal to a_0
  would make the proposal depend on a_0 and break Lemma 1.
- Only the image-side term (1) carries gradient in L_XBC; τ, g, r are frozen constants from the cache. The text side is
  trained only through L_MDM.

Defaults (HPO ranges in sec. 7.7): K = 1 on the laptop (K = 3 on cloud and as an ablation), α = 0.5, β = 1, λ = 1,
ρ = 0.25, m = 1, LoRA rank 16 on attention and MLP of the upper 12 of 24 blocks, AdamW lr 1e-4, cosine with 5 % warm-up,
clip 1.0, bf16, micro-batch 4 examples, effective batch 16, 20K examples per run, 256 px if the pilot passes (sec. 8).

### 3.3 Position selection (fixes F-A1) and proposal details

- Draw s uniformly over the caption's non-special positions. Compute r_s and p_0(. | c_{-s}, v) (two no-grad passes;
  both are needed for τ anyway) and D_s = KL(p_0(. | c_{-s}, v) || r_s). Accept the example with probability
  min(1, D_s / D_ref), D_ref = median D over 500 calibration examples drawn once. **If rejected, discard the example; do
  not redraw s for the same caption.** Acceptance then depends only on (v, c_{-s}, independent noise), never on a_0
  (Cor. 1.1); redrawing would leak a_0 through the other positions' D values.
- Effect (INF): function words and image-irrelevant positions (D near 0) are thinned out, so passes go to positions where
  the image matters. This is a hardness dial that keeps Lemma 1 exact, unlike POS-based span choice.
- r is renormalised over V_txt (no special, mask or image tokens). Sampling at temperature 1, no top-k or top-p. If a
  truncation is ever used, τ and g must use the truncated density that was sampled (Lemma 1 holds for any known proposal).
- |S| = 1 always (F-A2, P1'). Sub-word positions are allowed; the exactness claim is for single tokens, which may be
  word pieces.

### 3.4 Prior slot

g_u = log P_LM(c^(u)) - log r(u). P_LM is a frozen open causal LM of 7-8B parameters run in 4-bit, scoring the whole
candidate string (decode c^(u) to text, score, cache). The common normaliser of P_LM(a | c_{-s}) cancels in the softmax,
so whole-caption scores suffice. Licence of the LM must be verified before use (candidate models are not named here
because none was checked). Prop. 2 explains why the slot is needed; ablation g = 0 tests it.

### 3.5 Inference

- **Generation:** UniDisc's unchanged sampler and guidance. Zero extra forward passes.
- **Matching** (all arms use the same scorers; statistics fixed on dev, never on test):
  - S_img(c) = mean over M = 8 seeded draws of ĥ_θ(v; c; ω_m), CRN across the candidate captions of one row, t stratified
    as t_m = (m - U_m)/M. Unnormalised (both captions condition the same image; Bayes needs no length division).
  - S_txt(c) = -NELBO_θ(c | v) + NELBO_0(c | ∅), whole caption, M = 8 CRN draws over text masks; the blank term uses the
    **frozen** p_0 (closes the trainable-prior cheat, ROUND2-A sec. 1.1(c)).
  - S_sum = S_img + S_txt (two estimates of the same log-posterior ratio plus a caption-only constant difference).
  - The prior slot is **never** used at test time.
- Per-row matching cost: 2 candidates x 8 draws for S_img, the same for S_txt (blank terms cached once per caption).

### 3.6 Algorithms (line-numbered; each builds on the previous)

**Algorithm 1 — OfflineCache(D_train, p_0, P_LM, K, ρ, seed)** (no grad; once per data set, reused by every arm and seed)

    1  for each (v, c) in D_train, in a seeded order:
    2      s <- Uniform(non-special positions of c)
    3      r <- softmax over V_txt of p_0 logits at s, input (c_{-s}, ∅)                 # pass 1
    4      q <- softmax over V_txt of p_0 logits at s, input (c_{-s}, v)                 # pass 2
    5      D <- KL(q || r); with prob 1 - min(1, D / D_ref): continue  (discard example)
    6      a_1..a_K <- i.i.d. draws from r ;  a_0 <- c_s
    7      U, n <- distinct values of (a_0..a_K) with multiplicities
    8      τ_u <- log q(u) - log r(u)  for u in U
    9      g_u <- log P_LM(decode(c^(u))) - log r(u)  for u in U
    10     v' <- a random other training image (seeded); with prob ρ mark example "decoy":
    11         q' <- softmax at s, input (c_{-s}, v')                                     # pass 3 (decoy only)
    12         τ'_u <- log q'(u) - log r(u)
    13     store (id, s, U, n, τ, g, decoy flag, v' id, τ')

**Algorithm 2 — ImageScore(θ, v, {c^(u)}, m, seed)** (CRN image-direction estimate)

    1  for j = 1..m:
    2      t_j <- (j - U_j) / m  with U_j ~ Uniform(0,1) from seed ;  M_j <- each image position masked w.p. t_j (UniDisc schedule)
    3      for each u (batched): logp_u,j <- Σ_{i in M_j} log p_θ(v_i | v_{¬M_j}, c^(u))   # one forward per candidate
    4      ĥ_u,j <- w(t_j) * logp_u,j           # same (t_j, M_j) for every u
    5  return ĥ_u = mean_j ĥ_u,j

**Algorithm 3 — XBCStep(θ, batch, cache, α, β, λ, ρ)**

    1  for each example in batch:
    2      image <- v' if decoy flag else v ;  T <- τ' if decoy else τ
    3      ĥ <- ImageScore(θ, image, {c^(u)}, m, step seed)            # grad on
    4      z_u <- β ĥ_u + g_u + log n_u
    5      y <- softmax(T + log n)            if decoy
    6      y <- (1-α) e_{a_0} + α softmax(T + log n)   otherwise
    7      L_XBC <- - Σ_u y_u log softmax(z)_u
    8  backward(λ * mean L_XBC)            # first backward (frees the K+1 graphs)
    9  backward(mean L_MDM(v, c))          # second backward on the positive pairs; gradients accumulate
    10 optimizer step (AdamW), scheduler step, memory gate check (whole-GPU <= 9.0 GB)

**Algorithm 4 — Score(θ, row)** (evaluation): S_img, S_txt, S_sum as in sec. 3.5, plus the same scores with a
mismatched image (another val2017 image, seeded) for every row.

### 3.7 Complexity and extra passes (F = one forward pass of length T; T = 304 at 256 px, 1,072 at 512 px)

| arm | grad passes per example | no-grad passes (offline, once) | sequences in one graph | extra passes at generation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FT-pos | 1 (L_MDM) | 0 | b | 0 |
| BP-CML (α = 0, g = 0, ρ = 0) | 1 + d·m | 2/acc | d·b | 0 |
| XBC | 1 + d·m (d = distinct candidates <= K+1) | (2 + ρ)/acc + K+1 LM caption scores | d·b (two backwards) | 0 |

acc = acceptance rate of sec. 3.3 (unknown; HYP 0.3-0.5). With a LoRA grad pass at about 2.5 F (2 F upper-12 LoRA,
3 F all blocks; REDTEAM sec. 5), XBC at K = 1, m = 1 costs about 7.5 F per example vs 2.5 F for FT-pos (3x). Memory: the
red team's 512-px estimate for 16 sequences was 7.7-10 GB; activations scale with (sequences x tokens), so 256 px with
b = 4, K = 1 (8 sequences x 304 tokens, 14 % of that token count) is about 5-6 GB including weights (2.8 GB), workspace
and fp32 logits at masked image positions; K = 3 about 6.5-7.5 GB; 512 px with b = 4, K = 1 about 8.3-9.4 GB, i.e. at the
9 GB gate, so **512-px training is cloud-only**. All E0; the pilot measures them.

## 4. The structural property and whether XBC uses it

| model class | p(t \| i) | p(i \| t) | p(t), p(i) | exact one-token infill with both-side context, all vocab, one pass | can run XBC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| unified masked discrete diffusion (UniDisc, Muddit, MMaDA, Lumina-DiMOO) | yes (ELBO for many tokens; exact for one) | yes (ELBO) | yes via ∅ (quality depends on modality dropout in training) | **yes** | yes: teacher 2 passes, cacheable |
| unified AR (Janus, Show-o text side, Chameleon-type) | yes (exact) | Janus yes (exact, separate VQ path); Show-o masked image | yes | no: left context only; the Bayes posterior over K+1 fills needs K+1 suffix passes | yes, at K+1 teacher passes, and with Janus's decoupled encoders the two directions read different image representations |
| AR VLM (LLaVA-type) | yes | **no** | text only | no | no generation direction to teach |
| diffusion ITM on SD (HardNeg-DiffusionITM 2305.16397) | **no** | yes (ELBO) | p(i) via null text | no text model | no internal teacher; negatives must come from outside |
| CLIP / SigLIP | score only | score only | no | no | no densities, no self-proposal |

**Verdict.** XBC genuinely exploits the property: its teacher and its student are the two conditionals of one network,
and its proposal is the same network with the image removed. AR VLMs, CLIP and diffusion-ITM cannot run it at all.
Unified AR models can, at (K+1)-fold teacher cost and with a suffix term (REDTEAM F-A8), so the honest statement is
**"exact and cheap in masked models, possible but costlier in unified AR models"**. That makes it the central claim:

> In a unified masked discrete diffusion model, the understanding direction is an exact, one-pass Bayes teacher for the
> generation direction on self-generated hard candidates; training the generation direction toward it (with a prior
> slot that keeps caption-only evidence out of p(v | c)) makes the generator's conditional sensitive to the
> compositional distinctions its own understanding head already makes.

The optional unified-AR counterpart (Janus-1.3B or Show-o-1.3B, sec. 7.3) is there to measure the cost difference, not to
claim uniqueness. Caveat: MDM single-token conditionals need not be mutually consistent (Young and You, 2301.00068;
Torroba Hennigen and Kim, 2305.15501); XBC uses each conditional only as "the model's belief", which Lemma 1 permits.

### 5.2 Lemma 1, Corollary 1.1, Lemma 2 (stated for completeness; proofs one line each)

**Lemma 1** (known). Let a_0 | (v, c_{-s}) ~ P(. | v, c_{-s}) and a_1..a_K i.i.d. r(. | c_{-s}), independent of a_0
given (v, c_{-s}). For the unordered set, P(index i is true | set, v, c_{-s}) = softmax_i[log P(a_i | v, c_{-s}) - log r(a_i)].
*Proof:* the joint density of (true index i, values) is (K+1)^{-1} P(a_i | .) Π_{k≠i} r(a_k); divide by Π_k r(a_k).

**Corollary 1.1.** Lemma 1 still holds after conditioning on any event E whose indicator is a function of
(v, c_{-s}, s, noise independent of a_0). *Proof:* conditioning on E leaves P(a_0 | v, c_{-s}, E) = P(a_0 | v, c_{-s}).
Counterexample for redraw-on-reject: rejecting s' uses D_{s'}, a function of c_{-s'}, which contains c_s = a_0; the final
s then carries information about a_0. Hence Algorithm 1 discards rejected examples.

**Lemma 2** (known). For any joint P: log P(a | c_{-s}, v) - log P(a | c_{-s}) = log P(v | c_{-s}, a) - log P(v | c_{-s}).
So softmax_k[log P(a_k | c_{-s}, v) - log r(a_k)] = softmax_k[log P(v | c^(k)) + log P(a_k | c_{-s}) - log r(a_k)].

### 5.3 Proposition 2 (source of the caption-only detector; the prior slot)

**Statement.** Under Lemma 1's hypotheses, among critics f(v, c) used as softmax logits over the candidate set, the
population cross-entropy is minimised exactly by
f*(v, c^(k)) = log P(v | c^(k)) + Δ(c^(k)) + κ(v, c_{-s}),  Δ(c) = log P(a | c_{-s}) - log r(a | c_{-s}),
with κ arbitrary (set-constant). Δ is a function of the caption only, with E_{a~P}[Δ] = KL(P || r) and
E_{a~r}[Δ] = -KL(r || P); the expected logit advantage that Δ alone gives a true fill over a proposal fill is the Jeffreys
divergence J(P, r) = KL(P||r) + KL(r||P). If the logits are f(v, c) + g(c) with a fixed slot g = log P̃(a | c_{-s}) - log r(a),
the optimal f is log P(v | c) + log P(a|c_{-s}) - log P̃(a|c_{-s}) + κ, i.e. the residual caption-only term has Jeffreys
size J(P, P̃).

*Proof.* Lemma 1 gives the Bayes logits; Lemma 2 rewrites log P(a | c_{-s}, v) as log P(v | c) + log P(a | c_{-s}) minus a
set-constant. Log-loss is proper, so the population minimiser over unrestricted critics is any function equal to the Bayes
logits up to a set-constant. The expectations follow from the definitions of KL. With the slot, subtract g. ∎

**Consequences.** (i) The red team's F-A3b is not an optimisation accident: with r = p_0 ≠ P, BP-CML's optimum *requires*
the image NELBO to carry Δ. (ii) Because SugarCrepe positives are all human COCO captions, a learned Δ-like detector can
transfer to the test as "human original vs machine edit" (REDTEAM S-3). (iii) With the slot, what remains is the external
LM's error; the slot ablation (g = 0) and the mismatched-image control test the prediction. (iv) XBC's teacher logits τ
need no slot: τ is already the Lemma-1 logit under p_0's beliefs, whatever r is.

### 5.4 Proposition 3 (Jensen bias of the Monte-Carlo listwise loss)

**Statement.** Let ℓ(z) = -yᵀz + log Σ_u exp(z_u) with Σ y_u = 1. Let ĥ = h + ε with E[ε | h] = 0 (single-draw ELBO
estimates are unbiased for -NELBO), logits z = βh, ẑ = βĥ, and ε_⊥ = ε - (mean_u ε_u) 1. Then
0 <= E[ℓ(ẑ)] - ℓ(z) <= (β²/4) E||ε_⊥||².
Consequently, if J and Ĵ are the population exact and Monte-Carlo objectives and B(θ) the bound, then for the Monte-Carlo
minimiser θ̂ and the exact minimiser θ*: J(θ̂) <= J(θ*) + B(θ*).

*Proof.* ℓ is shift-invariant along 1, so ℓ(z + βε) = ℓ(z + βε_⊥). Convexity and Jensen give the lower bound. Taylor with
Lagrange remainder: ℓ(z + x) = ℓ(z) + ∇ℓ(z)ᵀx + ½ xᵀH(ξ)x, H = diag(q) - qqᵀ. For any x, xᵀHx = Var_q(x) <= range(x)²/4
<= ||x||²/2 (Popoviciu; (x_i - x_j)² <= 2(x_i² + x_j²)). The linear term has mean zero. Hence the excess is at most
¼ β² E||ε_⊥||². For the minimiser: J(θ̂) <= Ĵ(θ̂) <= Ĵ(θ*) <= J(θ*) + B(θ*). ∎

**Use.** CRN removes the shared part of ε; m draws divide E||ε_⊥||² by m. E||ε_⊥||² is measurable from replicate draws
(pilot step P-d), so the gap that F-A4 worried about becomes a reported number with a bound. With NELBO critics the
"exact" object is the expected NELBO, not log p; the ELBO gap is a separate, candidate-dependent shift (REDTEAM F-A5) that
Prop. 3 does not cover; it is reported as a limitation and probed by the slot and mismatched-image controls.

### 5.5 Propositions 1 and 4 (consistency, shrinkage, variance)

**Prop. 1.** (a) If p_θ can equal P and the teacher is P's own text conditional, then for every α in [0,1], λ >= 0 and
ρ in [0,1], P minimises the population objective (5) (with the prior slot exact, or with r = P(. | c_{-s})).
(b) With teacher posterior π_T ≠ π (the true candidate posterior), the population target is E[y | set, v] =
(1-α)π + απ_T, so the realisable image-side optimum is q* = (1-α)π + απ_T and KL(π || q*) <= α KL(π || π_T).
(c) For decoy examples the target is π_T(v'), so the image side is pushed toward the teacher's belief for v', which is
near uniform when v' is unrelated to c_{-s}.
*Proof.* (a) L_MDM is minimised by P; the cross-entropy part has target mean π (properness) and is minimised by q = π,
which P induces (Lemma 2 with exact slot). (b) Linearity of cross-entropy in the target; convexity of KL in its second
argument. (c) Same as (b) with α = 1. "Realisable" matters: the image-side posteriors are induced by one p_θ(v | c) across
all sets and need not reach q*. ∎

**Prop. 4** (known in substance, Menon et al. 2005.10419). The gradient of (3) is Σ_u (softmax(z)_u - y_u)∇z_u, linear in y.
With y = e_{a_0} the conditional mean given (set, v) is the same as with y = π, and by the law of total variance the
trace of the gradient covariance with the hard label exceeds that with y = π by E[Var(Σ_u y_u ∇z_u | set, v)] >= 0. So α
trades bias (b) against variance; synonym fills ("man" vs "person") that a hard label punishes get teacher mass instead
(addresses REDTEAM S-4 and B's false-negative point).

## 6. Novelty: honest verdict and upgrades

### 6.1 Verdict

**Incremental as a method (my estimate 0.55, pending a novelty audit of XBC specifically).** Every ingredient has a
precedent: image-direction likelihood fine-tuning on caption negatives (HardNeg-DiffusionITM 2305.16397), proposal-
corrected candidate softmax (1809.01812), self-generated negatives (ELECTRA 2003.10555, Electric 2012.08561, BPO
2403.08730), distillation as variance reduction (2005.10419), understanding-side signals improving a unified model's
generation (SILMM 2412.05818, self-feedback DPO for compositional T2I in AR LMMs; Unified-GRPO in 2509.09666,
reconstruction reward), generator-validator consistency training for LMs (2310.01846). What I did not find (five arXiv
queries today plus the audit's 40; absence is weak evidence): **training the generation conditional of a unified
discrete model toward the posterior given by its own understanding conditional over self-sampled candidates, with no
image generation in the loop, no reward model and no external negatives.** SILMM and Unified-GRPO generate images and
score them; XBC never samples an image during training, which is why it is cheap.

**Do not claim:** first hard-negative training of a generative VLM; Lemma 1, Lemma 2, M1-M4, P1-P5 as new; that
distillation reduces variance (known); that CML beats MLE; "blind-proof" against any text model stronger than p_0; first
likelihood evaluation of a masked diffusion captioner on SugarCrepe (2510.26799 did it); that understanding-generation
synergy is new as a question (RealUnify 2509.24897, Unison 2606.26984, synergy study 2609.01607).

**Strongest defensible sentence (if results hold):** "For unified masked discrete diffusion models, the understanding
conditional gives an exact one-pass Bayes posterior over self-sampled caption candidates; we use it to train the
generation conditional, with a frozen prior slot that provably keeps caption-only evidence out of p(v | c), and show on
[N] backbones that the generation direction becomes more sensitive to compositional distinctions at [cost], against
positive-only, hard-label, hinge, ranking-NCE and preference baselines at equal budget."

### 7.6 Statistics and resolution

- Unit: (row, seed); rows clustered by image; within-seed image-cluster bootstrap, then a hierarchical bootstrap over
  seeds; paired McNemar per seed as support. Effect sizes: accuracy difference in points, discordant-pair odds ratio,
  Cohen's h.
- **Resolution (E0 arithmetic).** All 7,511 rows sit on 1,560 images (HARDNEG sec. 1). The five length-matched subsets
  hold 4,757 rows; after removing family H (300 images) and the pilot (about 250-300 images), about 900-1,000 images and
  about 2,700-2,900 rows remain. With discordance 0.10-0.15 and an effective n of about 1,500, the paired SE is about 0.9
  points; adding a seed SD of 0.5-1 point over 3-5 seeds gives an MDE (80 % power, two-sided α 0.05) of about 2.6-3.3
  points, above the red team's 2-2.4 because their count ignored image clustering. **Decision margin: 3 points, or the
  statistician's MDE from pilot discordance if larger.** The pilot uses 60 rows per subset to protect the test pool.
- Seeds: 3 on the laptop (screening only), **5 for FT-pos, BP-CML and XBC on the final runs** (cloud), 3 for other arms.

### 7.7 HPO protocol (nested, multi-fidelity, equal per arm)

- **Nesting:** outer = test sets, touched once after freezing; inner = a 1,000-pair dev set on 1,000 held-out train2017
  images, **mixed type** so no arm is tuned on its own data type (E-7): one third external-LM single-token fills, one third
  rule replacements (cohyponyms, length-matched), one third symmetric swaps. Dev also used for early stopping and for
  choosing the S_img/S_txt/S_sum scorer per arm.
- **Search:** each arm searches **its own** shared hyperparameters (lr log-U[2e-5, 5e-4], rank {8, 16, 32}, depth {12, 24},
  λ log-U[0.1, 3]) plus its own specific ones (XBC: α U[0,1], β log-U[0.5, 2], ρ {0, 0.25}, K {1, 3} if memory allows;
  HardNeg: clip λ; BPO: DPO β; Electric: temperature). **12 Sobol trials per arm, the same for every arm** (fixes E-6),
  ASHA on examples 2.5K -> 7.5K -> 20K, η = 3 (12 -> 4 -> 1). Tuning metric: dev S_img accuracy (FT-pos also reports S_txt).
- **Fidelity check first:** 6 configurations (3 FT-pos, 3 XBC) at 2.5K and 20K; if Spearman ρ < 0.6, use 8 random trials at
  full fidelity per arm. Edge optima: one range extension, reported. Search seed 0; final seeds 1-5. Trial counts per arm
  in the paper.
