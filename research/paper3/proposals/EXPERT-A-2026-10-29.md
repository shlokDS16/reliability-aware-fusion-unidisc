# Paper 3: candidate new models (expert A)

## 0. Verdict in eight lines

1. **Rank 1, SCG (Self-Counterfactual Grounding)**: a LoRA-adapted unified masked diffusion model (UniDisc 1.4B) trained with

## 2. Notation (shared)

Image tokens v = (v_1..v_m), caption tokens t = (t_1..t_n), joint denoiser p_theta(x_i | x_unmasked) over masked positions.
"Blind" means the other modality fully masked (UniDisc is trained with whole-modality masking, so these conditionals exist;
our GATE pipeline already uses them). p_0 = the frozen pretrained UniDisc. For a text span S (positions) with context
t_{-S}:

  c_theta(x; S, t, v) = log p_theta(t_S = x | t_{-S}, v)          (image-conditional, trained)
  b_0(x; S, t)        = log p_0(t_S = x | t_{-S}, v = all-mask)    (image-blind, frozen reference)
  g_theta(x)          = c_theta(x) - b_0(x)                         (conditional PMI estimate)

For |S| > 1 both are factorised one-step conditionals (product over positions in S of the per-position marginals from one
pass with S masked). The image-direction quantities swap the roles of t and v with a region R of image tokens.

## 3. Candidate 1 (rank 1): SCG, Self-Counterfactual Grounding for unified masked diffusion

### 3.1 Idea

Train the denoiser so that, with the image visible, it prefers the true fill of a masked span over fills the *same model*
would write without looking at the image. Those fills are fluent and plausible in text (on the text manifold), so the only
way to reject them is to use the image: they are the negatives that Paper 2's Cor. 1.2 says no unimodal score can catch.

### 3.2 Training objective

For a pair (v, t) from the training set: sample a span S (type from {noun, adjective, relation/verb, random 1-3 tokens};
parser-free variant: random), mask it, and in **one no-grad pass** of p_0 with the image masked read the blind distribution
q(. | t_{-S}) = p_0(t_S | t_{-S}, blind). Draw K fills a_1..a_K ~ q (one step, factorised). In **one grad pass** of p_theta
with S masked and the image visible, read c_theta at the true fill a_0 = t_S and at every a_k (the input is identical for all
candidates because S is masked). Loss (listwise, Plackett-Luce first position = InfoNCE):

  L_SCG = - log [ exp(beta g_theta(a_0)) / sum_{k=0..K} exp(beta g_theta(a_k)) ]                     (1)

  L = L_SCG^text + L_SCG^img + lambda_MDM * L_MDM(v, t)                                                (2)

L_MDM is UniDisc's own masked ELBO on clean pairs (keeps generation). L_SCG^img is the same construction with an image region
R (4x4 to 8x8 tokens) masked, negatives drawn from p_0(v_R | v_{-R}, caption masked), image visible outside R, caption visible.
Identical fills (a_k = a_0) are kept: they contribute a constant to (1) and no gradient, so no filtering is needed (and
filtering would change q and break Prop. A1).

Why a frozen blind reference: if b were computed by the trained model, (1) could be lowered by degrading the language prior
for real captions instead of improving grounding. Freezing it removes that path (the analogue of DPO's reference). Check:
text-only perplexity of p_theta on COCO val captions must stay within a preregistered margin.

### 3.3 Inference

- Verification / matching (image-to-text and text-to-image): score s(t, v) = sum over a fixed deterministic partition of the
  caption into M = 4 mask groups of sum_{k in group} [c_theta(t_k) - b_0(t_k)] (blind terms cached once for all models and
  seeds, since b_0 is frozen); decision argmax over candidates. Same for the image direction. Length handling preregistered
  (sum vs mean; SugarCrepe add_* subsets are 2.5-4.5 tokens longer in the negative, [L] feasibility note section 4).
- Generation: unchanged UniDisc sampler; SCG is evaluated for "no harm" and for faithfulness (GenEval subset).

### 3.4 Theory hook (to be written as propositions and checked by red-team)

**Prop. A1 (Bayes-optimal critic, exact).** Let the true fill a_0 ~ P(t_S | t_{-S}, v) and K negatives a_k ~ q(. | t_{-S})
i.i.d., independent of v given t_{-S}. Given the unordered candidate set and v, the posterior that candidate i is the true
fill is softmax_i[ log P(a_i | t_{-S}, v) - log q(a_i | t_{-S}) ]. Proof: P(i true | set, v) is proportional to
P(a_i | ., v) * prod_{k != i} q(a_k | .), divide by prod_k q(a_k | .). So (1) with beta = 1 is the log-loss of a correctly
specified posterior model when c_theta = log P(. | t_{-S}, v) and b_0 = log q. Corollary: the image-conditional that minimises
L_MDM (the true conditional) also minimises L_SCG in the population, i.e. **the generative and the discriminative objectives
share a minimiser**; L_SCG re-weights a finite-capacity fit toward the image-dependent part of the conditional. This is the
point that separates SCG from losses that add a separate discriminative score (CLIP-style hard-negative losses).
Caveat: exact for |S| = 1 and for factorised one-step fills scored by the factorised conditional; for joint multi-token
fills it holds for the factorised surrogate only. State it so.

**Prop. A2 (blind-proof pairs).** If q = P(t_S | t_{-S}) (the true image-marginal conditional), then given t_{-S} the
candidates are exchangeable without v, so every score that does not read v has expected top-1 accuracy exactly 1/(K+1)
(mid-rank ties). With q = p_0 (the model's, not the truth), the deviation from chance of any image-free score is bounded by
the total variation between p_0 and P (Paper 2 Prop. 1(a) gives the AUROC form). Consequence: accuracy above chance on SCG
pairs is evidence of image use, which removes the "blind LM beats the VLM" failure that Lin et al. [A] report for generative
scores on adversarial benchmarks. It also gives a **new blind-proof evaluation set** if the test-time generator is a
*different* text model (so the model under test is not scored on its own generator).

**Prop. A3 (cost).** Because S is masked in the input, the positive and all K negatives are scored from the same grad pass;
a pairwise/listwise step costs one forward-backward plus one no-grad blind pass, independent of K. An autoregressive DPO step
scores chosen and rejected under policy and reference (four sequence passes). This is a property of masked diffusion and
worth one paragraph and one measured throughput table.

Link to Paper 2 (non-overlapping): Paper 2 measured that a caption-withheld gate cannot fix corruption decisions; Paper 3
changes the *model* using the cross-modal information Paper 2's evidence-separation rule deliberately excluded, and uses
Paper 2's Prop. 1 to say which negatives are informative. Paper 3 must not re-run Paper 2's question (EXP-003 rule also holds).

## 4. Candidate 2 (rank 2): RGA, Reliability-Gated cross-modal Attention masked diffusion

### 4.1 Architecture

Reliability head at layer l* (e.g. 8 of 24): rho_j = w^T LN(H^{l*}_j) + b, r_j = sigmoid(rho_j). Two variants:
r^uni (computed in a pass with the other modality masked: caption-withheld, obeys Paper 2's evidence-separation rule) and
r^x (computed in the joint pass: sees both modalities). In every layer l > l*, head h:

  A^{l,h}_{ij} = q_i . k_j / sqrt(d_h) + softplus(gamma^{l,h}) * 1[mod(i) != mod(j)] * log(r_j + eps)     (3)

with gamma initialised so that softplus(gamma) = 0 at step 0 (model identical to UniDisc at initialisation). Equivalently the
attention weight to key j is multiplied by r_j^{beta}: a tempered reliability prior on which cross-modal keys a query reads.
Implemented as an additive float bias to SDPA (this disables the flash kernel; memory per layer O(T^2): 16 heads x 1100^2 x
2 B x B = 0.31 GB at B = 8, fine with per-layer checkpointing; kernel choice on our torch build unverified, check in P0).

### 4.2 Training

Corruption process per training pair (labels z_j = 1 if token j was corrupted): (i) masking (standard); (ii) uniform code
replacement; (iii) realistic pixel corruptions (JPEG, noise, blur, splice) re-tokenised offline, z = changed code; (iv)
on-manifold resampling of a region from p_0(v_R | v_{-R}, caption masked); (v) cross-modal swap (a region taken from an image
of a different caption). Loss:

  L_RGA = L_MDM^{mix}(clean targets at masked AND corrupted positions) + lambda_r * sum_j BCE(r_j, 1 - z_j) [+ lambda_s L_SCG]   (4)

### 4.3 Inference

Verification: S(c) = sum_j w(r^uni_j) Delta_j(c), with w the SNR-optimal transform of the posterior (below) fitted on dev;
plus the in-network gating, which changes Delta_j itself (this is what post-hoc gating in Paper 2 could not do). Generation
and editing: remask tokens with r^x below a threshold (PRISM-like, but cross-modal).

### 4.4 Theory hook

- Paper 2 Prop. 1(d)-(e) and Cor. 1.2: r^uni is provably at AUROC 1/2 on corruption (iv) (Gibbs-invariant resampling);
  r^x is not. **Preregistered, falsifiable prediction**: r^uni AUROC on (iv) within [0.45, 0.55]; r^x above it; r^uni well
  above 0.5 on (ii)-(iii). Getting the predicted pattern is itself a result (theory confirmed in a 1.4B model).
- Sufficiency (extends Paper 2 Prop. 3): under a two-class mixture of token evidence (clean: mean mu_0, var s_0^2; corrupt:
  mu_1, s_1^2) the SNR-optimal weight is w(p) = [(1-p) mu_0 + p mu_1] / [(1-p) s_0^2 + p s_1^2 + p(1-p)(mu_0 - mu_1)^2],
  a function of the posterior p = P(z = 1 | features) only. So a calibrated detector is sufficient for optimal soft weighting
  (to be proven with Paper 2's correlated-token caveats).
- Explaining-away rule: a reliability that conditions on the hypothesis caption can down-weight exactly the evidence against
  that caption, so r^x must never weight the verification of the same caption. This is a design constraint stated as a
  proposition, not a footnote.

## 6. Protocol (all candidates)

**Splits.** Unit of generalisation = image. Train and dev from COCO train2017, grouped by image id; tests are fixed external
benchmarks, evaluated once per final model. Leak checks: image-id disjointness train vs every test set (VG/COCO id mapping if
VG is ever added); caption string match train vs test; SugarCrepe rows one per image for any per-image statistic.

**HPO (equal budget per fine-tuned family).** 24 trials per family, Sobol sampling, ASHA on the training-examples axis
(2.5K -> 7.5K -> 20K, eta = 3), Optuna storage on disk for resumability. Before trusting the pruner: 6 configs run to full
fidelity at 256 px, Spearman rank correlation of dev accuracy at 2.5K vs 20K examples must be >= 0.6, else plain random
search at full fidelity with 12 trials. Spaces (SCG): lr log-U[2e-5, 5e-4]; LoRA rank {8, 16, 32}; LoRA depth {8, 12, 24};
beta log-U[0.1, 2]; lambda_MDM log-U[0.1, 3]; K {1, 4, 8}; span mix {uniform, noun-heavy}. Fixed: AdamW, cosine with 5 %
warmup, grad clip 1.0, bf16, batch 8 (accumulation if memory forces it; batch is never changed without retuning lr).
RGA adds l* {6, 8, 12}, lambda_r log-U[0.1, 3], corruption mix. Optimum on the edge of a range triggers one range extension
and is reported. Tuning metric: dev pairwise accuracy on the rule-based and external-LM generator pairs (not the own
generator). Search seed 0; final seeds 1-5. Test sets are never used for tuning or early stopping.

**Final evaluation.** 5 seeds for SCG and the two decisive baselines (FT-rule, FT-pos); 3 seeds for other arms and ablations
(disclosed). Primary endpoint (to be frozen in a SAP by the statistician): SugarCrepe macro accuracy over the 7 subsets,
image-to-text, SCG vs FT-rule; secondary: SugarCrepe-C severity 3, Winoground text score, What'sUp, blind-proof set, GenEval
no-harm margin. Uncertainty: hierarchical bootstrap (images, then seeds); paired test on per-image correctness averaged over
seeds; Holm over the primary family. Report ties and losses per subset.

**Sanity checks before any long run.** Overfit 64 pairs; initial-loss check (L_SCG at step 0 equals the zero-shot PMI
log-loss); shuffled-image training erases the gain; blind scorer at chance on the blind-proof set.

## 7. Compute plan on 12 GB (arithmetic; P0 replaces every assumption)

Assumptions (unverified, measured in P0): sustained 25 TFLOP/s bf16 on the laptop; duty cycle 0.5 (wall = 2 x compute);
N = 1.4e9; T = 1,100 tokens at 512 px (1,024 image + up to 76 text), T = 320 at 256 px (if UniDisc works at 256 px; unverified).
One forward F = 2 N T = 3.1e12 FLOP (512 px), 0.9e12 (256 px). Grad pass with LoRA on the upper 12 blocks and per-layer
checkpointing ~ 2.5 F (forward, recompute of the upper half, activation backward of the upper half).

### 7.1 Memory at 512 px, batch 8

bf16 weights 2.8 GB; LoRA params plus fp32 master and Adam states ~ 0.25 GB (rank 16 on all attention and MLP projections,
~14M params x 16 B);
checkpointed block inputs 8 x 1,100 x 2,048 x 2 B x 24 = 0.86 GB; one block recompute ~ 0.4 GB; logits only at masked
positions, fp32, chunked (MDM pass at 50 % masking: 8 x 550 x 48,385 x 4 B = 0.85 GB if not chunked); CUDA context and
workspace ~ 1 GB. Total ~ 6.3 GB. Cap: 9.0 GB whole-GPU, gated during each chunk; fallback batch 4 x accumulation 2.
Full fine-tuning is impossible: AdamW fp32 states and master weights for 1.4e9 params ~ 22 GB.

### 7.2 Cost per training example (SCG)

1 grad pass (span scoring, all K at once) 2.5 F + 1 no-grad blind pass 1 F + L_MDM every second step 1.25 F = 4.75 F
= 1.5e13 FLOP (512 px) -> 0.59 s compute, 1.2 s wall; 256 px -> 0.17 s compute, 0.35 s wall.
One run of 20K examples: 6.7 h wall (512 px), 1.9 h wall (256 px). RGA: ~3.5 F to 4.5 F per example, similar.

### 7.3 Totals for SCG (wall hours, attended)

| item | 256 px | 512 px |
|---|---|---|
| P0 pilot (memory, throughput, zero-shot headroom 100/subset + blind, generator audit sample) | 6 | 8 |
| HPO: 6 fidelity-check runs + 24 trials x 5 families x ~0.3 run | 12 + 68 | 40 + 240 |
| final: 3 arms x 5 seeds + 3 arms x 3 seeds = 24 runs | 46 | 160 |

HPO dominates; to keep it affordable, families share the search for common hyperparameters (lr, rank, depth) and only the
family-specific ones are searched per family, which cuts HPO to about one third (256 px total ~ 140-160 h). At ~6 attended
GPU-hours per day that is 4-5 weeks, in parallel with other sessions' GPU needs: tight. 512 px is not feasible locally.

## 8. Risks and kill criteria

| id | when | criterion | action |
|---|---|---|---|
| K-H (headroom) | P0, day 1 | zero-shot UniDisc PMI accuracy on SugarCrepe (100 per subset) >= 0.92 macro, or the blind score within 0.03 of it on >= 5 subsets | switch primary test to Winoground / What'sUp / SugarCrepe-C; if all at ceiling or all at chance, stop SCG |
| K-G (generator) | P0 | hand audit of 100 self-negatives: < 50 % are true negatives for the image (rest paraphrases or still true) | change span types; if still < 50 %, SCG loses its efficiency argument; continue only with K >= 4 |
| K-L (low fidelity) | after 2.5K-example pilot, 3 configs | dev accuracy gain over FT-pos < 2 points | stop SCG |
| K-P (primary) | final | SCG vs FT-rule interval includes 0 and point gain < 1 point | report as a tie; the paper becomes theory + blind-proof benchmark, not "a new model" |
| K-N (no harm) | final | text perplexity up > 10 % or GenEval down > 2 points at the chosen lambda_MDM | re-tune lambda_MDM once; else report the trade-off |
| K0 (RGA, CPU, now) | after the learned-ceiling analysis (`research/paper2/posthoc/LEARNED-CEILING-PLAN-2026-10-29.md`) | not a kill by itself: a flat post-hoc ceiling says weighting fixed evidence cannot help, so RGA must show in P0 that gating restores Delta at intact tokens under noise | if the 1-day RGA pilot does not move mean Delta at intact tokens under noise sigma 0.1 by a margin set in the SAP, stop RGA |
| K-D (RGA theory) | RGA pilot | r^uni AUROC on off-manifold corruption < 0.8 | stop; the head cannot carry the gate |
| K-A (RGA architecture) | final | gated vs corruption-augmented un-gated LoRA: gain < 1 point with interval including 0 | drop the architecture claim |

Other risks: COCO captions (SugarCrepe positives) may be memorised by UniDisc (unmeasured); add a membership check (likelihood
of COCO val captions vs paraphrases) and lean on Winoground/What'sUp for the generalisation claim. Concurrent work on
post-training unified MDMs is moving monthly (re-run the novelty search at submission). GPU crashes (guarded chunks,
checkpoint every chunk). A1's exactness is limited to factorised fills; say so.
