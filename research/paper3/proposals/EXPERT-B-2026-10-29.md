## 0. Ranked summary

| Rank | Model | One-line idea | What it fixes in the field | Novelty (search-level) | Laptop-only feasible? | Weeks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **CB-MDM**: Contrastive Bidirectional Masked Diffusion | Train a unified MDM so that its own masked ELBOs, in both directions (image given text, text given image), rank the true caption above *hard* negatives, with a loss whose population minimiser is the same as the generative loss | Unified MDMs are trained only on positive pairs; they cannot learn "what the image is *not*", and random-negative matching is saturated (our Paper 2 data) | moderate-high; closest is HardNeg-DiffusionITM (continuous SD, one direction) | yes, slowly (about 60-80 attended GPU-h); cloud recommended | 8-9 |

**Recommendation.** Lead with **CB-MDM (1)** and fold **Co-MDM's coherence term (2)** into it as a principled second
component and an ablation. The combined paper has one objective with three short theorems (shared population minimiser;
gradient saturation of random negatives, which explains the Paper 2 null from the training side; common-random-number
variance reduction), one matched-size triad of counterparts (masked discrete: UniDisc 1.4B; autoregressive: Janus 1.3B;
continuous: Stable Diffusion 2.1 with HardNeg-DiffusionITM), and both understanding and generation endpoints. That is
the version I think could be among the stronger TNNLS papers: a new learning objective with theory that a reader can
apply to any unified MDM, tested where the headroom is (hard negatives), with the "random negatives teach nothing"
finding from Paper 2 turned into a proposition rather than left as a null.

Hard-rule check: none of the three repeats EXP-003 (UniDisc joint text-image *sample* consistency as a function of the
sampler). Candidates 1-2 change the *training objective* and are evaluated on likelihood matching and on text-to-image
generation; no joint text-image sampling or sampler comparison is planned. No screening or review method anywhere.

Gaps I infer (INF, from the above and our own data):

- **G1. Positive-only training.** Every unified MDM above is trained with a denoising ELBO on positive pairs plus, at
  most, RL with an external reward. HardNeg-DiffusionITM (2305.16397) states the same gap for continuous diffusion and
  closes it only for Stable Diffusion, in one direction. No unified masked discrete model is trained to separate a
  caption from its minimally edited negative.
- **G2. Random negatives are saturated.** OURS: on COCO with a random other caption, UniDisc's clean none-arm accuracy
  is 0.880 and corruptions that change most image codes flip only 1.0-1.8 % of decisions (A2.3 audit, section 4);
  the primary GATE family was negative (paper2/sections/results.tex, Table res_primary). The audit's mechanism: the
  per-item evidence margin is far larger than the corruption's shift. Any objective or test built on random negatives
  has little signal; hard negatives are where the margin is small (HARDNEG-FEASIBILITY: SugarCrepe, 7,511 rows,
  MIT, COCO val2017 images).
- **G3. One clock for all modalities and no coherence constraint.** UniDisc (E3) and the tri-modal MDM (E2-partial) use
  one masking time per sequence. Both conditionals p(T|I) and p(I|T) and both marginals live in one network, so the
  identity log p(I) + log p(T|I) = log p(T) + log p(I|T) is available as a training signal, but nobody uses it. OURS
  (probe 1, research/paper2/IDEAS.md): the two directions of UniDisc disagree in accuracy (text 0.730, image 0.783;
  PMI-normalised text 0.940), which is at least consistent with incoherence (INF, not tested).
- **G4. Absorbing MDMs cannot revise visible conditioning tokens.** Self-correction work is text-only, generation-only
  or training-free (list above). OURS: GATE detects corrupted image codes (AUROC about 0.96, A2.3 audit 3.4) but does
  not change decisions on random negatives; under noise sigma 0.10 the changed-code oracle raises accuracy by 0.070
  (results.tex Table res_mech), so a *trained* repair has measured headroom there.

## 2. Candidate 1 (rank 1): CB-MDM, Contrastive Bidirectional Masked Diffusion

### 2.1 Formulation
Pair $x=(x^I,x^T)$; image codes $x^I\in[V_I]^{N_I}$, text tokens $x^T\in[V_T]^{N_T}$. Absorbing forward process with
schedule $\alpha_t$; $M_t$ the masked set; $p_\theta(\cdot\mid x_t)$ the denoiser (UniDisc backbone + LoRA).

Directional conditional NELBOs (mask one modality, the other fully visible):
$$\ell_\theta(x^I\mid c)=\mathbb E_{t,M_t}\Big[w(t)\sum_{i\in M_t\cap I}-\log p_\theta\big(x^I_i\mid x^I_{t},\,c\big)\Big]\;\ge\;-\log p_\theta(x^I\mid c),$$
$$\ell_\theta(c\mid x^I)=\mathbb E_{t,M_t}\Big[w(t)\sum_{i\in M_t\cap T}-\log p_\theta\big(c_i\mid c_t,\,x^I\big)\Big],\qquad \ell_\theta(c\mid\varnothing)\ \text{(image fully masked)}.$$
Scores (higher = better match), length-normalised by the number of scored tokens:
$$s^{\to I}_\theta(x^I,c)=-\ell_\theta(x^I\mid c)/N_I,\qquad s^{\to T}_\theta(x^I,c)=\big[\ell_\theta(c\mid\varnothing)-\ell_\theta(c\mid x^I)\big]/N_T\ \ (\text{PMI form}).$$
For a fixed image, $s^{\to I}$ differences are PMI differences because $\ell(x^I\mid\varnothing)$ cancels.

Hard-negative objective over a candidate set $C=\{c^+,c^-_1,\dots,c^-_K\}$ (mined hard negatives + one random):
$$\mathcal L_{\rm NCE}^{d}=-\log\frac{\exp(\beta\,\hat s^{d}_\theta(x^I,c^+))}{\sum_{c\in C}\exp(\beta\,\hat s^{d}_\theta(x^I,c))},\qquad d\in\{\to I,\to T\},$$
where $\hat s$ is a one-sample Monte-Carlo NELBO estimate computed with **common random numbers**: the same $t$ and the
same mask $M_t$ for every $c\in C$ (image direction: same image mask; text direction: same relative text mask, padded).
Total loss:
$$\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\rm MDM}(x^I,c^+)\;+\;\lambda_I\,\mathcal L^{\to I}_{\rm NCE}\;+\;\lambda_T\,\mathcal L^{\to T}_{\rm NCE}\;\;[+\;\lambda_c\,\mathcal L_{\rm coh}\ \text{from Candidate 2}].$$
Variants as ablations: reference-anchored scores $\hat s_\theta-\hat s_{\rm ref}$ (Diffusion-DPO / VRPO style), the
HardNeg-DiffusionITM clipped-negated loss ported to MDM, and pairwise ($K=1$) logistic.

**Architecture.** Unchanged UniDisc transformer; LoRA (rank 16-32) on attention and MLP projections; no new head (the
scores are the model's own ELBOs). This matters: the method is an *objective*, portable to MMaDA, Lumina-DiMOO or
LaViDa-O.

**Inference.** (a) Matching: $s=\tfrac12(z(s^{\to I})+z(s^{\to T}))$ with $K_{\rm eval}$ mask samples and CRN across
candidates. (b) Generation: the standard UniDisc sampler and CFG, unchanged, so any T2I gain is from the weights.
(c) Optional self-reranking: best-of-$N$ T2I samples ranked by $s^{\to T}$ (reported separately, compute-matched).

### 2.2 Theory hook (three propositions, to be written and red-teamed)
- **P1 (shared population minimiser).** With exact log-likelihoods and $\beta=1$, the InfoNCE-optimal critic is
  $f^*(x^I,c)=\log p(x^I\mid c)+g(x^I)$ (Poole et al. 1905.06922; CPC 1807.03748). So if $p_{\rm data}$ is in the
  model class, $\theta^*$ minimising $\mathcal L_{\rm MDM}$ also minimises $\mathcal L^{\to I}_{\rm NCE}$, and $\mathcal L$
  has the same minimiser for every $\lambda\ge0$: the discriminative term reallocates finite capacity, it does not
  bias the generative target. With NELBOs, the perturbation is bounded by the spread of the ELBO gap across captions;
  for absorbing MDMs the gap is the Jensen gap over generation orders (ARDM 2110.02037; RADD 2406.03736), which is
  zero for an order-coherent model. That links P1 to Candidate 2.
- **P2 (gradient saturation; why random negatives teach nothing).** For $K=1$,
  $\nabla\mathcal L=-\beta\,\sigma(-\beta\Delta)\nabla\Delta$ with $\Delta=\hat s(c^+)-\hat s(c^-)$. If $\Delta$ has
  mean $\mu>0$ and variance $v$, $\mathbb E\,\sigma(-\beta\Delta)\le 4v/\mu^2+e^{-\beta\mu/2}$ (Chebyshev split at
  $\mu/2$). Random negatives have large $\mu^2/v$ (OURS, qualitatively: margins far exceed corruption shifts, A2.3
  section 4), so their gradient vanishes; hard negatives with small $\mu^2/v$ carry it. The same ratio sets the
  power of a paired test, so P2 connects the Paper 2 null (evaluation) to training.
- **P3 (CRN variance).** $\operatorname{Var}[\hat\Delta_{\rm CRN}]=\operatorname{Var}\hat s(c^+)+\operatorname{Var}\hat s(c^-)-2\operatorname{Cov}$;
  sharing $(t,M_t)$ makes the covariance large because most NELBO variance comes from the mask draw (analogue of
  VRPO, 2505.19223, for preference pairs; our delta is shared *image* masks across captions). Measurable on CPU from
  stored per-token arrays of a scoring run.

### 2.7 Training recipe on 12 GB and GPU-hour arithmetic (E0 unless marked)
- Memory: 1.4B weights in bf16 about 2.8 GB; LoRA rank 32 adds tens of millions of parameters and optimiser state in
  the hundreds of MB; gradient checkpointing per block; logits 1072 x 48,385 in fp32 about 0.2 GB per sequence;
  micro-batch 2-4 sequences, gradient accumulation to an effective 32 triples. OURS reference point: a joint 512-px
  sample used peak 8.2 GB (IDEAS.md). Keep the project's 75 % memory cap and in-chunk stop (laptop-gpu-crashes memory).
- Time: OURS, inference took about 0.10 s per network call (6.6 s / 64 calls; whether a call included a CFG pair was
  not checked). Training forward + backward with recomputation is about 3-4x a forward, so about 0.3-0.4 s per
  1072-token sequence (E0). One triple with $K=1$ needs 5 sequences (joint generative, 2 image-direction, 2
  text-direction): about 1.5-2 s. 25k triples, 1 epoch: **about 10-14 laptop GPU-h per run** (E0). If the checkpoint
  runs at 256 px (256 image tokens, sequence about 300), divide by about 3.
- Evaluation: SugarCrepe with 300 rows per subset (2,100 rows), 4 candidate-scores per row, $K_{\rm eval}=4$ mask
  samples: about 16 calls x 0.1 s = 1.6 s per row, about 1 h per model; GenEval 553 prompts x 4 images x 64 steps,

- Program: 3 seeds of CB-MDM and of the positive-only control, 6 one-seed ablations, Janus counterpart (2 arms x 3 seeds),
  SD counterpart (2 arms x 1-3 seeds): about 20-25 training runs and 15 evaluated models. **Laptop only: 250-350 GPU-h**,

### 2.9 Risks and kill criteria (preregistered before any training)
- **K0 headroom (uses Paper 2 family H, no new GPU):** if base UniDisc accuracy on the SugarCrepe rows is >= 0.95 in
  every subset, or within 0.02 of a text-only blind scorer, stop (no headroom or no grounding).
- **K1 pilot (2k triples, about 1.5-3 laptop GPU-h):** on held-out generated negatives (COCO train2017 held-out images,
  not SugarCrepe), CB-MDM must beat the positive-only LoRA by >= 5 points; else stop.
- **K2 generation guard:** GenEval overall must not fall by more than 0.02 and FID-5k must not rise by more than 10 %
  against the positive-only control; one pre-stated retry with lower $\lambda$, then stop.
- **K3 language-prior check:** a text-only scorer fine-tuned the same way must recover less than half of CB-MDM's
  SugarCrepe gain; otherwise the claim is "learned a caption prior", reported as such.
- Risks: novelty overlap with DPT/HardNeg-DiffusionITM (mitigated by the bidirectional unified setting and P1-P3);
  UniDisc's missing licence (release LoRA deltas and code, not merged weights; ask the authors); UniDisc's weak T2I may
  make GenEval noisy (use 4 seeds per prompt and paired tests); GPU crashes (chunked training with checkpoints every
  10-15 min, the existing duty-cycle runner).

## 3. Candidate 2 (rank 2): Co-MDM, a coherent two-path masked diffusion model

### 3.1 Formulation
A unified MDM gives two factorisations of the same pair:
$$\mathcal N_{T\to I}(x)=\ell_\theta(x^T\mid\varnothing)+\ell_\theta(x^I\mid x^T),\qquad \mathcal N_{I\to T}(x)=\ell_\theta(x^I\mid\varnothing)+\ell_\theta(x^T\mid x^I).$$
For any coherent joint with exact likelihoods both equal $-\log p(x^I,x^T)$; equivalently the two PMIs agree,
$\mathrm{PMI}^{\to I}=\ell(x^I\mid\varnothing)-\ell(x^I\mid x^T)=\ell(x^T\mid\varnothing)-\ell(x^T\mid x^I)=\mathrm{PMI}^{\to T}$.
Incoherence $\kappa_\theta(x)=\mathcal N_{T\to I}(x)-\mathcal N_{I\to T}(x)$. Training loss:
$$\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\rm MDM}+\lambda_c\,\mathbb E_x\big[\rho(\hat\kappa_\theta(x))\big],\quad \rho=\text{Huber},$$
with $\hat\kappa$ from 4 CRN-coupled sequences (text masked / image masked, with and without the other modality).
Also a **two-clock training distribution**: per-modality mask ratios $(t_I,t_T)\sim\pi$ with mass on the corners
(conditionals, marginals) and the diagonal (joint), replacing UniDisc's single $t$ plus whole-modality masking (E3,
model.py:844, 469-528). MDMs are time-agnostic (2409.02908, 2406.03736), so no time embedding is needed; $\pi$ only
moves training mass between conditionals.

Inference: symmetric PMI $\tfrac12(\mathrm{PMI}^{\to I}+\mathrm{PMI}^{\to T})$ for matching; $|\hat\kappa|$ as an
uncertainty signal (flag pairs the model is internally inconsistent about); optional compute-matched best-of-$N$ T2I.

### 3.2 Theory hook
- Coherence is necessary for a joint: $\kappa\equiv0$ for every exact joint; $\mathbb E[\kappa]$ is the difference of
  two ELBO gaps plus model incoherence, so $\mathcal L_{\rm coh}$ also shrinks the order-Jensen gap of the NELBO
  (ARDM 2110.02037), tightening the likelihood used for matching. The regulariser shares the population minimiser with
  $\mathcal L_{\rm MDM}$ (zero at $p_{\rm data}$).
- Variance: symmetric PMI averages two estimators with correlation $\rho$; variance falls by $(1+\rho)/2$; measurable.
- Two-clock allocation: the NELBO weights each conditional by the time it spends near the corresponding corner of the
  $(t_I,t_T)$ square; a proposition can give the allocation that minimises the variance of the PMI estimator at fixed
  training budget (to derive; HYP).

### 3.3 Closest prior work and delta
Dual Supervised Learning 1707.00415 (Xia et al., ICML 2017) imposes $P(x)P(y\mid x)=P(y)P(x\mid y)$ between **two
separate models** with marginals from external LMs; Dual Learning 1611.00179; cycle consistency ITIT 2310.03734;
UniDiffuser 2303.06555 (per-modality noise levels, continuous); LatentUMM 2605.17766 (latent alignment), Unified-GRPO
2509.09666 (reconstruction reward), Unison 2606.26984 (consistency benchmark). Delta: one MDM supplies all four
terms, so the constraint is internal, cheap and exact up to ELBO gaps; tied to the order-coherence of MDMs; two-clock
masking. Novelty risk: moderate (the identity is classical; its use as an internal MDM regulariser appears new at
search level, E1).

## 4. Candidate 3 (rank 3): RH-MDM, reliability-aware hybrid-noise unified MDM

### 4.1 Formulation
Per-modality forward kernel: each token of modality $m$ survives with probability $\alpha_t$; a corrupted token becomes
[MASK] with probability $1-\varepsilon_m$ and is replaced by $v'\sim Q_m(\cdot\mid v)$ with probability $\varepsilon_m$.
$Q_I$ is **data-driven**: the empirical code-transition kernel of VQ re-encodes of degraded images (JPEG, noise, blur;
the GATE pipeline already produces such pairs: OURS, JPEG 10 changed 986.7 of 1024 codes on the headroom items,
results.tex Table res_headroom); $Q_T$ is a character-level typo kernel pushed through the tokenizer. The denoiser
predicts $x_0$ at **all** positions (not SUBS copy-through), plus a reliability head $r_\theta^i\approx P(x^i_t=x^i_0\mid x_t)$.
Loss: hybrid-kernel NELBO (GIDD 2503.04482 weights) + BCE on $r$. Inference: (i) matching with SNR-optimal
reliability weights $S=\sum_i w(r_i)\Delta_i/\sum_i w(r_i)$ (theory reference: THEORY-ADDENDUM Prop. 3, which needs
its r1 corrections); (ii) remask tokens with $r_i<\tau$, $\tau$ conformally calibrated on clean data (GATE calibrate.py),
then re-infill before scoring or captioning.

### 4.2 Theory hook
Bayes reliability under the hybrid kernel:
$\operatorname{logit} r^*_i=\log\frac{p(v_i\mid x_{-i})}{\sum_u p(u\mid x_{-i})Q(v_i\mid u)}+\log\frac{\alpha}{(1-\alpha)\varepsilon}$,
so the trained denoiser plus a known kernel gives reliability in closed form; GATE's training-free statistic is a
plug-in estimate of the first term with a crude null. Corollary (matches THEORY-ADDENDUM Prop. 1): corruptions drawn
from the model's own conditional are undetectable, which predicts the hard "plausible corruption" cell of
Trust-but-Verify (project read: recall 0.035-0.35).

### 4.3 Prior work, delta, baselines
Prior: GIDD, SCDD 2603.02230, XDLM 2602.01362, Trust-but-Verify 2610.22380, Token-Critic 2209.04439, PRISM 2510.01384,
ProSeCo 2602.11590, Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion-Image 2606.29814, FreeCorrection 2602.02927, CO2Jump 2607.13188.
Delta: unified image-text, corrupted *conditioning* (not own outputs), modality-specific data-driven kernels, closed-form
reliability, conformal remasking. Narrow and crowded. Baselines: base UniDisc, GATE (training-free), pixel denoiser
comparator (already in Paper 2), uniform-kernel GIDD-style fine-tune (no data kernel), Token-Critic-style separate
critic, FreeCorrection, positive-only LoRA. Data: COCO train2017 with synthetic degradations; tests on COCO val with
ImageNet-C-style corruptions (protocol 1907.07484 already used), SugarCrepe under corruption, typo prompts on GenEval.

### 4.4 Compute, risks, kill, timeline
2 sequences per example (corrupted + clean target pass) about 0.8 s (E0); building $Q_I$ needs VQ re-encodes (cheap,
encoder only). About 80-120 laptop GPU-h total. Kill: on the K1 pilot, accuracy under noise sigma 0.10 must exceed
the changed-code oracle's +0.070 (OURS headroom) over base; else stop. Main risk: Paper 2 showed that random-negative
decisions barely depend on which codes are corrupted, so gains may only appear under noise or on hard negatives;
reviewers will see it as GATE-plus-training. Timeline 9-10 weeks.

## 5. Why candidate 1 (with 2 inside) can make a strong TNNLS paper
- **Learning-systems contribution**, not an evaluation: a new objective with a consistency guarantee (P1), a mechanism
  for when it helps (P2, which is also the explanation of our own random-negative null), and an estimator (P3).
- **Headroom by design:** trained and tested where decisions are close (hard negatives), the axis where Paper 2 found
  its limit.
- **Unification payoff measured, not asserted:** the discriminative signal enters the same weights that generate; GenEval
  attribute binding and position are the test of "understanding improves generation", a question RealUnify, Unison and
  2607.04423 report as open.
- **Matched-size counterparts:** masked discrete (UniDisc 1.4B), autoregressive (Janus 1.3B) and continuous (SD 2.1)
  trained with the same objective on the same triples: a reviewer cannot say the gain is the objective on one family only.
- **Rigour carried over from Paper 2:** preregistration, positive-only matched control, blind-text control, paired tests,
  CRN, per-item arrays on disk.
- What would weaken it: K0 shows no SugarCrepe headroom; generation does not move (then the paper is an understanding
  paper with a theory section, still publishable, less striking); novelty audit finds a 2026 unified-MDM hard-negative paper.
