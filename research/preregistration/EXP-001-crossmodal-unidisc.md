## 0. Summary

One frozen checkpoint (UniDisc 1.4B interleaved, revision 2b344ca8, no training). Task: **prefix-conditioned joint completion**. A short real COCO caption prefix is observed; the remaining 32 caption positions and the 512-px image are generated jointly with plain sampling at temperature 1 and no guidance as the primary value rule. Two measurements. (1) The per-step trace of the model's own cross-modal dependence S_theta in both directions with the mixture-reference estimator, stratified so that length or termination information is separated from content, with validity gates (H-A). (2) A family of commit schedules at matched forward passes and matched per-window per-modality token budgets, differing in which windows commit text and image tokens in separate passes, plus a text-front joint arm that separates "text earlier" from "not co-committed" (H-B), scored by a matched-minus-shuffled CLIP lift and read through three conditions (matched-driven, completion-driven, VLM-concordant) before it is worded as consistency. Window arms test whether the T->I price predicts where separation helps beyond time (H-C). A positive, a null and a reversed result are each reported. **Framing (novelty audit): the contribution is the measurement (H-A) and the test of whether it predicts the cost of joint commitment (H-C, DA - DA-low, DA - TFJ); the DA arm tests the framework's prediction and credits DAPD for the principle; the statistical primary remains H-B1.**

## 1. Claim contract

### 1.1 Claim, one sentence
In an all-discrete unified model sampled with many tokens per pass, given a short caption prefix, the model's own cross-modal dependence between the jointly generated caption remainder and the image is concentrated early in the trajectory and on object words (under within-modality confidence ranking), and commit schedules that do not co-commit the high-dependence text-image pairs yield higher text-image consistency than joint commitment at the same number of forward passes.

Credit and scope (novelty audit): the principle "avoid updating strongly coupled tokens in one step" is DAPD's (2603.12996) and its test-based form is PUNT's (2510.21961); that co-committing dependent tokens costs quality is credited to those sources and to the framework-v2 identity, and is not claimed as new. The card measures the model's own per-step text-image dependence in a model that generates both, and tests whether it predicts the cost of joint commitment in this setting. The second half of the sentence above is the framework's prediction under test, not a new sampler.

### 1.2 Mechanism
A joint pass commits a text set a and an image set b from a product of predicted marginals. Its factorisation error contains a cross-modal part (framework-v2 C: TC(a,b) = TC(a) + S_{T->I} + TC(b|a), S_{T->I} = sum_{j in b} I(a; b_j | s); symmetric S_{I->T}). A text-first pure window commits a first and b in later passes, so b sees a; it removes the T->I part only, because a still does not see the window's image tokens, exactly as in a joint pass (X1-m1). The toy [V] (primer section 4) shows the trade-off: at equal tokens per pass, separating the modalities removes the cross term and pays within-modality total correlation, and can lose to an interleaved schedule. Here separating costs a pass, so the image tokens of a pure window are committed in fewer, larger groups, and the window's 128 image tokens see the window's text 0-3 passes earlier. The card therefore distinguishes three effects of a pure window: avoided T->I co-commitment (DA - TFJ), text visible earlier (TFJ - J), and image density (Jd - J, DA - DA-low). The primary contrast J versus DA measures their net.

"Joint commitment costs where cross-modal dependence is high" is close to an identity (primer section 0 item 5). The empirical content tested here: (a) where in the trajectory and on which tokens the model's dependence sits (H-A); (b) whether any schedule exploits it at matched passes, and through which of the three effects (H-B); (c) whether the measured quantity predicts the gain from separation window by window, beyond time (H-C).

### 1.3 Closest methods and the exact distinction
- MMaDA-Parallel Table 10 (fully parallel 71.0/63.2/59.8, semi-parallel 68.3/60.7/57.5, sequential 64.2/56.5/54.1) [S, primer]. Confounds: sequential text cannot see the image; training differs; passes not matched; one run; GPT-4.1 judge; no interval; its released sampler is not fully parallel (E3). Distinction: one set of weights, bidirectional attention in every arm, every committed token visible to every later pass, matched passes and matched per-window budgets, paired seeds, intervals. The sequential comparison itself (TF, IF) is conditional stage 4b only.
- UniDisc App. I, Fig. 24-25 [S, primer]: logit distance conditional-vs-unconditional shrinks with decoding; CFG only on steps 1-3 of 24 gives CLIP 0.301 against 0.283 on steps 22-24. Guidance drops a whole modality, so it measures the value of the other modality's information per step, not the cost of co-commitment, and it changes marginals. Distinction: no guidance in the primary condition; reproduced as positive control PC-B, not used as evidence. The guidance weight is cfg x (1 - t) in the code (setup.md 2.2), so it is step-dependent by construction.
- LaViDa-O layout-first (Tables 13, 16) [S, primer]: trained planner, extra passes, image-only baseline. Distinction: no training, matched passes, joint baseline.
- Framework-v2 estimator and the EB-sampler identity (2505.24857 Eq. 7) are the accounting.
- **Estimator primitive: PUNT 2510.21961 (Sec 3.2-3.3, Alg. 1; text or protein only)**: reveal an anchor set, re-query, and take the KL between a token's conditionals with and without it; Info-Gain 2602.18176 is a further source. The primitive is theirs. Distinction here: a mixture reference that removes the marginal-inconsistency term (verified on toys, 8.7), both text-to-image and image-to-text directions in a model that generates both, a stratified decomposition that routes length or termination information away from content, and validity gates (floor per probe, injection, bias) [E1 for the prior art as summarised in the novelty audit].
- **Closest per-step dependence measurements in text-output models: VRCD 2605.25820** (Sec 3.2 Eq 2, Sec 4.3 Fig 3a: a per-step overlap of token-to-image attention among co-committed text tokens) and **VIG-Sampler 2608.26580** (Sec 2.2 Eq 5: attention-mass bins and cumulative text information gain). Neither measures text-image conditional dependence in a model that emits both, and neither reports an early-concentration finding (per the step-profile dossier, a full-text read by another agent, not re-read here). Their profiles, like ours, are conditioned on the sampler's own trajectory, which is why condition R is a registered arm of H-A.
- **Schedule: DAPD 2603.12996 (ICML 2026)** states the principle (select a set with no strong dependence edges); DEMASK 2604.02560, DUS, Halton 2503.17076, LaViDa-O's stratified sampler and the text-output multimodal samplers VRCD and VIG-Sampler are the same family (novelty audit row C2: incremental). The DA arm is a test of the framework's prediction at matched passes, with the profile built from our own measurement; it is **not** claimed as a new sampler, and "first dependence-aware sampler" must not appear in Paper 1.
- **Novelty status (X1-m4)**: the `novelty-auditor` verdict exists (research/review/audits/2026-10-14-novelty-exp001.md, E1 level, weak search surface: C1 novel as a measurement with confidence 0.60, C2 incremental, estimator credited to PUNT). ### 1.4 Competing explanations (each has a control, section 9)
1. Caption debris, watermark or style matching read as consistency (X1-F1). Control: prefix task, PG-V, reading conditions R-b and R-c, guardrail (vii).
2. Diversity-driven lift (X1-M1). Control: dC / dB decomposition (R-a), tightened guardrails, shared prefix across arms.
3. The observed prefix, not the co-generated completion, carries the lift. Control: prefix-only lift and completion increment (7.1), reading condition R-b.
4. Text earlier within a window (X1-M7). Control: TFJ.
5. Within-modality cost: a pure text pass commits 4 text tokens where a joint pass commits 1; the image passes of a pure window carry 42-43 tokens where joint passes carry 32. Control: DA-low, TFJ, Jd, text perplexity and image-quality secondaries.
6. Off-support states (text-first, image-first). Control: window arms keep the per-modality masked fractions equal at every window boundary; TF and IF (conditional) are not read as support for P5b.
7. Estimator artefacts: marginal inconsistency (0.081 nats per token at zero dependence in block J), finite-R bias (cap ln R), chunked-draw bias, bf16 quantisation that rises with entropy, the bound S_j <= H(marginal_j), non-semantic length dependence. Controls: mixture reference, injection tests G3 and G3b, R and chunk sweeps, G1b per probe, entropy-normalised statistics, stratified decomposition.
8. Position-rule, padding, composition and **sampler-trajectory** artefacts ("the dependence is front-loaded" versus "this sampler commits dependent tokens early"; coordinator input). Control: pad-class exclusion, per-position statistic, and condition R as a registered second arm of H-A (random-position states, same probes, side-by-side ratios and levels at equal revealed fraction).
9. Placement: any separation helps equally; time rather than dependence. Control: DA versus DA-low (a late-placement control if H-A holds), time-only comparator in H-C.

## 2. Hypotheses (falsifiable; direction and minimum effect of interest)

Notation: k labels = pass budgets, k0 = N 32, k+ = N 16, k- = N 64 (total tokens per pass about 1056/N: 33, 66, 16.5). W = 8 equal windows of the pass index. "Early" = windows 1-2, "late" = windows 7-8. Lift = the primary endpoint of 7.1 (points, 100 x cosine). Hypotheses are two-sided in their tests so a reversed result is detected. **Scope sentence for every claim (E5c): "on UniDisc 1.4B, at the resolution PG-1 selects, with the prefix rung PG-V selects, under the position rule the gates select, under within-modality confidence ranking unless condition R agrees".**

**H-A (P5a), measurement hypothesis.** Under plain sampling at temperature 1 from the post-prefix state, on UniDisc 1.4B, **decided at k0 only (k+ is descriptive)**. Receiving positions J_d at a probe = still-masked positions of the receiving modality, excluding for I->T the **pad-class** positions (mbar_j puts at least 0.5 of its mass on ids {0, 2}; state-time rule, no look-ahead). The statistic is the per-position mean of the content-class information, S-bar^d_n = (1/|J_d|) sum_{j in J_d} I_hat_j^c, where I_hat^c is the total estimate for I->T and the within-content term of 8.2 for T->I (X1-M5). Pass 1 is reported separately (its state is the post-prefix start).
- H-A1 (early concentration): for each direction d in {T->I, I->T}, ratio_d = [pooled mean of S-bar^d over trajectories and the centre probes of windows 1-2 (passes 2, 6)] / [same over windows 7-8 (passes 26, 30)] is at least 1.5 (minimum effect of interest; 1.0 is flat). Both directions must pass. Pooled mean = (sum over trajectories and probes of the per-probe sums of I_hat^c) / (sum of the |J_d|), so empty probes carry no 0/0.
- H-A1-Q (entropy-normalised, receiving side; E3): R_Q,d = [(sum over trajectories and early probes of sum_j I_hat_j^c) / (sum of sum_j H(mbar_j))] / [same at late probes], over receiving positions with H(mbar_j) >= 0.05 nats; excluded shares reported per direction and window.
- H-A1-Q2 (entropy-normalised, both sides; X1-M3): R_Q2,d as R_Q,d with denominator sum_j min(H_src, H(mbar_j)); H_src is, for T->I, w_content x H(p1_a | content class) of the committed text token a, and for I->T the sum of the one-pass marginal entropies of the committed image set (an upper bound, so min is H(mbar_j) in practice).
  **Wording rule.** A raw H-A1 lower limit >= 1.5 supports "per-position cross-modal dependence, in nats, is front-loaded under confidence ranking". "Front-loaded beyond the entropy available" needs the R_Q,d **and** R_Q2,d lower limits >= 1.0 in both directions. Otherwise Paper 1 says the trace follows the entropy profile. Position-free wording ("the model's dependence", without "under confidence ranking") needs the condition-R trace to agree: same side of 1.0 for each ratio and a lower 90 percent limit > 1.0 (64 trajectories); otherwise the confidence-ranking wording stays (X1-M2). **Two-way reading of the pair of traces (sampler versus model):** both ratios >= 1.5 (random-position lower 90 percent limit > 1.0): "the model's per-position cross-modal dependence is front-loaded, independent of the position rule tested"; confidence ratio >= 1.5 and random-position ratio with an upper 90 percent limit < 1.25: "front-loaded under this sampler only; the profile follows which positions the confidence rule leaves masked"; random-position ratio above the confidence ratio: reported as such; neither distinguishable: "not distinguishable at 64 trajectories". The levels S-bar(confidence) / S-bar(random) at equal pass index (equal revealed fraction) are reported at every probe.
- H-A2 (object words): in the I->T direction, on all still-masked text positions of the **completion region** at the early centre probes (windows 1 and 2), the pooled ratio of means [sum of Q over object-noun positions / their count] / [sum of Q over function-word positions / their count] is at least 1.5; Q is the information fraction of 8.5. Bootstrap over trajectories. The share of positions excluded by H(mbar) < 0.05 nats is reported by type.
- Operationalisation of "layout and object tokens": objects = word-initial subtokens whose word is tagged NOUN or PROPN by the pinned tagger on the final caption (prefix + completion; only completion-region positions are typed); function words = DET, ADP, AUX, PRON, CCONJ, SCONJ, PART, PUNCT; "layout" = a fixed list of spatial-relation words (left, right, above, below, behind, in front of, next to, top, bottom, center, middle, background, foreground; frozen in the repository before the pilot), exploratory because of few occurrences. Positions after the first `</s>` or equal to pad (id 0) are excluded from types. Image-side "salient regions" are dropped from the confirmatory claim; an exploratory map is allowed (8.6).
- Companion, descriptive (the co-commitment price): the **committed-set** quantities S^{T->I} (sum of the T->I total over the 32 committed image positions) and S^{I->T} (over the committed text position), with pad-class recipients excluded and the excluded share per probe reported at every probe (statistician e4, X1-M2). They feed DA and H-C, not H-A1.

**H-B (P5b), schedule hypothesis.** At matched forward passes N and matched per-window per-modality token budgets (5), on the condition the gates of section 6 select:
- H-B1 (primary): Lift(DA) - Lift(J) > 0 at k0, reported "at or above the MEI" if the estimate is >= 1.0 point. DA separates the four windows with the highest profiled T->I price (D1); J is joint in every window.
- H-B1' (placement): Lift(DA) - Lift(DA-low) > 0 at k0.
- H-B4 (mechanism): Lift(DA) - Lift(TFJ) > 0 at k0 (separation beyond text-earlier).
- H-B3 (dose, estimation only): DA - J does not increase as k decreases (k+ >= k0 >= k-). Not tested.
- Exploratory, in no family: Alt - J at k+, TFJ - J (text-earlier alone), Jd and J-global contrasts, and (conditional stage 4b) H-B2: TF - Alt and IF - Alt one-sided upper limits below 1.0 (unadjusted intersection-union form, E6).
- **Reading conditions (10.2, 7.4)** applied to a positive H-B1 before it is worded as "improves text-image consistency": R-a matched-driven (dC lower 95 percent limit > 0), R-b completion-driven (completion-increment contrast lower 95 percent limit > 0), R-c VLM-concordant (V-lift contrast lower 90 percent limit > 0), all guardrails of 7.3 passed. Failing R-a: "baseline-driven". Failing R-b: "changes how well the image follows the observed prefix". Failing R-c: "changes CLIP lift only". Each is a restriction on wording and adds no alpha.
The minimum effect of interest of 1.0 point equals the scale of the differences UniDisc's authors report between CFG-window conditions (0.301 vs 0.293 vs 0.283 on a 0-1 cosine scale, i.e. 0.8 to 1.8 points) [S, primer C3]; it is a documented scale, not a tuned one. It exists on the CLIP scale only, which is why CLIP lift stays the primary.

**H-C (validity).** Let Delta_{w,k} = Lift(Sep_w) - Lift(J) at budget k, Sep_w separating only window w. Let M_{w,k} = P_w x S^{T->I}(centre of window w) measured on the **profile set D1** (committed-set T->I total, X1-m1), P_w = passes in the window. Then rho_strat = mean over k in {k0, k+} of the within-k Spearman correlation of (M_{w,k}, Delta_{w,k}) over w = 1..8 is at least 0.5 (one-sided permutation p <= 0.05, permuting windows within each k). **Beyond time (X1-M6):** the time-only comparator is E_{w,k} = P_w x (D1 per-window mean marginal entropy of the still-masked image positions); H-C is worded "S_theta predicts where separation helps" only if rho_strat(M, Delta) - rho_strat(E, Delta) has a paired-bootstrap lower 90 percent limit > 0 as well; otherwise "consistent with S_theta, not distinguishable from time", and S_theta is a descriptive trace. H-C is conditional on H-B showing a nonzero Delta somewhere and on a variance check (stop rule 4).

## 4. Task, data, seeds, gates

### 4.1 The task and why (X1-F1)
**G0' (primary): prefix-conditioned joint completion.** Start: the official layout with a COCO-derived prefix (4.2) in the prompt slot, all 32 tail text positions and all L_I image positions masked. Output: one caption (prefix + generated tokens before the first `</s>` or pad, special tokens stripped, detokenised with the pinned Llama-2 tokenizer) and one image (VQ-16 decode). Only here are both modalities committed under a free schedule. T2I with a clean caption has no text set to co-commit; likewise I2T.

Why a prefix, and why this one. [S/E4] The only free tail observed so far, after a complete caption, read as debris (3 item 1c), and nothing about an empty user turn pushes the tail toward a description. CLIP trained on web alt-text links "copyright", dates and watermark strings to images with overlaid text and stock-photo style, so lift on debris would measure style matching (red-team X1-F1). The jointly generated text has to be about the image's content while remaining co-generated with it. A prefix that stops **mid-phrase, just before the first object noun after the subject** ("A man riding a", next word: surfboard) does three things: it makes the continuation grammatically a noun phrase, so the tail's first content token is an object slot that the image and the text must settle together (the position where cross-modal dependence is expected to be largest, H-A2); it puts the model in caption mode with real COCO style; and it is identical in every arm for a given seed, so prefix-driven topic diversity cancels in contrasts (X1-M1). A complete caption or a category phrase fills the noun slot and leaves only details to co-generate, and the smoke sample shows debris after a complete caption. The cost: the claim narrows to prefix-conditioned completion, the dependence measured is conditional on the prefix, and the observed prefix itself carries T->I information to the image that no schedule changes (hence R-b and prefix-only lift, 7.1). Whether the tail is a continuation or a new segment is not known (3 item 1c); PG-V decides.

### 4.2 The prefix rule (a pure function of the COCO caption text; frozen in the repository before PG-V)
Tag the lower-cased COCO caption with the pinned tagger. Let t1 be the first NOUN or PROPN token and t2 the first NOUN or PROPN token after t1 (**rung 1**), t3 the first after t2 (**rung 2**). The prefix is the words before t2 (rung 1) or before t3 (rung 2). An item is eligible if the rung's prefix has 3 to 6 words (rung 1) or 5 to 9 words (rung 2) and the target noun exists. Items are never selected or dropped by any model output. The prefix text is detokenised to Llama tokens with a leading space convention fixed in the pinned code. Example (rung 1): "A man riding a surfboard on a wave" gives "A man riding a".

### 4.3 Pre-pilot gate PG-1 (resolution, both legs; X1-M8, e2)
- **Guided leg (feasibility of the checkpoint at 512 px).** Official sampler (50 steps, CFG 3.0), T2I with the **clean full captions** of 32 COCO items disjoint from every pool, generated at 512 px and at 1,024 px from the same captions (paired). Pass if the mean paired difference in raw matched CLIP score (100 x cosine, 512 minus 1,024) is >= -2.0 points and the 512-px matched-minus-shuffled lift >= 0.8 x the 1,024-px lift. Cost about 0.7 compute-hours (1,024 px guided pass 1.25 s x 50 x 32 plus 512 px).
- **Condition-P leg (the condition the experiment runs).** J at k0, unguided, deterministic confidence ranking, rung-1 prefixes of the first 16 gate items, at 512 px and at 1,024 px on the same prefixes (about 6 compute-minutes). Degeneracy screen, registered now (feasibility thresholds, not hypothesis tests): (i) median distinct-VQ-code share of the 512-px images >= 0.5 x the median share of 32 real COCO images encoded at 512 px; (ii) mean CLIP-IQA at 512 px >= the 1,024-px mean minus 0.5 x the SD of CLIP-IQA across the 1,024-px images; (iii) the engineer's visual log (a 4 x 4 grid) shows at most 2 of 16 near-uniform images.
- **Decision table.** Both legs pass at 512 px: resolution 512, position rule P. Guided leg passes, condition-P leg fails at 512: repeat the condition-P leg with P_rand (random positions within modality, values still sampled at temperature 1); if it passes, resolution 512 with **P_rand as the primary position rule** (the R12 fork, same target, E5); if it fails, repeat both legs at 256 px against the same 1,024-px values; if 256 px also fails the experiment stops for the orchestrator (the 1,024-px tier is withdrawn, change log E7). Statistician OC for the paired guided leg: pass probability 0.99 when 512 px is truly as good, 0.87 at the stated deficit [C, round 2, assumptions there].
- Tier cost at 256 px: c_I = 32, t_b about 0.04 s [A, cost model 0.12 s per 1,000 tokens], core path about 3.3 compute-hours.

### 4.4 Pre-pilot construct gate PG-V (caption validity; X1-F1)
Run on pool PG [90000, 90048) with J at k0 at the selected resolution and position rule, rung-1 prefixes, **before D0 is generated**. J only: no arm contrast exists, so no firewall issue.
- **Statistic.** For each sample, N_i = the completion nouns: word-initial subtokens of the generated region whose word is NOUN or PROPN on the final caption, not in the prefix, not in the frozen non-depictive stoplist (`analysis/EXP-001/nonvisual_nouns.txt`; initial content: photo, photograph, image, picture, pic, view, copyright, stock, illustration, background, foreground, front, side, top, bottom, left, right, middle, center, edge, part, kind, type, lot, way, time, day, year). presence_i(image) = 1 if the pinned VLM answers "Is there a {noun} in this image?" with P(yes) >= 0.5 for at least one noun in N_i. Matched rate m = mean_i presence_i(image_i). Shuffled rate s = the mean over 4 fixed derangements of presence_i(image_pi(i)).
- **Gate passes iff** (a) the share of samples with N_i non-empty is >= 0.60, (b) m >= 0.50, (c) m - s >= 0.25. Operating characteristic [A, n = 48, SE of m about 0.07, of m - s about 0.09]: at true m = 0.65 and m - s = 0.40 the gate passes with probability about 0.9; at m - s = 0.25 about 0.5; at 0.10 about 0.05. The thresholds echo the red-team's "fewer than about half of the captions name an object found in the image" and are feasibility choices.
- **Checker precondition (PC-F).** On 48 real COCO pairs (Ref-real items, real captions' nouns) the same checker must give m_real >= 0.70 and m_real - s_real >= 0.40; otherwise the checker, not the model, is invalid, and another candidate VLM is pinned (pre-freeze amendment) and PC-F rerun.
- **Ladder.** Fail at rung 1: repeat PG-V at rung 2 on pool PG2 [90100, 90148) (a longer prefix, 5-9 words, more caption context). Pass at rung 2: rung 2 is the primary prefix for all pools; claims are worded at that rung. Fail at rung 2: **H-B and H-A2 are declared untestable on this checkpoint under prefix-conditioned completion; no D0 or later stage runs; the report is a feasibility result for the orchestrator** (stop rule 11.1).
- **Reported alongside (descriptive).** Pad share of the tail and completion length; CLIP lift and presence rates of the **debris reference D-ref**: J under the v0.2 task (empty prompt, same pool PG seeds), which documents what the old endpoint would have scored.
- The gate is a feasibility check on J. It does not replace the reading conditions on contrasts.

### 4.5 Unit, references, seeds
- **Unit of analysis**: the seed index i; seed i carries one COCO prefix. All arms use the same i and the same prefix.
- **References on a public prompt set (not in any hypothesis test).** COCO val2017: Ref-real (500 real pairs through every metric), Ref-T2I (the **clean full caption** of the prefix item, tail left masked, image decoded in the 24-image-pass structure of Alt at k0; n = 250), same value rule and metrics. Ref-I2T is dropped (descriptive, unused). COCO is used for evaluation only and is not redistributed.
- **Not covered**: multi-segment interleaved generation, text lengths other than 32 tail tokens, resolutions other than the one PG-1 selects, unconditional (empty-prompt) generation except as D-ref.
- **Seed pools (disjoint; counter-based generator keyed by pool and i; each pool has its own block of COCO items)**: PG [90000, 90048) and PG2 [90100, 90148); D0 pilot [0, 64); D1 profile [10000, 10128), the first 64 reused for k+; **D2 H-A confirmation [20000, 20256)** at k0; D3 arms [30000, 31000), the first n_conf used; D4 secondary conditions [40000, 40250); D5 blind-model null [50000, 50128).
- **Common random numbers**: sample i draws a float64 uniform u_j for every position j once; a position's value is the inverse-CDF draw from its float64 marginal at the pass in which it is committed, in every arm. Probes use a separate stream and never touch the sampler stream.

## 5. Arms and schedules

### 5.1 Geometry and the plan generator
L_gen = L_T + L_I (1,056 at 512 px; the prefix is observed and not counted). N passes; W = 8 windows; c_T = L_T / W = 4 text and c_I = L_I / W = 128 image tokens per window (at 512 px); P_w = N / W passes per window. Requirements: W divides N and P_w >= 2. Grid: **k0: N = 32 (P_w = 4), k+: N = 16 (P_w = 2), k-: N = 64 (P_w = 8)**. At 256 px the same N grid is used with c_I = 32.

Within a modality the committed positions are the top-count masked positions by maximum marginal probability (ties: lowest position index); condition R and the P_rand fallback use uniformly random positions; values are drawn from the unmodified marginals (section 6). Every arm uses the same weights, attention (full bidirectional), position embeddings, and every committed token is visible to every later pass.

Window rules (pass q = 1..P_w inside window w; floor rule f(q, c, m) = floor(q c / m) - floor((q-1) c / m)):
- **Joint window**: text count f(q, c_T, P_w), image count f(q, c_I, P_w) (at k0: 1 text and 32 image per pass). Per-window totals exactly (c_T, c_I).
- **Pure window**: pass 1 of the window is a text-only pass committing all c_T text tokens; passes 2..P_w are image-only with counts f(q-1, c_I, P_w - 1) (at k0: 42, 43, 43 image tokens; at k+: one pass of 128; at k-: seven passes of about 18). Per-window totals exactly (c_T, c_I). Text goes first inside a pure window (image-first is exploratory).
- **Text-front joint window (TFJ)**: pass 1 commits all c_T text tokens **and** f(1, c_I, P_w) image tokens (at k0: 4 text and 32 image); passes 2..P_w are image-only with f(q, c_I, P_w) image tokens (at k0: 32 each). Per-window totals exactly (c_T, c_I); same image density as a joint window; the window's text is visible to passes 2..P_w exactly as in a pure window.
A schedule is a set M of non-joint windows of one kind.

| Arm | Definition | Role |
|---|---|---|
| J | M = {} | primary comparator |
| Alt | M = {1..8} pure (8 text-only passes, one per window) | modality-pure everywhere |
| DA | pure windows M = the 4 windows with the largest profiled **T->I committed-set price** on D1, fixed before any D3 run | dependence-aware schedule, zero extra passes at test time |
| DA-low | pure windows M = the 4 windows with the smallest profiled price | low-profile placement control (late-placement if H-A holds); NC-7 |
| TFJ | **TFJ windows** on the DA window set, the other windows joint | separates text-earlier from avoided co-commitment (X1-M7); pass-matched |
| Sep_w, w = 1..8 | M = {w} pure | H-C window arms (conditional stage 4) |
| TF | passes 1..8 text-only (4 tokens each), then N - 8 image-only passes (floor rule) | modality-sequential; off-support; conditional stage 4b |
| IF | N - 8 image-only passes, then 8 text-only passes | modality-sequential; off-support; conditional stage 4b |
| J-global | no plan: pooled top-count by max probability over both modalities, total tokens per pass f(q, L_gen, N) | descriptive; conditional stage 4b |
| Jd | diagnostic, not pass-matched: Alt's image pass structure (N - 8 image passes) with the 32 text tokens co-committed by the floor rule over those passes | isolates image density from separation; exploratory (E7a) |
| O | the official sampler with its defaults (maskgit, 50 steps, CFG 3.0, r_temp 4.5, final noise removal) | reference only, condition G |

The "dependence-aware" claim is tested at phase level (which windows to separate), not pair by pair; an instance-level controller costs extra passes and is future work. The window set for DA comes from the D1 profile only and is saved to `runs/EXP-001/da_windows.json` with a hash before D3 starts. The same window set (in normalised time) is used at k+ and k-. Contrasts involving Jd, J-global, TFJ - J, TF and IF are exploratory (E7a).

### 5.2 Matching rules and why
- Matched forward passes N in every arm except Jd (reported with its N - 8) and O (50 steps, guided passes count 2). TFJ, DA, DA-low, Alt, Sep_w are pass-matched and per-window budget-matched.
- Matched per-window per-modality commit budgets (window arms): masked fractions per modality agree at every window boundary, so states stay near the training support.
- **Tokens per pass are not matched** in modality-pure windows (text pass 4 tokens, image passes 42-43 against 1 and 32 in joint passes); this is why DA-low, Jd and TFJ exist. TFJ keeps image density at 32 and puts 4 text tokens in one pass.
- No time input exists (3 item 2), so no time-matching rule is needed.
- The MMaDA-Parallel lesson: no arm uses a differently trained model or an attention mask that blocks anything; "text first" means the window's image positions stay masked while its text is committed.

## 6. Value rule (fixed across arms) and the gates that select the condition

- **Primary condition P**: plain sampling at temperature 1 from the unmodified marginals, no top-p, no top-k, no guidance, position rule = within-modality confidence ranking (a deterministic function of the state). With a state-only position rule and values drawn from the product of marginals, the value slot V of framework-v2 B is 0 and G = 0, so arm differences are differences in schedule, not value rule.
- **Float precision and renormalisation**: model log-probabilities (bf16) are exponentiated and renormalised inside the modality slice in float64; all categorical draws and every KL use float64; inverse-CDF with float64 cumulative sums.
- **Secondary condition G (official guided setting)**: J, Alt, DA with their plans, values sampled at temperature 1 from guided marginals (CFG 3.0, weight cfg x (1 - t), a guided pass counts 2), through `ordered_commit(guided=True)`; plus O. Labelled mixed effects (guidance changes cross-modal conditioning). Conditional stage 6.
- **Robustness condition R**: condition P with uniformly random positions within modality. Trace on 64 trajectories (core; the agreement check of 2 needs it) and arms J, Alt, DA at n = 250 (conditional stage 7). **If P_rand is the primary condition, condition R is condition P with the same arms, n and trace (E5b).**
- **Dynamic-range gate and the one pre-registered fork (R12, E5)**: Gate statistic = the point estimate of Lift(J) on D0 at k0 with the full pipeline (full caption). If it is below 2.0 points, **J and Alt at k0 are regenerated on D0 under P_rand** (E5a), the statistic is recomputed for J, and if P_rand passes the primary estimand becomes "DA - J at k0 under P_rand" and D1, D2, D3 run under P_rand with the sizing SE (10.4) taken from Alt - J under P_rand; if both fail, H-B is declared untestable on this checkpoint and only H-A (descriptive) proceeds under P. The fork has two triggers with one target: this gate and the condition-P leg of PG-1 (4.3). The three gate outcomes (PG-1, PG-V, dynamic range) are logged together before D1 starts. The primary estimand is always "DA - J at k0 under the resolution, prefix rung and position rule the gates select". Operating characteristic [A]: SE of the gate statistic is about 0.5-0.7 points at n = 64, so a true Lift(J) of 2.5 fails about 20 percent of the time; a false fail changes the condition, it does not end the experiment. The prefix raises Lift(J) for every arm equally; PG-V (c) is the check that the completion adds to it.

## 7. Endpoints

### 7.1 Primary (one): matched-minus-shuffled CLIP lift of the full caption
Model: CLIP ViT-L/14 (candidate `openai/clip-vit-large-patch14` [E0]; revision hash and file SHA recorded before PG-V), text truncated at 77 tokens (prefix + at most 32 tokens is at most about 45 Llama tokens, so truncation is expected to be rare and its rate is reported), the checkpoint's own image preprocessing. For a cell (arm, k) with n samples, e_I(x_i), e_T(c_i) are L2-normalised embeddings, c_i the full caption (prefix + generated),
C_i = cos(e_I(x_i), e_T(c_i)), B_i = 0.5 x [mean_{j != i} cos(e_I(x_j), e_T(c_i)) + mean_{j != i} cos(e_I(x_i), e_T(c_j))], **Lift_i = 100 (C_i - B_i)**.
The endpoint of an arm is mean_i Lift_i; a contrast is the paired mean difference over seeds. The shuffled baseline is computed inside the arm's own samples. No sample is excluded; empty or degenerate completions are scored as they are (the prefix remains) and counted in the guardrails. The per-seed influence value of the mean Lift is psi_i = 100 (C_i - 2 B_i) (Hajek projection; statistician S1); sizing uses it through the bootstrap SE (10.4).
**Why CLIP stays primary.** The documented minimum effect of interest exists on this scale only; the n x n similarity matrix makes the baseline and every bootstrap replicate cheap; and the behaviour of a VLM scorer on a 1.4B model's images is unmeasured until PC-F and PG-V. The construct concern is handled by the task (4), the gates, and reading condition R-c, not by switching the scale.
**Derived quantities (descriptive, same bootstrap):** the matched term C and baseline term B separately (X1-M1); **prefix-only lift**, the same lift with the text replaced by the prefix alone; **completion increment** = Lift(full) - Lift(prefix-only) per seed.

### 7.2 Companion: V-lift (required by reading condition R-c)
The pinned VLM scores s(x, c) = P("Yes") for the question 'Is this figure showing "{caption}"? Please answer yes or no.' (template to be confirmed at pin time). V_i = s(x_i, c_i) - 0.5 [s(x_i, c_{pi1(i)}) + s(x_{pi2(i)}, c_i)] with two fixed derangements, run on **every** scored sample of the D3 arms (three forwards per sample; no 250-subset any more). Contrasts are paired means over seeds with the same bootstrap. No minimum effect of interest exists on this scale: the card registers only the sign and interval (R-c) and reports the achieved minimum detectable difference from the pilot SD of V differences. The pilot may write that SD (an SD of influence values, permitted by R1).

### 7.3 Secondary (descriptive, intervals, uncorrected, flagged) and guardrails
- Raw matched score 100 C_i; the primary contrast also expressed as a share of the gap Lift(Ref-real) - Lift(J) (O2).
- Object presence (4.4 statistic) on the five k0 arms; completion nouns per caption.
- Image quality: KID (Inception features, subsets) against 5,000 COCO val2017 images; FID not used (n = 500 is below the feature dimension). CLIP-IQA per image.
- Text quality: GPT-2 large perplexity of caption content tokens (rev 32b71b12); non-special completion length; distinct-1 and unigram entropy across a cell's captions; pad share of the tail.
- Diversity: mean pairwise cosine of image embeddings and, separately, of caption embeddings in a cell.
- Expected regressions (stated now): text perplexity higher in Alt, DA, DA-low, TFJ (4 text tokens per text pass against 1 in joint passes); KID higher possibly in Alt (denser image passes).
- **Guardrails for a positive H-B1 (point estimates, O3, O4)**: (i) DA's mean completion length within [0.8, 1.25] of J's; (ii) mean pairwise **image**-embedding cosine within **0.01** of J's and (ii-b) mean pairwise **caption**-embedding cosine within 0.01 (tightened from 0.05 because, in the red-team's synthetic model, 0.012 of cosine moved the lift by 0.9 points, about 60-75 lift points per unit cosine; the real CLIP slope is unknown [A] and the R-a decomposition is the actual protection); (iii) empty-completion rate not higher by more than 2 percentage points; (iv) rate of captions truncated at 77 CLIP tokens within 5 percentage points of J's; (v) mean CLIP-IQA not lower than J's by more than 0.2 x the SD of per-image CLIP-IQA in J; (vi) KID not higher than J's by more than 1 SD of the KID subset estimates of J; (vii) the object-presence rate not lower than J's by more than 3 percentage points. A positive with a failed guardrail is reported as "positive with regression" and is not used as support without that qualifier.

### 7.4 The reading of a positive H-B1 (three registered conditions)
R-a (matched-driven): the paired 95 percent interval of dC = mean(C_DA - C_J) has a lower limit > 0 (the Lift contrast is 100 (dC - dB)); if not, the positive is labelled "baseline-driven" and is not support. R-b (completion-driven): the paired 95 percent interval of the completion-increment contrast has a lower limit > 0; if not, "changes how well the image follows the observed prefix". R-c (VLM-concordant): the paired 90 percent interval of the V-lift contrast has a lower limit > 0 (equivalent to a one-sided 5 percent test, an intersection-union with H-B1); if not, "changes CLIP lift only". Only a positive that passes R-a, R-b, R-c and the guardrails is worded "improves text-image consistency of the co-generated text". The wording ladder is in 13.

### 7.5 Measurement endpoint for H-A
The per-probe traces of section 8, both directions: per-position means, committed-set sums, within-content, between-class and within-pad terms, the information fractions Q, and the floors.

## 8. Estimator protocol for S_theta

### 8.1 Definition
At a probed pass n of a J-arm trajectory with state s (prefix and everything committed so far), the committed sets are the plan's sets: text set A^T (1 token at k0, 2 at k+) and image set A^I (32 at k0, 64 at k+), chosen by the position rule as a function of s alone. For every still-masked image position j (set J_I), the model's dependence is I_theta(a; b_j | s) = E_a KL(p(b_j | s, a) || mbar_j), mbar_j = E_a p(b_j | s, a), with a drawn from the model's own joint over A^T. This is the named split "text-first S_theta" (framework-v2 C). The **committed-set price** S^{T->I}_n = sum_{j in A^I} I_theta(a; b_j | s) is the quantity a text-first pure window removes; the **per-position statistic** S-bar of 2 averages over the receiving positions. S^{I->T} is symmetric (b drawn jointly, text marginals requeried). S_theta is the model's own price of co-commitment; it is not the symmetric cross term X and not a statement about the data law (red-team M10).

### 8.2 Procedure per probed pass, per direction
1. Baseline pass at s (the sampler's own pass; shared). Record the one-pass marginals p1_j.
2. **T->I, stratified (X1-M5).** The committed text token a at k0 is one token with marginal p1_a. Pad class P = ids {0, 2}; w = p1_a(P) in float64. Draw R = 8 values: 4 exactly from the conditional of a given P (pad or EOS) and 4 from the conditional given not-P (content); if one class has mass below 0.01 all 8 come from the other class and that class's weight is carried as its true tiny value. One requery pass at s plus a^(r) stores the image marginals m_r(j, .) for all j in J_I (float64, renormalised). Passes added: k_T x R = 8 at k0. At k+ (2 tokens) the draw is sequential and iid, total only (the decomposition is not applied; k+ is descriptive).
3. I->T: for r = 1..R, draw the image set b in confidence order in **g = 4 chunks** (8 tokens each at k0): within a chunk from the product of marginals, between chunks the marginals are requeried with the earlier chunks revealed (3 intermediate passes), then one requery pass gives the text marginals for all still-masked text positions. Passes added per r: g = 4 (32 per probe). Approximate joint draw; sensitivity is gate G5.
4. Class means and the decomposition (T->I, k0): mbar_c(j) = mean of m_r(j) over the draws in class c; mbar_j = w mbar_P(j) + (1-w) mbar_C(j). With KL_r(j) = KL(m_r(j) || mbar_{class(r)}(j)): within-pad_j = w x mean_{r in P} KL_r(j); within-content_j = (1-w) x mean_{r in C} KL_r(j); between_j = w KL(mbar_P(j) || mbar_j) + (1-w) KL(mbar_C(j) || mbar_j); total I_hat_j = between_j + within-pad_j + within-content_j (an exact identity at any R). The pad/EOS indicator carries length or termination information (between), the pad-versus-EOS identity is non-semantic too (within-pad), and **the content term within-content_j is the T->I quantity H-A1 uses**. For the unstratified totals (I->T, k+) I_hat_j = (1/R) sum_r KL(m_r(j) || mbar_j), mbar_j the mean (plug-in mixture estimator; bounded above by ln R per position).
5. Naive reference for the inconsistency check: N_j = (1/R) sum_r KL(m_r(j) || p1_j) = I_hat_j + KL(mbar_j || p1_j) (exact identity); I_inc,j = KL(mbar_j || p1_j) is reported at every probe next to S_theta.
6. Committed-set sums S^{T->I}_n = sum_{j in A^I} I_hat_j (total), its content term, and S^{I->T}_n = sum_{j in A^T} I_hat_j with pad-class recipients (mbar mass on P >= 0.5) excluded and the excluded share reported. Per-position statistics of 2 over J_d.
Probe isolation: probes run on copies of the state and never change the sampler's RNG state or the trajectory. A saturation diagnostic (share of positions with I_hat_j > 0.5 ln R) is reported. The class-conditional plug-in bound is ln 4 per class term at R = 8 (conservative for front-loading: the bias is negative and larger where dependence is larger, toy 35 percent low at R = 4, 1.4 percent at R = 64 [V]).

### 8.3 What is probed and how many trajectories
- R = 8 for D1 and D2. All R draws are stored: two disjoint halves (R = 4) give the Monte Carlo SD; the extrapolation 2 I(8) - I(4) is a sensitivity analysis only.
- **D1 (profile; DA windows, H-C predictor, E4 power projection)**: 128 trajectories at k0 with **T->I** probes at the centre pass of each window: centre(w) = (w - 1) P_w + 2, i.e. passes 2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30 of 32; **I->T** probes at passes 2, 6, 26, 30 on the first 64 of these trajectories (for the E4 projection and the H-A2 projection only); 64 trajectories at k+ with T->I probes at the first pass of each 2-pass window (centre(w) = 2(w - 1) + 1, passes 1, 3, ..., 15).
- **D2 (H-A confirmation)**: 256 trajectories at k0 with both directions at passes 2, 6, 26, 30, and pass 1 on the first 128. No extension (R7). G1b is re-measured on the first 32 trajectories (8.7).
- Condition R trace: 64 trajectories at k0, probes at passes 2, 6, 26, 30 and pass 1 on 32.
- Probe passes at k0: T->I R k_T = 8, I->T R g = 32, both = 40 per probed pass; k+ T->I 16.

### 8.4 Cost as a formula
Extra forward passes per probed pass = R k_T (T->I) + R g (I->T). Per trajectory: passes_traj = N + sum over probes of (R k_T [+ R g]) x f_ord, f_ord in {1, 2} (2 only if G5 requires both orders). Defaults: k0 D1: 32 + 8 x 8 = 96 (+ 4 x 32 = 128 on 64 trajectories); k0 D2: 32 + 4 x 40 = 192 (+ 40 for the pass-1 probe on 128); k+ D1: 16 + 8 x 16 = 144. GPU time = passes x t_b (t_f for fp32 probe passes if G1b requires, after measuring t_f).

### 8.5 Information fraction, normalisation and the exclusion rules
Q_j = I_hat_j^c / H(mbar_j), the share of position j's mixture-marginal entropy that the revealed other modality explains (0 <= Q_j <= 1 because I <= H). Positions with H(mbar_j) < 0.05 nats are excluded from Q, R_Q, R_Q2. Q controls the bound S_j <= H_j (nouns are high-entropy slots, function words are not). R_Q2 adds the source side: I(a; b_j | s) <= min(H(a | s), H(b_j | s)); H_src as in 2. POS labels come from the final caption at word-initial subtokens, assigned after the trajectory ends; because the label is realised, a position's type is correlated with its marginal, which is why Q is the primary statistic for H-A2. Exclusions for H-A1: I->T pad-class recipients (state-time rule, 2); T->I handled by the content term; the excluded share of recipients and the share of draws in the pad class per probe are reported. The **all-masked-position quantity is reported only as a per-position mean**; a summed version is never evidence of front-loading (X1-m2, it grows with the number masked).

### 8.6 Exploratory maps (no decision rule)
S_{T->I} per image position on the 2D token grid against a foreground map of the final image (pinned segmenter, quality on these images unverified); S_{I->T} by the spatial-word list; correlation of sample-level cumulative S with Lift within the J arm.

### 8.7 Validity checks of the estimator (gates)
- **G0 unit test on the toy**: the production estimator, run on the tables of `analysis/framework/check_crossmodal_mixture.py`, reproduces: identity naive = I + KL(mbar||p1) to 1e-12; block J gives naive 0.08109, mixture 0; finite-R estimate below I_theta in 40/40 within 3 standard errors. **Added**: the stratified estimator satisfies total = between + within-pad + within-content to 1e-12 and reproduces the unstratified total in expectation on a toy with a known class structure.
- **G1 noise floor, per probe and per direction (X1-M4)**. (G1a) the same state run in different batch positions and compositions; (G1b) a **no-information reveal**: R = 8 requeries, each revealing a different position among those with max marginal >= 0.999 (post-EOS or pad tail positions; fewer than 6 at a probe: use positions with max marginal >= 0.99 of any modality and report it; still fewer: that probe is flagged "floor unmeasured" and is dropped from H-A1), and the mixture estimator applied to the R requeries (these differ only by input-dependent rounding). It is measured **at passes 2, 6, 26 and 30 (and 1) on the same 16 pilot trajectories**, the floor being the mean over the same receiving positions as the probe's statistic; the floor-versus-entropy curve (floor per position against H(mbar_j)) is reported; the floor is **re-measured on the first 32 D2 trajectories**. **Pass rule**: for each probe and each direction the probe-level mean of the statistic is at least 20 times that probe's own mean floor. "Smallest S value" in v0.2 meant nothing definable; this replaces it. Failing that, probe passes run with fp32 weights (6.3 GB, t_f to be measured) and the gate repeats; failing that too, H-A and H-C are declared not measurable.
- **G2 null by construction (NC-1)**: the modality-blind wrapper (text marginals from a pass with all image positions masked; image marginals from a pass with all text positions masked, **prefix included**) must give |S^{T->I}|, |S^{I->T}| and I_inc below the G1 floor at all probes of 16 trajectories.
- **G3 injection test (block J on the real network)**: wrapper that returns the modality-blind image marginals mixed 70/30 with uniform over the image slice whenever any text token beyond s is revealed, unmixed otherwise. True dependence is 0. Required: mixture estimator below the G1 floor; naive estimator equals I_inc within 1e-6 and is above 10 x the floor at the first probe.
- **G3b non-semantic injection (new, X1-M5)**: wrapper that returns the modality-blind image marginals mixed 70/30 with uniform when the revealed token is in the pad class P and unmixed otherwise (a length-only dependence). Required: the total T->I statistic exceeds 10 x the floor at the first probe; the between-class term carries at least 90 percent of it; the within-content term is below the floor. This shows the decomposition routes length or termination information away from the H-A1 statistic.
- **G4 bias**: on 16 trajectories, probes at windows 1, 4, 8, compare R = 8 with R = 32 (16 pad-class and 16 content-class draws); the early/late ratio of the per-position statistic computed with R = 8 must be within 25 percent of the one with R = 32, in both directions. The pilot prints only this agreement, not the ratio itself (e1).
- **G5 draw order and granularity (E2)**: on 16 trajectories, at the probes of passes 2 and 30, the I->T estimate for g = 4, g = 8 and g = 32 (exact). Statistic per probe: ratio of means over trajectories, S(g)/S(32). Registered g = 4 if both per-probe ratios lie in [0.77, 1.30] and the early/late ratio of means with g = 4 is within 25 percent of that with g = 32; else g = 8 under the same rule (cost per probe R (k_T + 8) = 72 passes, D2 +1.5 compute-hours, stated); else I->T is "chunk-dependent": H-A1 is decided on T->I alone, H-A2 is reported descriptively, and the H-A claim is worded "T->I only". The Monte Carlo SD of each estimate (from the two R = 4 halves) is reported with G5. The confidence order is compared with its reverse on 8 of these trajectories; an order difference above 0.3 makes the S_theta used afterwards the mean over both orders (f_ord = 2).
- **G6 probe invariance**: generation with probes on equals generation with probes off (identical token sequences; if kernels are nondeterministic, the rate of differing sequences must not exceed that between two probe-off runs). I2 and I3 borrow the same rate rule.
- **Precision block (X1-m3)**: on 64 states from D0 J trajectories at 512 px, bf16 weights against the checkpoint's fp32 weights with bf16 autocast under the **current shim** (file SHA-256 and version string in the manifest): top-1 agreement, mean TV, mean entropy difference, row-sum deviation. The 256-px figures of 3 item 5b are not used for any decision.

## 10. Statistics

### 10.1 Unit, design, interval, p-values
Unit = seed i. Every comparison is paired over seeds. Interval: percentile paired bootstrap over seeds, B = 10,000, recomputing the shuffled baseline inside each replicate (partners with the same original seed index excluded when a seed is drawn twice); BCa as a sensitivity. In a synthetic check the bootstrap SE matched the true sampling SD (0.307 against 0.306) [C]. Sensitivity for the primary (O8): an arm-swap randomization test (swap DA and J samples within seed, recompute both baselines, 10,000 draws); reported, not decisive.
**Two-sided p for a paired contrast (R5)**: p = 2 min{(1 + #{theta* <= 0})/(B + 1), (1 + #{theta* >= 0})/(B + 1)}, capped at 1. If any p lies within 2 sqrt(p(1-p)/B) of the Holm threshold it is compared with, that bootstrap is rerun once with B = 100,000 and a fixed seed recorded in the manifest, and the B = 100,000 value is used. For H-A and H-C see 10.3.
Common random numbers help for plans sharing a prefix of windows (window arms, TFJ and DA share their joint windows) and not for TF/IF. The sample-size rule uses a directly estimated paired SE and needs no rho term.

### 10.2 Primary contrast, multiplicity, outcome classes, error control
- **Fixed-sequence gatekeeping at FWER 0.05 for H-B (R3).** Step 1: H-B1 (DA - J at k0), two-sided 0.05. Step 2, only if step 1 rejects: Holm at 0.05 over the **four** secondary tests (1) Alt - J at k0, (2) DA - J at k+, (3) DA - DA-low at k0 (H-B1'), (4) DA - TFJ at k0 (H-B4). If step 1 does not reject, the four are reported with Holm-adjusted p-values and labelled exploratory. A test that is not run enters Holm with p = 1. DA - J at k- is part of H-B3 (estimation only). TFJ - J, Jd, J-global, TF, IF contrasts and H-B2 are exploratory, in no family (E6, E7a).
- **Outcome classes (R4)** for a contrast with estimate d and 95 percent interval (lo, hi), m = 1.0: *positive* = Holm-adjusted p <= 0.05 (for the primary: unadjusted p <= 0.05) and d > 0, labelled "at or above the MEI" if d >= m; *reversed* = the same with d < 0; *null* = (lo, hi) inside (-m, m); *inconclusive* = otherwise. Unadjusted 95 percent intervals are always reported. Operating characteristics [C] (n = 500, sigma_L = 4, rho 0):

| true d (points) | P(lo > 0) | P(lo > 0 and d_hat >= 1) | P("null") |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | 0.024 | 0.000 | 0.954 |
| 0.5 | 0.507 | 0.024 | 0.506 |
| 1.0 | 0.976 | 0.501 | 0.026 |
| 1.25 | 0.999 | 0.839 | 0.002 |
| 1.5 | 1.000 | 0.976 | 0.000 |

  "Power 0.977" is P(significant) at d = 1.0; the probability of being labelled "at or above the MEI" at d = 1.0 is 0.50 and at least 0.84 for d >= 1.25. sigma_L is replaced by the pilot value (10.4).
- **Reading conditions R-a, R-b, R-c (7.4)** are wording restrictions on a positive primary. R-a and R-b are 95 percent two-sided intervals on dC and the completion-increment contrast, R-c a 90 percent interval on the V-lift contrast; R-c is an intersection-union with the primary, so no alpha is spent. Their power is lower than the primary's by construction; the report states the achieved precision of each and, when a condition fails with an interval that includes the MEI-sized effect, calls it "not confirmed", not "absent".
- **Conjunctions are intersection-union tests** and need no multiplicity adjustment: the H-B null across k+ and k0 and contrasts.
- **Error control is per claim (R11).** H-B: FWER 0.05 as above. H-A: one conjunctive claim (H-A1 both directions and H-A2, intersection-union, one-sided 0.025 per ratio, i.e. the 95 percent two-sided lower limit). H-C: one-sided 0.05. No Bonferroni across EXP-001 and EXP-002 (EXP-002 is demoted, D0013). Paper 1 also reports the sensitivity with every confirmatory level halved (primary 0.025, Holm family 0.025, H-A 0.0125 per ratio, H-C 0.025) and which claims survive.
- Secondary metrics of 7.3 are descriptive with intervals, uncorrected, flagged.

### 10.3 H-A and H-C statistics
- H-A1: pooled ratio of means (early over late) per direction (2), percentile bootstrap over trajectories, B = 10,000; supported if the lower 95 percent limit is >= 1.5 in both directions. Weak falsification: the **97.5 percent upper limit** of any of the three ratios (two directions and H-A2) is < 1.5; this is a disjunction, so P(false weak falsification) can reach about 0.075 when all three true ratios equal 1.5, and this is stated in the report (O6). Reversed: the upper limit of an H-A1 ratio is < 1.0.
- H-A1-Q, H-A1-Q2 and H-A2: as in 2, same bootstrap. The condition-R agreement check (2) uses the same statistic on 64 trajectories.
- **No extension (R7a); power stated from D1 before D2 (E4).** The earlier figures [C, statistician, delta method] P(lower limit >= 1.5) = 0.935 at a true ratio of 1.75 hold only at cv 0.6 [A]; they are 0.74 at cv 0.8, 0.55 at 1.0 and 0.28 at 1.5, and about 0.25 with 30 percent zero values at cv 1.0 (simulated). After D1 and before D2, the cv of the window-pair averages (early, late) and their correlation are computed from D1 at k0 for each direction (I->T from the 64 trajectories with I->T probes) and for H-A2, and the projected P(lower limit >= 1.5 | ratio 1.75) per part and for the conjunction at n = 256 is logged. Paper 1 quotes the logged figure, not 0.935. The per-position statistic averages over many positions and is expected to have a smaller cv than the committed-set sum [I]; D1 decides. If the projection is below 0.5 for a part the orchestrator may approve the optional extension n_D2 <= 512 (+4.2 wall-clock hours); otherwise D2 runs at 256 and "inconclusive" is the expected label for that part.
- H-C: rho_strat = mean of the within-k Spearman correlations of (M_{w,k}, Delta_{w,k}) over w = 1..8, for k in {k0, k+}; one-sided permutation test permuting windows within each k separately (100,000 draws); supported if rho_strat >= 0.5 and p <= 0.05. One-sided 5 percent critical value of rho_strat = 0.440 [C, statistician; reproduced here by simulation of the mean of two independent exact n = 8 permutation distributions, 0.4405]. If only one stratum is run: exact one-sided 5 percent critical value 0.643 (P(rho >= 0.643) = 0.048 at n = 8, enumerated here). The pooled Spearman over 16 cells is not used (false support in 97 percent of cases by a between-k shift alone, statistician's simulation). The interval for rho_strat is a double bootstrap (seeds for Delta, trajectories of D1 for M). M comes from D1, which is independent of D3. **Time comparator** (X1-M6): the same statistic with E_{w,k} in place of M; the difference rho_strat(M) - rho_strat(E) is bootstrapped paired over the same resamples; the "beyond time" wording needs its lower 90 percent limit > 0.

### 10.4 Sample size and its stated assumptions [A until the pilot]
- Assumptions: sigma_L = 4 points; rho_pair = 0; effect of interest m = 1.0. Under the influence-based SD the required n rises by about 35 percent (Holm-level n about 680) [I, statistician power notes].
- n for power 0.90 at two-sided alpha: n = ((z_{1-a/2} + z_{0.90}) sigma_d / m)^2 with sigma_d = 5.66: 337 at alpha = 0.05 and 505 at 0.05/7 [C]. At n = 500: power 0.977 and 0.897 (sigma_L = 4), 0.885 and 0.682 (5), 0.750 and 0.478 (6).
- **Rule (R2, E5a, E7b)**: sigma_d,up = sqrt(64) x (bootstrap SE of the paired contrast Alt - J at k0 on D0, full Lift pipeline with in-replicate baseline recomputation, B = 10,000, **under the condition the gates select**) x sqrt(63 / chi2_{0.20, 63}). n_req = ceil(1.5 x ((1.96 + 1.2816) sigma_d,up / 1.0)^2). **n_conf = min(n_cap, max(500, n_req))**, where **n_cap** is the largest n in [500, 1000] whose projected core-path wall-clock (12.1 formula with the pilot's measured t_b under gpu_guard and the measured wall-to-compute ratio) is at most 26 hours. At t_b = 0.129 s and duty 0.5, n_cap is about 530; at t_b = 0.09 s it is 1,000. If n_req > n_conf, all tests still run and the achieved MDE (1.96 + 1.2816) sigma_d,up / sqrt(n_conf) is reported. (Alt - J is the conservative proxy for DA - J: it differs from J in all 8 windows, DA in 4; DA - J may be more strongly paired, so n_conf is conservative, statistician concern.) The VLM-based V-lift SD from the pilot is used only to report the achieved MDE for R-c.
- H-C: the SE of Delta_w is projected from the pilot Alt - J bootstrap SE; stop rule 4's bound of 0.15 point needs rho_pair >= 0.65 at n = 500 or >= 0.30 at n = 1000 (by the card's own formula, hand-computed). H-C is likely underpowered; "not tested at this budget" is the expected outcome unless the pilot shows strong pairing.

## 11. Stop rules and falsifiers

Staging is by information per GPU-hour (section 12). Each stage ends in a written verdict file; the next runs only if its precondition holds.

1. **Stage gates.** PG-1, PG-V, PC-F, G0 to G6, G3b and the NC/PC checks must pass at stages 0-2; a failed positive control or gate stops the later stages for the measurement it protects (8.7). **A PG-V failure at rung 2 ends the experiment as a feasibility result.**
2. **H-A.** Positive: H-A1 lower limits >= 1.5 in both directions and H-A2 lower limit >= 1.5 (with the wording rules of 2). Weak falsification as in 10.3; reversed as in 10.3. Decided once at n = 256 (or at the approved n_D2), no extension. The trace shape (monotone, mid-trajectory peak) is reported whatever the verdict.
3. **H-B, stage 3.** Run arms J, Alt, DA, DA-low, TFJ at k0 and J, DA at k+. If the contrasts Alt - J and DA - J are null (interval inside (-1, 1)) at k0 and DA - J is null at k+, H-B is reported as "null at k in {k+, k0}" (an intersection-union conclusion), the conditional stages 4, 4b and 5 do not run, and the claim is limited to those k and this value rule. If any contrast is positive, run stage 5 (k-) after the reading conditions are scored. If reversed (J better than Alt or DA with p <= 0.05 and d <= -1), run stages 4b and 4 to localise it (each needs the orchestrator's yes).
4. **H-C, stage 4.** Run the Sep arms only if (i) some stage-3 contrast is positive or reversed, and (ii) the projected SE(Delta_w) from the pilot Alt - J SE at the allowed n is at most 0.15 point (needs rho_pair >= 0.65 at n = 500 or >= 0.30 at n = 1000). Otherwise H-C is "not tested at this budget", S_theta stays descriptive; decided before any Sep run and logged.
5. **Dose.** H-B3 reported as a pattern of point estimates (k+ >= k0 >= k-); estimation only.
6. **Budget stop.** The core path (stages 0-5 of 12.1) has a cap of **about 25 wall-clock hours (26 by the n_cap rule)**. Every conditional stage needs its precondition and the orchestrator's yes; its wall-clock is stated in 12.1 and the cumulative wall-clock may not pass 40 hours without a new yes. Exceeding the core cap stops the work for the orchestrator. **A gpu_guard temperature stop (exit 90) is not a failure of a stage but is logged; three temperature stops in one stage stop the stage for the orchestrator.**
7. **What makes P5 leave the review's open problems or be reported as negative.**
   - Negative (on this checkpoint, resolution, prefix rung and value rule only): H-A weak falsification in any of its ratios, or H-B null at k+ and k0 with all gates passed. A negative H-A with a positive H-B is reported as such (separation helps although the trace is not front-loaded; the mechanism claim is dropped).
   - Drop P5 from the open problems (replace with "measured, small"): both of the above hold, G0 to G6 passed, and condition R reproduces the null for H-A.
   - Keep P5 open and say the measurement was inconclusive: any of G1 to G4 failed, the dynamic-range gate failed in both conditions, or an interval straddles its effect of interest at the fixed n.
   - Do not generalise to any other checkpoint, to guided sampling, to unconditional generation, or to the data law q in any of these cases.

### 12.2 GPU safety protocol (every GPU stage; new constraint)
- **Wrapper.** Every GPU process runs as `python tools/gpu_guard.py --log runs/EXP-001/<stage>/telemetry-<stage>-<chunk>.csv --max-temp 85 --max-minutes 15 -- python <stage script> --resume` (arguments as in tools/gpu_guard.py; defaults: three consecutive hot readings at 5-second sampling stop the job; the CSV is flushed per row and records temperature, power, clocks, utilisation, memory, pstate and throttle reasons). Exit 91 is a normal chunk boundary; exit 90 is a temperature stop.
- **Duty cycle about 50 percent.** A driver script runs a chunk, then idles with no GPU process for at least as long as the chunk ran (CPU-only analysis, tagging and bootstrap may run in the idle time), and starts the next chunk only when the GPU is below 65 C [A, the card's choice; the guard's stop is 85 C]. After an exit 90 the next chunks use `--max-minutes 10` and the idle period doubles; three exit-90 stops in one stage stop it (11.6).
- **Batch size at most 4** (effective batch at most 4 for CFG stages: batch 2 with the doubled guided pass). There is no batching speed-up, so batch 1-2 is the default.
- **Resumable with checkpoints.** Outputs are written atomically per (stage, arm, seed); a manifest lists completed units; a restart skips them. The counter-based generator makes a resumed seed identical to an uninterrupted one (verified by G6's rate rule). Each chunk gates on free system commit (3 item 5c) before it starts.
- **Telemetry is analysed at the end of each stage** (maximum and median temperature, throttle-reason share, mean SM clock) and written in the verdict file. The pilot re-measures t_b and the wall-to-compute ratio under the guard, and the n_cap rule (10.4) uses the measured values.
- No other GPU load runs during a stage. A crash or reboot is logged, the stage resumes from the manifest, and the orchestrator is told (failures ledger).
- The first chunk of each new stage is run at `--max-minutes 5` with the engineer watching the telemetry.

## 14. Risks the red-team should attack first

1. The prefix task may not make the tail a continuation (3 item 1c): the free tail may still be a new segment; PG-V is the only guard, and a second failure ends the experiment. Even when PG-V passes, a gate on 48 samples with thresholds chosen here (m >= 0.5, m - s >= 0.25) may admit mostly easy, generic nouns ("man", "table").
2. The observed prefix gives the image T->I information that no schedule changes, so cross-modal dependence between the *remaining* text and the image may be small; the all-masked per-position statistic can sit near the floor (G1b per probe decides) and H-A may be "not measurable".
3. The tail is 32 positions for a completion of about 8-10 tokens; most text positions are post-caption pad (3 item 1b [A]). Under confidence ranking the content tokens may be committed late, so the text-side committed-set statistics describe pad; the per-position statistic and the exclusion rule are the mitigations, and the composition of the still-masked set changes over time (hard positions remain), which Q and condition R only partly control.
4. The T->I statistic rests on a single committed text token per probe at k0 (entropy bound H(a)); R_Q2 normalises by it but the content term within a stratified draw uses 4 content-class draws at R = 8 (plug-in cap ln 4).
5. The I->T draw is a chunked approximation; the bias direction is unknown (G5 tests it on 16 trajectories at two probes).
6. The bf16 floor under the corrected shim is not measured yet (3 item 5d); G1b may fail at early probes where the floor is largest.
7. TFJ commits 4 text tokens with 32 image tokens at pass 1, which enlarges the within-pass text-image cross term relative to J; DA - TFJ and TFJ - J are therefore not clean single-factor contrasts, and the card says so (the image-density factor remains in DA - TFJ via 42-43 against 32 image tokens).
8. H-C has 8 cells per stratum, noisy Delta and a time comparator that may be collinear with M; it may be untestable by design.
9. The primary metric is one CLIP model whose real diversity slope is unknown; the VLM scorer has no documented minimum effect of interest and its behaviour on this checkpoint's images is unmeasured until PC-F and PG-V.
10. The VLM environment may conflict with the generation venv (transformers pin); evaluation runs separately.
11. No licence for the checkpoint limits what can be published; the release policy withholds generations.
12. Sampler-trajectory confound: the condition-R trace has 64 trajectories, enough to resolve a ratio of about 1.3 or more against 1.0 at a plausible cv (about 0.5 [A]) and not a smaller gap; "not distinguishable" is then the honest label and sampler-free wording is withheld.
13. Wall-clock is about 26 hours with no slack: a measured t_b above 0.129 s (throttling under the duty cycle) pushes the core past the cap and forces n = 500 with the stage list unchanged or a scope trim the orchestrator must approve.
