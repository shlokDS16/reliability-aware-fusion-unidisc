## 1. Claim contract

### 1.1 Claim, one sentence
In an all-discrete unified model sampled with many tokens per pass, given a short caption prefix, the model's own cross-modal dependence between the jointly generated caption remainder and the image is concentrated early in the trajectory and on object words (under within-modality confidence ranking; H-A, measured from a source that does not depend on the commit plan), and commit schedules that avoid co-committing the text-image pairs the joint schedule commits at the highest text-to-image price yield higher text-image consistency than joint commitment at the same number of forward passes (H-B). The two halves are separate claims: the DA windows come from the committed-set price measured on D1, not from the H-A trace, so neither half implies the other (R2-F1).

Credit and scope (novelty audit): the principle "avoid updating strongly coupled tokens in one step" is DAPD's (2603.12996) and its test-based form is PUNT's (2510.21961); that co-committing dependent tokens costs quality is credited to those sources and to the framework-v2 identity, and is not claimed as new. The card measures the model's own per-step text-image dependence in a model that generates both, and tests whether it predicts the cost of joint commitment in this setting. The second half of the sentence above is the framework's prediction under test, not a new sampler.

### 1.2 Mechanism
A joint pass commits a text set a and an image set b from a product of predicted marginals. Its factorisation error contains a cross-modal part (framework-v2 C: TC(a,b) = TC(a) + S_{T->I} + TC(b|a), S_{T->I} = sum_{j in b} I(a; b_j | s); symmetric S_{I->T}). A text-first pure window commits a first and b in later passes, so b sees a; it removes the T->I part only, because a still does not see the window's image tokens, exactly as in a joint pass (X1-m1). The toy [V] (primer section 4) shows the trade-off: at equal tokens per pass, separating the modalities removes the cross term and pays within-modality total correlation, and can lose to an interleaved schedule. Here separating costs a pass, so the image tokens of a pure window are committed in fewer, larger groups, and the window's 128 image tokens see the window's text 0-3 passes earlier. The card therefore distinguishes three effects of a pure window: avoided T->I co-commitment (DA - TFJ), text visible earlier (TFJ - J), and image density (Jd - J, DA - DA-low). The primary contrast J versus DA measures their net.

"Joint commitment costs where cross-modal dependence is high" is close to an identity (primer section 0 item 5). The empirical content tested here: (a) where in the trajectory and on which tokens the model's dependence sits (H-A); (b) whether any schedule exploits it at matched passes, and through which of the three effects (H-B); (c) whether the measured quantity predicts the gain from separation window by window, beyond time (H-C).

## 2. Hypotheses (falsifiable; direction and minimum effect of interest)

Notation: k labels = pass budgets, k0 = N 32, k+ = N 16, k- = N 64 (total tokens per pass about 1056/N: 33, 66, 16.5). W = 8 equal windows of the pass index. "Early" = windows 1-2, "late" = windows 7-8. Lift = the primary endpoint of 7.1 (points, 100 x cosine). Hypotheses are two-sided in their tests so a reversed result is detected. **Scope sentence for every claim (E5c, R2-m1): "on UniDisc 1.4B, at the resolution PG-1 selects, with the prefix rung PG-V selects, for the caption form the prefix rule selects (a subject-verb-article caption whose prefix ends before a second noun), under the position rule the gates select, under within-modality confidence ranking unless condition R agrees".**

**H-A (P5a), measurement hypothesis.** Under plain sampling at temperature 1 from the post-prefix state, on UniDisc 1.4B, **decided at k0 only (k+ is descriptive)**. Definitions, all fixed here (R2-F1, T1, R2-M2, r1):
- **Pad class** P = ids {0, 2} (`<unk>` used as pad, `</s>`). A still-masked text position j at a probe with state s is **state-pad** if the baseline-pass marginal p1_j (float64, renormalised inside the text slice) puts at least 0.5 of its mass on P. This is a function of the state alone (no look-ahead and no draws). Positions at or after the caption end of the final caption (4.1b) are **post-caption**; they are assigned after the trajectory ends, like POS labels, and are treated as pad-class for H-A.
- **T->I source (plan-independent).** At a probe, the source a* is the still-masked tail position with tail index at most L_c that is not state-pad and has the largest max_v p1_j(v) (ties: lowest index). L_c is the 90th percentile of the completion length (tokens before the caption end) over the PG-V J samples of the rung that passes, rounded up and bounded to [8, 16], written to `analysis/EXP-001/L_c.txt` and hashed before D0. The rule does not depend on the commit plan or the position rule and is identical under conditions P and R. If no such position exists, the probe has **no source**: it enters neither numerator nor denominator of any pooled statistic, and the share of such probes is reported per probe pass and arm. Because a* is not state-pad, its pad-class mass w = p1_{a*}(P) is below 0.5 and the content weight 1 - w exceeds 0.5 (8.2).
- **Receiving positions** J_d at a probe. T->I: all still-masked image positions (a fixed count per probe). I->T: still-masked text positions that are neither state-pad nor post-caption. The mbar-based pad-class rule of v0.3 (mbar_j puts at least 0.5 of its mass on P) is reported as a sensitivity (r1).
- The statistic is the per-position mean of the content-class information, S-bar^d_n = (1/|J_d|) sum_{j in J_d} I_hat_j^c, where I_hat^c is the total estimate for I->T and the within-content term of 8.2 for T->I from the source a* (X1-M5). Pass 1 is reported separately (its state is the post-prefix start).
- H-A1 (early concentration): for each direction d in {T->I, I->T}, ratio_d = [pooled mean of S-bar^d over trajectories and the centre probes of windows 1-2 (passes 2, 6)] / [same over windows 7-8 (passes 26, 30)] is at least 1.5 (minimum effect of interest; 1.0 is flat). Both directions must pass. Pooled mean = (sum over trajectories and probes of the per-probe sums of I_hat^c) / (sum of the |J_d|), taken over the trajectories and probes that have a source (T->I) or a non-empty J_T (I->T), so empty probes carry no 0/0 and no weight. Because |J_T| varies with the exclusions, trajectories with more non-pad masked positions weigh more: the estimand is the mean over positions (one clause in Paper 1).
- H-A1-Q (entropy-normalised, receiving side; E3): R_Q,d = [(sum over trajectories and early probes of sum_j I_hat_j^c) / (sum of sum_j H(mbar_j))] / [same at late probes], over receiving positions with H(mbar_j) >= 0.05 nats; excluded shares reported per direction and window.
- H-A1-Q2 (entropy-normalised, both sides; X1-M3): R_Q2,d as R_Q,d with denominator sum_j min(H_src, H(mbar_j)); H_src is, for T->I, (1 - w) x H(p1_{a*} | content class) of the H-A source a*, and for I->T the sum of the one-pass marginal entropies of the committed image set (an upper bound, so min is H(mbar_j) in practice). H_src is reported at every probe for both directions; for I->T also the mean marginal entropy per committed image code, because under confidence ranking those codes may be background early (red-team R2-F1, I->T analogue).
  **Wording rule.** A raw H-A1 lower limit >= 1.5 supports "per-position cross-modal dependence, in nats, is front-loaded under confidence ranking". "Front-loaded beyond the entropy available" needs the R_Q,d **and** R_Q2,d lower limits >= 1.0 in both directions. Otherwise Paper 1 says the trace follows the entropy profile. **Decision table for condition R (T5; replaces the v0.3 two-way reading).** Per direction d, with L_R and U_R the 90 percent percentile-bootstrap limits of the H-A1 ratio statistic on the n_R random-position trajectories (64, or 128 by the rule of 8.3), and applied only where H-A1 is positive under confidence ranking: (i) L_R > 1.0: "front-loaded under both position rules tested" (the >= 1.5 magnitude is claimed for confidence ranking only, unless L_R >= 1.5); (ii) L_R <= 1.0 and U_R < 1.25: "front-loading in direction d follows the confidence rule"; (iii) otherwise "not distinguishable at n_R trajectories". **Position-free wording** ("the model's dependence", without "under confidence ranking") needs (i) in both directions; otherwise the confidence-ranking wording stays (X1-M2). Agreement between two position rules shows robustness across two samplers, not sampler-freedom, and the wording stays that narrow ("independent of the position rule tested"). Always reported: the ratio of ratios (confidence over random) with an unpaired bootstrap 90 percent interval, and S-bar(confidence) / S-bar(random) at each probe (equal pass index, equal revealed fraction). **Composition-standardised decision statistic (v0.5; replaces the v0.4 "source-limited" label; red-team R3-F1 and R3-M3, statistician U1 and U2).** The masked pool shrinks over the trajectory, so the sources (T->I) and receiving text positions (I->T) differ in token type between early and late probes, not only in time; a raw ratio confounds the two. Therefore: (a) **logging**: for every T->I source and every I->T receiving text position, the position index, token id and the pinned tagger's label on the final caption are stored; (b) **strata** s in {object noun (NOUN, PROPN word-initial), other content (VERB, ADJ, ADV, NUM, other non-function), function (the function set of H-A2)}; post-caption positions are excluded; (c) **standardised ratio** ratio_std,d = [sum_s omega_s x mean early S-bar in stratum s] / [sum_s omega_s x mean late S-bar in stratum s], with fixed weights omega_s = the stratum's share of all source (T->I) or receiving (I->T) observations pooled over the four H-A centre probes; (d) **thin strata**: if any stratum with omega_s >= 0.10 has fewer than 20 observations at the early or the late probes, the direction is "inconclusive (composition)"; (e) **complete-case check (U2)**: ratio_std,d is computed twice, on all trajectories with a source at the probe (pooled) and on the trajectories with a source (T->I) or non-empty J_T (I->T) at all four centre probes (complete-case); the decision statistic is the pair, read as an intersection-union test: H-A1 is supported in direction d only if the lower 95 percent limits of both are >= 1.5; (f) **negatives (U1)**: a direction counts as Negative only if the 97.5 percent upper limits of both standardised ratios are < 1.5 **and** R_Q2,d fails by the same limits; R_Q2 never creates a positive; (g) **composition guard, reported**: h = (mean early H_src) / (mean late H_src) and the early-minus-late noun share of the sources; if h lies outside [0.85, 1.18] or the absolute noun-share difference exceeds 0.20, the raw ratio is reported as descriptive only and the wording says "composition differs between early and late"; the standardised pair decides in every case. Condition R uses the same statistic. The raw pooled ratio of the v0.4 definition is reported beside it.
- H-A2 (object words): in the I->T direction, on all still-masked text positions of the **caption region** (before the caption end, 4.1b; state-pad positions excluded) at the early centre probes (windows 1 and 2), the pooled ratio of means [sum of Q over object-noun positions / their count] / [sum of Q over function-word positions / their count] is at least 1.5; Q is the information fraction of 8.5. Bootstrap over trajectories. The share of positions excluded by H(mbar) < 0.05 nats is reported by type.
- Operationalisation of "layout and object tokens": objects = word-initial subtokens whose word is tagged NOUN or PROPN by the pinned tagger on the final caption (prefix + completion; only caption-region positions of the completion are typed); function words = DET, ADP, AUX, PRON, CCONJ, SCONJ, PART, PUNCT; "layout" = a fixed list of spatial-relation words (left, right, above, below, behind, in front of, next to, top, bottom, center, middle, background, foreground; frozen in the repository before the pilot), exploratory because of few occurrences. Positions at or after the caption end (4.1b) are excluded from types. Image-side "salient regions" are dropped from the confirmatory claim; an exploratory map is allowed (8.6).
- Companion, descriptive (the co-commitment price): the **committed-set** quantities S^{T->I} (sum of the T->I total over the 32 committed image positions, from the plan's own committed text token) and S^{I->T} (over the committed text position), with state-pad recipients excluded and the excluded share per probe reported at every probe (statistician e4, X1-M2). They feed DA and H-C, not H-A1. S^{T->I} is measured on D1 only; D2 carries the H-A source probe in its place. D1 reports both T->I quantities at the four H-A centres on the same 128 trajectories, so their relation is visible.
- Descriptive, from stored arrays (r2): for I->T, the chain-rule split of each receiving KL into the pad-versus-content indicator part and the within-class part.

**H-B (P5b), schedule hypothesis.** At matched forward passes N and matched per-window per-modality token budgets (5), on the condition the gates of section 6 select:
- H-B1 (primary): Lift(DA) - Lift(J) > 0 at k0, reported "at or above the MEI" if the estimate is >= 1.0 point. DA separates the four windows with the highest profiled T->I price (D1); J is joint in every window.
- H-B1' (placement): Lift(DA) - Lift(DA-low) > 0 at k0.
- H-B4 (mechanism): Lift(DA) - Lift(TFJ) > 0 at k0 (separation beyond text-earlier).
- H-B3 (dose, estimation only): DA - J does not increase as k decreases (k+ >= k0 >= k-). Not tested.
- Exploratory, in no family: Alt - J at k+, TFJ - J (text-earlier alone), Jd and J-global contrasts, and (conditional stage 4b) H-B2: TF - Alt and IF - Alt one-sided upper limits below 1.0 (unadjusted intersection-union form, E6).
- **Reading conditions (10.2, 7.4)** applied to a positive H-B1 before it is worded as "improves text-image consistency": R-a matched-driven (dC lower 95 percent limit > 0), R-b completion-driven (completion-increment contrast lower 95 percent limit > 0), R-c VLM-concordant (V-lift contrast lower 90 percent limit > 0), all guardrails of 7.3 passed. A failed condition gives "not shown", not a positive alternative (T4): failing R-a, "matched-driven not shown"; failing R-b, "completion-driven not shown"; failing R-c, "not confirmed by the VLM score (achieved MDE reported)". The positive alternative labels need their own interval (7.4). Each is a restriction on wording and adds no alpha.
The minimum effect of interest of 1.0 point equals the scale of the differences UniDisc's authors report between CFG-window conditions (0.301 vs 0.293 vs 0.283 on a 0-1 cosine scale, i.e. 0.8 to 1.8 points) [S, primer C3]; it is a documented scale, not a tuned one. It exists on the CLIP scale only, which is why CLIP lift stays the primary.

**H-C (validity).** Let Delta_{w,k} = Lift(Sep_w) - Lift(J) at budget k, Sep_w separating only window w. Let M_{w,k} = P_w x S^{T->I}(centre of window w) measured on the **profile set D1** (committed-set T->I total, X1-m1), P_w = passes in the window. Then rho_strat = mean over k in {k0, k+} of the within-k Spearman correlation of (M_{w,k}, Delta_{w,k}) over w = 1..8 is at least 0.5 (one-sided permutation p <= 0.05, permuting windows within each k). **Beyond time (X1-M6):** the time-only comparator is E_{w,k} = P_w x (D1 per-window mean marginal entropy of the still-masked image positions); H-C is worded "S_theta predicts where separation helps" only if rho_strat(M, Delta) - rho_strat(E, Delta) has a paired-bootstrap lower 90 percent limit > 0 as well; otherwise "consistent with S_theta, not distinguishable from time", and S_theta is a descriptive trace. H-C is conditional on H-B showing a nonzero Delta somewhere and on a variance check (stop rule 4).

## 4. Task, data, seeds, gates

### 4.1 The task and why (X1-F1)
**G0' (primary): prefix-conditioned joint completion.** Start: the official layout with a COCO-derived prefix (4.2) in the prompt slot, all 32 tail text positions and all L_I image positions masked. Output: one caption (the prefix followed by the generated tokens up to the **caption end** of 4.1b, special tokens stripped, detokenised with the pinned Llama-2 tokenizer) and one image (VQ-16 decode). Only here are both modalities committed under a free schedule. T2I with a clean caption has no text set to co-commit; likewise I2T.

Why a prefix, and why this one. [S/E4] The only free tail observed so far, after a complete caption, read as debris (3 item 1c), and nothing about an empty user turn pushes the tail toward a description. CLIP trained on web alt-text links "copyright", dates and watermark strings to images with overlaid text and stock-photo style, so lift on debris would measure style matching (red-team X1-F1). The jointly generated text has to be about the image's content while remaining co-generated with it. A prefix that stops **mid-phrase, just before the first object noun after the subject** ("A man riding a", next word: surfboard) does three things: it makes the continuation grammatically a noun phrase, so the tail's first content token is an object slot that the image and the text must settle together (the position where cross-modal dependence is expected to be largest, H-A2); it puts the model in caption mode with real COCO style; and it is identical in every arm for a given seed, so prefix-driven topic diversity cancels in contrasts (X1-M1). A complete caption or a category phrase fills the noun slot and leaves only details to co-generate, and the smoke sample shows debris after a complete caption. The cost: the claim narrows to prefix-conditioned completion, the dependence measured is conditional on the prefix, and the observed prefix itself carries T->I information to the image that no schedule changes (hence R-b and prefix-only lift, 7.1). Whether the tail is a continuation or a new segment is not known (3 item 1c); PG-V decides.

### 4.1b The caption-end rule (R2-M2; a pure function of the final tail tokens, frozen in the repository before PG-V)
Let t_1..t_32 be the final tail tokens and P = {0, 2}. A token is *alphanumeric* if its decoded piece contains a letter or a digit, and a *terminator* if its decoded piece contains ".", "!", "?" or a newline. Let f be the smallest index with t_f alphanumeric (none: f = infinity), e0 the smallest index with t_i in P (none: infinity) and e1 the smallest index i > f with t_i a terminator (none: infinity), and **e2 the smallest index i > f whose decoded piece contains "(", ")", "©", "/", "|", "@", "http", "www" or ".com", equals "copyright" or "stock" (case-insensitive), or consists of digits only** (none: infinity; the debris pattern list is frozen in `analysis/EXP-001/debris_patterns.txt` with the other Appendix B lists; v0.5, red-team R3-M1). **Caption end e = min(e0, e1, e2, 33).** PG-V reports the share of samples whose caption end is set by e2. The completion is t_1..t_{e-1}, empty if e <= f (including f = infinity); the caption is the prefix followed by the completion. Positions i >= e are **post-caption**; post-caption tokens not in P are **debris**; the **debris share** of a sample is the number of debris tokens divided by 32. The rule is applied unchanged to every endpoint (7.1-7.3), to the PG-V statistic (4.4), to H-A typing and the I->T receiver exclusion (2), to NC-4 (8.8) and to the guardrails (7.3). CLIP lift on the untruncated caption (prefix plus all non-pad tail tokens before the first `</s>` or pad) is a sensitivity analysis, reported beside the primary. The rule is a heuristic on token pieces (an abbreviation containing a full stop ends a caption early; debris without a terminator and without any e2 pattern stays inside the caption); the debris share, the e2 share and the sensitivity report how much that matters (14 item 16).

### 4.2 The prefix rule (a pure function of the COCO caption text; frozen in the repository before PG-V)
Tag the lower-cased COCO caption with the pinned tagger. Let t1 be the first NOUN or PROPN token and t2 the first NOUN or PROPN token after t1 (**rung 1**), t3 the first after t2 (**rung 2**). The prefix is the words before t2 (rung 1) or before t3 (rung 2). An item is eligible if the rung's prefix has 3 to 6 words (rung 1) or 5 to 9 words (rung 2) and the target noun exists. Items are never selected or dropped by any model output. The prefix text is detokenised to Llama tokens with a leading space convention fixed in the pinned code. Example (rung 1): "A man riding a surfboard on a wave" gives "A man riding a". The rule selects subject-verb-article captions whose prefix ends in a determiner or preposition; the eligible share of COCO val2017 is reported and every claim is scoped to that caption form (R2-m1).

### 4.3 Pre-pilot gate PG-1 (resolution, both legs; X1-M8, e2)
- **Guided leg (feasibility of the checkpoint at 512 px).** Official sampler (50 steps, CFG 3.0), T2I with the **clean full captions** of 32 COCO items disjoint from every pool, generated at 512 px and at 1,024 px from the same captions (paired). Pass if the mean paired difference in raw matched CLIP score (100 x cosine, 512 minus 1,024) is >= -2.0 points and the 512-px matched-minus-shuffled lift >= 0.8 x the 1,024-px lift. Cost about 0.7 compute-hours (1,024 px guided pass 1.25 s x 50 x 32 plus 512 px).
- **Condition-P leg (the condition the experiment runs).** J at k0, unguided, deterministic confidence ranking, rung-1 prefixes of the first 16 gate items, at 512 px and at 1,024 px on the same prefixes (about 6 compute-minutes). Degeneracy screen, registered now (feasibility thresholds, not hypothesis tests): (i) median distinct-VQ-code share of the 512-px images >= 0.5 x the median share of 32 real COCO images encoded at 512 px; (ii) mean CLIP-IQA at 512 px >= the 1,024-px mean minus 0.5 x the SD of CLIP-IQA across the 1,024-px images; (iii) the engineer's visual log (a 4 x 4 grid) shows at most 2 of 16 near-uniform images.
- **Decision table.** Both legs pass at 512 px: resolution 512, position rule P. Guided leg passes, condition-P leg fails at 512: repeat the condition-P leg with P_rand (random positions within modality, values still sampled at temperature 1); if it passes, resolution 512 with **P_rand as the primary position rule** (the R12 fork, same target, E5); if it fails, repeat both legs at 256 px against the same 1,024-px values; if 256 px also fails the experiment stops for the orchestrator (the 1,024-px tier is withdrawn, change log E7). Statistician OC for the paired guided leg: pass probability 0.99 when 512 px is truly as good, 0.87 at the stated deficit [C, round 2, assumptions there].
- Tier cost at 256 px: c_I = 32, t_b about 0.04 s [A, cost model 0.12 s per 1,000 tokens], core path about 3.4 compute-hours of passes (12.1).

### 4.4 Pre-pilot construct gate PG-V (caption validity and prefix common cause; X1-F1, R2-M1, R2-M2)
Run on pool PG [90000, 90048) with J at k0 at the selected resolution and position rule, rung-1 prefixes, **before D0 is generated**. **The pool is 24 COCO prefixes x 2 seeds**: seed 90000 + 2p + e (e in {0, 1}) carries prefix p, and the two seeds of a prefix differ only in the sampler's uniforms (the same 48 samples as in v0.3). Intervals use the prefix as the cluster (24 clusters). J only: no arm contrast exists, so no firewall issue.
- **Statistic.** For each sample, N_i = the completion nouns: word-initial subtokens of the completion (tail tokens before the caption end, 4.1b) whose word is NOUN or PROPN on the final caption, not in the prefix, not in the frozen non-depictive stoplist (`analysis/EXP-001/nonvisual_nouns.txt`; initial content: photo, photograph, image, picture, pic, view, copyright, stock, illustration, background, foreground, front, side, top, bottom, left, right, middle, center, edge, part, kind, type, lot, way, time, day, year). presence_i(image) = 1 if the pinned VLM answers "Is there a {noun} in this image?" with P(yes) >= 0.5 for at least one noun in N_i (a sample with N_i empty counts 0). Matched rate m = mean_i presence_i(image_i). **Cross-prefix shuffled rate** s = the mean over 4 fixed derangements of presence_i(image_pi(i)), each derangement mapping a sample to a sample with a **different prefix** (never the same prefix, so the shared prefix cannot be a common cause). **Same-prefix rate** s_sp = the mean over samples of presence_i(image of the other seed with the same prefix). Per-noun presence rates (matched, cross-prefix, same-prefix) are reported beside the OR-rule rates, because "at least one noun" grows with the noun count and small VLMs lean toward "yes" (R2-m2).
- **Gate passes iff** (a) the share of samples with N_i non-empty is >= 0.60, (b) m >= 0.50, (c) m - s >= 0.25, (d) the median debris share (4.1b) is below 0.5. Operating characteristic of (a)-(c) [C, statistician round 3, simulated with n = 48, four derangements, per-caption noun genericity Beta with concentration 4, all [A]]: non-empty share 0.75-1.0 with m 0.65-0.75 and m - s 0.40: pass 0.97-0.99; non-empty share 0.65 with m 0.65 and m - s 0.40: 0.79 (criterion (a) binds); m 0.55 with m - s 0.40: 0.80 (criterion (b) binds); m - s 0.30: 0.66-0.87; m - s 0.25: 0.45-0.51; m - s 0.10: 0.00-0.02 (criterion (c) binds). (a) and (b) therefore bind near their thresholds. The SD of m - s is 0.03-0.08 because matched and shuffled presence share the caption's nouns. Terminal false stop over the two-rung ladder: 0.003, 0.04 or 0.16 if each adequate rung passes with 0.95, 0.8 or 0.6. (d) is not modelled. The thresholds echo the red-team's "fewer than about half of the captions name an object found in the image" and are feasibility choices.
- **Common-cause check (R2-M1; reported, not a gate criterion).** m - s_sp is reported with a 90 percent cluster-bootstrap interval over the 24 prefixes. [C, red-team round 2, synthetic, perfect checker] If the completion and the image are independent given the prefix, PG-V passes with probability 0.97-1.00 while m - s_sp is about 0; with true co-generation dependence the gap opens (m 0.85-0.91 against a same-prefix rate of 0.41-0.63). If the lower 90 percent limit of m - s_sp is <= 0, the line "no measurable co-generation dependence at this rung (PG-V, 24 prefixes)" is logged with the gate outcomes before D1. The run is not stopped by this alone (24 clusters give low power, SE of m - s_sp about 0.08-0.10 [A]); H-A is protected by PC-D' (8.8), and the report states that H-B has no lever if the dependence is absent.
- **Checker precondition (PC-F).** On 48 real COCO pairs (Ref-real items, real captions' nouns) the same checker must give m_real >= 0.70 and m_real - s_real >= 0.40; otherwise the checker, not the model, is invalid, and another candidate VLM is pinned (pre-freeze amendment) and PC-F rerun. Operating characteristic [C, statistician round 3]: pass probability at least 0.99 at m_real 0.85 with s_real <= 0.30, but 0.79 at s_real 0.40 and 0.71 at (m_real 0.75, s_real 0.30); generic COCO nouns ("man", "table") may push s_real toward 0.40, in which case the checker is re-pinned, not the threshold lowered.
- **Ladder.** Fail at rung 1: repeat PG-V at rung 2 on pool PG2 [90100, 90148) (24 prefixes x 2 seeds again; a longer prefix, 5-9 words, more caption context). Pass at rung 2: rung 2 is the primary prefix for all pools; claims are worded at that rung. Fail at rung 2: **H-B and H-A2 are declared untestable on this checkpoint under prefix-conditioned completion; no D0 or later stage runs; the report is a feasibility result for the orchestrator** (stop rule 11.1).
- **Reported alongside (descriptive).** Pad share of the tail, completion length (tokens before the caption end) and the debris share per sample; the distribution of the caption-end index, from which L_c is set (2); **the class of the plan's committed text token at every pass of the 48 J trajectories**, in particular the share of passes 2 and 6 whose committed token is state-pad or post-caption (the registered falsifier of the v0.3 definition, R2-F1: a share below 0.3 would say the v0.3 source was adequate; the v0.4 source rule stays either way) and, at passes 1, 2, 6, 26, 30, the share of trajectories that have an H-A source and the mean w; CLIP lift and presence rates of the **debris reference D-ref**: J under the v0.2 task (empty prompt, same pool PG seeds), which documents what the old endpoint would have scored. These are logged with the D0 outputs.
- The gate is a feasibility check on J. It does not replace the reading conditions on contrasts.

### 4.5 Unit, references, seeds
- **Unit of analysis**: the seed index i; seed i carries one COCO prefix. All arms use the same i and the same prefix.
- **References on a public prompt set (not in any hypothesis test).** COCO val2017: Ref-real (500 real pairs through every metric), Ref-T2I (the **clean full caption** of the prefix item, tail left masked, image decoded in the 24-image-pass structure of Alt at k0; n = 250), same value rule and metrics. Ref-I2T is dropped (descriptive, unused). COCO is used for evaluation only and is not redistributed.
- **Not covered**: multi-segment interleaved generation, text lengths other than 32 tail tokens, resolutions other than the one PG-1 selects, unconditional (empty-prompt) generation except as D-ref.
- **Seed pools (disjoint; counter-based generator keyed by pool and i; each pool has its own block of COCO items)**: PG [90000, 90048) and PG2 [90100, 90148) (each 24 COCO prefixes x 2 seeds, 4.4); D0 pilot [0, 64); D1 profile [10000, 10128), the first 64 reused for k+; **D2 H-A confirmation [20000, 20256)** at k0; D3 arms [30000, 31000), the first n_conf used; D4 secondary conditions [40000, 40250); D5 blind-model null [50000, 50128).
- **Common random numbers**: sample i draws a float64 uniform u_j for every position j once; a position's value is the inverse-CDF draw from its float64 marginal at the pass in which it is committed, in every arm. Probes use a separate stream and never touch the sampler stream.

## 5. Arms and schedules

### 5.1 Geometry and the plan generator
L_gen = L_T + L_I (1,056 at 512 px; the prefix is observed and not counted). N passes; W = 8 windows; c_T = L_T / W = 4 text and c_I = L_I / W = 128 image tokens per window (at 512 px); P_w = N / W passes per window. Requirements: W divides N and P_w >= 2. Grid: **k0: N = 32 (P_w = 4), k+: N = 16 (P_w = 2), k-: N = 64 (P_w = 8)**. At 256 px the same N grid is used with c_I = 32.

Within a modality the committed positions are the top-count masked positions by maximum marginal probability (ties: lowest position index); condition R and the P_rand fallback use uniformly random positions; values are drawn from the unmodified marginals (section 6). Every arm uses the same weights, attention (full bidirectional), position embeddings, and every committed token is visible to every later pass.

Window rules (pass q = 1..P_w inside window w; floor rule f(q, c, m) = floor(q c / m) - floor((q-1) c / m)):
- **Joint window**: text count f(q, c_T, P_w), image count f(q, c_I, P_w) (at k0: 1 text and 32 image per pass). Per-window totals exactly (c_T, c_I).
- **Pure window**: pass 1 of the window is a text-only pass committing all c_T text tokens; passes 2..P_w are image-only with counts f(q-1, c_I, P_w - 1) (at k0: 42, 43, 43 image tokens; at k+: one pass of 128; at k-: seven passes of about 18). Per-window totals exactly (c_T, c_I). Text goes first inside a pure window (image-first is exploratory).
- **Text-front joint window (TFJ)**: pass 1 commits all c_T text tokens **and** f(1, c_I, P_w) image tokens (at k0: 4 text and 32 image); passes 2..P_w are image-only with f(q, c_I, P_w) image tokens (at k0: 32 each). Per-window totals exactly (c_T, c_I); same image density as a joint window; the window's text is visible to passes 2..P_w exactly as in a pure window.
A schedule is a set M of non-joint windows of one kind.

| Arm | Definition | Role |
|---|---|---|
| J | M = {} | primary comparator |
| Alt | M = {1..8} pure (8 text-only passes, one per window) | modality-pure everywhere |
| DA | pure windows M = the 4 windows with the largest profiled **T->I committed-set price** on D1, fixed before any D3 run | dependence-aware schedule, zero extra passes at test time |
| DA-low | pure windows M = the 4 windows with the smallest profiled price | low-profile placement control (late-placement if H-A holds); NC-7 |
| TFJ | **TFJ windows** on the DA window set, the other windows joint | separates text-earlier from avoided co-commitment (X1-M7); pass-matched |
| Sep_w, w = 1..8 | M = {w} pure | H-C window arms (conditional stage 4) |
| TF | passes 1..8 text-only (4 tokens each), then N - 8 image-only passes (floor rule) | modality-sequential; off-support; conditional stage 4b |
| IF | N - 8 image-only passes, then 8 text-only passes | modality-sequential; off-support; conditional stage 4b |
| J-global | no plan: pooled top-count by max probability over both modalities, total tokens per pass f(q, L_gen, N) | descriptive; conditional stage 4b |
| Jd | diagnostic, not pass-matched: Alt's image pass structure (N - 8 image passes) with the 32 text tokens co-committed by the floor rule over those passes | isolates image density from separation; exploratory (E7a) |
| O | the official sampler with its defaults (maskgit, 50 steps, CFG 3.0, r_temp 4.5, final noise removal) | reference only, condition G |

The "dependence-aware" claim is tested at phase level (which windows to separate), not pair by pair; an instance-level controller costs extra passes and is future work. The window set for DA comes from the D1 profile only and is saved to `runs/EXP-001/da_windows.json` with a hash before D3 starts. The same window set (in normalised time) is used at k+ and k-. Contrasts involving Jd, J-global, TFJ - J, TF and IF are exploratory (E7a).

### 5.2 Matching rules and why
- Matched forward passes N in every arm except Jd (reported with its N - 8) and O (50 steps, guided passes count 2). TFJ, DA, DA-low, Alt, Sep_w are pass-matched and per-window budget-matched.
- Matched per-window per-modality commit budgets (window arms): masked fractions per modality agree at every window boundary, so states stay near the training support.
- **Tokens per pass are not matched** in modality-pure windows (text pass 4 tokens, image passes 42-43 against 1 and 32 in joint passes); this is why DA-low, Jd and TFJ exist. TFJ keeps image density at 32 and puts 4 text tokens in one pass.
- No time input exists (3 item 2), so no time-matching rule is needed.
- The MMaDA-Parallel lesson: no arm uses a differently trained model or an attention mask that blocks anything; "text first" means the window's image positions stay masked while its text is committed.

## 6. Value rule (fixed across arms) and the gates that select the condition

- **Primary condition P**: plain sampling at temperature 1 from the unmodified marginals, no top-p, no top-k, no guidance, position rule = within-modality confidence ranking (a deterministic function of the state). With a state-only position rule and values drawn from the product of marginals, the value slot V of framework-v2 B is 0 and G = 0, so arm differences are differences in schedule, not value rule.
- **Float precision and renormalisation**: model log-probabilities (bf16) are exponentiated and renormalised inside the modality slice in float64; all categorical draws and every KL use float64; inverse-CDF with float64 cumulative sums.
- **Secondary condition G (official guided setting)**: J, Alt, DA with their plans, values sampled at temperature 1 from guided marginals (CFG 3.0, weight cfg x (1 - t), a guided pass counts 2), through `ordered_commit(guided=True)`; plus O. Labelled mixed effects (guidance changes cross-modal conditioning). Conditional stage 6.
- **Robustness condition R**: condition P with uniformly random positions within modality. Trace on 64 trajectories, or on 128 if the rule of 8.3 selects it (core; the decision table of 2 needs it), and arms J, Alt, DA at n = 250 (conditional stage 7). The H-A source is the same plan-independent rule as under condition P, so a random-position probe has a content source whenever one exists (R2-M6). **If P_rand is the primary condition, condition R is condition P with the same arms, n and trace (E5b), and the second H-A arm then runs on the condition PG-1 found degenerate; the reading carries that label.**
- **Dynamic-range gate and the one pre-registered fork (R12, E5)**: Gate statistic (R2-M3) = the point estimate of J's **mean completion increment**, Lift(full caption) - Lift(prefix-only), on D0 at k0 with the full pipeline (caption-end truncation, 4.1b). The observed prefix gives Lift(J) a component that no schedule changes and that alone is likely above 2.0 points, so a gate on Lift(J) cannot detect a dead completion; the increment is the range a schedule can move. The increment is reported beside the MEI, and Paper 1 states the share of that range that the MEI (1.0 point) represents. If the increment is below 1.0 point (1 MEI; v0.5, red-team R3-M2: a 2.0-point threshold sent a live but small range to "untestable" with probability about 0.6-0.8 at a true 1.5 points; between 1.0 and 2.0 the experiment proceeds under P and Paper 1 states that the MEI is half or more of the movable range), **J and Alt at k0 are regenerated on D0 under P_rand** (E5a), the statistic is recomputed for J, and if P_rand passes the primary estimand becomes "DA - J at k0 under P_rand" and D1, D2, D3 run under P_rand with the sizing SE (10.4) taken from Alt - J under P_rand; if both fail, H-B is declared untestable on this checkpoint and only H-A (descriptive) proceeds under P. The fork has two triggers with one target: this gate and the condition-P leg of PG-1 (4.3). The three gate outcomes (PG-1, PG-V, dynamic range) are logged together before D1 starts. The primary estimand is always "DA - J at k0 under the resolution, prefix rung and position rule the gates select". Operating characteristic [A, not re-derived for the increment; replaced by the D0 bootstrap SE of the increment, reported]: if the SE of the gate statistic is about 0.5-0.7 points at n = 64, a true increment of 2.5 fails about 20 percent of the time; a false fail changes the condition, it does not end the experiment. The prefix raises Lift(J) for every arm equally; PG-V (c) and (m - s_sp) are the checks that the completion adds to it and depends on the image.

## 7. Endpoints

### 7.1 Primary (one): matched-minus-shuffled CLIP lift of the full caption
Model: CLIP ViT-L/14 (candidate `openai/clip-vit-large-patch14` [E0]; revision hash and file SHA recorded before PG-V), text truncated at 77 tokens (prefix + at most 32 tokens is at most about 45 Llama tokens, so truncation is expected to be rare and its rate is reported), the checkpoint's own image preprocessing. For a cell (arm, k) with n samples, e_I(x_i), e_T(c_i) are L2-normalised embeddings, c_i the caption (the prefix followed by the completion up to the caption end, 4.1b; "full" in this card means prefix + completion, as opposed to prefix-only),
C_i = cos(e_I(x_i), e_T(c_i)), B_i = 0.5 x [mean_{j != i} cos(e_I(x_j), e_T(c_i)) + mean_{j != i} cos(e_I(x_i), e_T(c_j))], **Lift_i = 100 (C_i - B_i)**.
The endpoint of an arm is mean_i Lift_i; a contrast is the paired mean difference over seeds. The shuffled baseline is computed inside the arm's own samples. No sample is excluded; empty or degenerate completions are scored as they are (the prefix remains) and counted in the guardrails. The per-seed influence value of the mean Lift is psi_i = 100 (C_i - 2 B_i) (Hajek projection; statistician S1); sizing uses it through the bootstrap SE (10.4).
**Why CLIP stays primary.** The documented minimum effect of interest exists on this scale only; the n x n similarity matrix makes the baseline and every bootstrap replicate cheap; and the behaviour of a VLM scorer on a 1.4B model's images is unmeasured until PC-F and PG-V. The construct concern is handled by the task (4), the gates, and reading condition R-c, not by switching the scale.
**Derived quantities (descriptive, same bootstrap):** the matched term C and baseline term B separately (X1-M1); **prefix-only lift**, the same lift with the text replaced by the prefix alone; **completion increment** = Lift(full) - Lift(prefix-only) per seed. The prefix-only text is shorter than the full caption and CLIP responds to length, so the increment mixes "more words" with "right words" (R2-m4); paired arm contrasts cancel most of it, and **R-b is read only as a contrast, never per arm**. Sensitivity (descriptive): the primary and R-a on the untruncated caption of 4.1b.

### 7.2 Companion: V-lift (required by reading condition R-c)
The pinned VLM scores s(x, c) = P("Yes") for the question 'Is this figure showing "{caption}"? Please answer yes or no.' (template to be confirmed at pin time). V_i = s(x_i, c_i) - 0.5 [s(x_i, c_{pi1(i)}) + s(x_{pi2(i)}, c_i)] with two fixed derangements, run on **every** scored sample of the D3 arms (three forwards per sample; no 250-subset any more). Contrasts are paired means over seeds with the same bootstrap. No minimum effect of interest exists on this scale: the card registers only the sign and interval (R-c) and reports the achieved minimum detectable difference from the pilot SD of V differences. The pilot may write that SD (an SD of influence values, permitted by R1).

### 7.3 Secondary (descriptive, intervals, uncorrected, flagged) and guardrails
- Raw matched score 100 C_i; the primary contrast also expressed as a share of the gap Lift(Ref-real) - Lift(J) (O2).
- Object presence (4.4 statistic) on the five k0 arms; completion nouns per caption.
- Image quality: KID (Inception features, subsets) against 5,000 COCO val2017 images; FID not used (n = 500 is below the feature dimension). CLIP-IQA per image.
- Text quality: GPT-2 large perplexity of caption content tokens (rev 32b71b12; the caption is truncated at the caption end); completion length (tokens before the caption end); distinct-1 and unigram entropy across a cell's captions; pad share and debris share of the tail.
- Diversity: mean pairwise cosine of image embeddings and, separately, of caption embeddings in a cell.
- Expected regressions (stated now): text perplexity higher in Alt, DA, DA-low, TFJ (4 text tokens per text pass against 1 in joint passes); KID higher possibly in Alt (denser image passes).
- **Guardrails for a positive H-B1 (point estimates, O3, O4)**: (i) DA's mean completion length within [0.8, 1.25] of J's; (ii) mean pairwise **image**-embedding cosine within **0.01** of J's and (ii-b) mean pairwise **caption**-embedding cosine within 0.01 (tightened from 0.05 because, in the red-team's synthetic model, 0.012 of cosine moved the lift by 0.9 points, about 60-75 lift points per unit cosine; the real CLIP slope is unknown [A] and the R-a decomposition is the actual protection); (iii) empty-completion rate not higher by more than 2 percentage points; (iv) rate of captions truncated at 77 CLIP tokens within 5 percentage points of J's; (v) mean CLIP-IQA not lower than J's by more than 0.2 x the SD of per-image CLIP-IQA in J; (vi) KID not higher than J's by more than 1 SD of the KID subset estimates of J; (vii) the object-presence rate not lower than J's by more than 3 percentage points. A positive with a failed guardrail is reported as "positive with regression" and is not used as support without that qualifier.

### 7.4 The reading of a positive H-B1 (three registered conditions, and what a failure may be called)
R-a (matched-driven): the paired 95 percent interval of dC = mean(C_DA - C_J) has a lower limit > 0 (the Lift contrast is 100 (dC - dB)). R-b (completion-driven): the paired 95 percent interval of the completion-increment contrast has a lower limit > 0. R-c (VLM-concordant): the paired 90 percent interval of the V-lift contrast has a lower limit > 0 (equivalent to a one-sided 5 percent test, an intersection-union with H-B1). Only a positive that passes R-a, R-b, R-c and the guardrails is worded "improves text-image consistency of the co-generated text". The wording ladder is in 13.
**A failed condition is "not shown", not a positive alternative (T4).** A positive alternative label needs its own interval, and each is a wording rule that spends no alpha:
- R-a fails: "matched-driven not shown". "Baseline-driven" (the positive is not support) only if the 95 percent interval of the baseline-term contrast, -100 dB, excludes 0 and that of 100 dC contains 0.
- R-b fails: "completion-driven not shown". "Changes how well the image follows the observed prefix" only if the prefix-only lift contrast has a 95 percent lower limit > 0.
- R-c fails: "not confirmed by the VLM score (achieved MDE reported)". The v0.3 label "changes CLIP lift only" is dropped.
- DA - TFJ not positive (R2-M5): "showing the window's text earlier explains the gain" only if TFJ - J has a 95 percent lower limit > 0 (exploratory, flagged) **and** the DA - TFJ 95 percent interval lies inside (-1, 1). Otherwise "mechanism not separable: a co-commitment benefit and the image-density cost may cancel" (DA - TFJ nets the removal of 128 co-committed pairs per window against image passes of 42-43 tokens instead of 32); Jd - J (density alone) is reported beside it.
When a condition fails with an interval that includes an MEI-sized effect, the report calls it "not confirmed", not "absent" (10.2).

The per-probe traces of section 8, both directions: per-position means, committed-set sums, within-content, between-class and within-pad terms, the information fractions Q, and the floors.

## 8. Estimator protocol for S_theta

### 8.1 Definition
At a probed pass n of a J-arm trajectory with state s (prefix and everything committed so far), the committed sets are the plan's sets: text set A^T (1 token at k0, 2 at k+) and image set A^I (32 at k0, 64 at k+), chosen by the position rule as a function of s alone. For every still-masked image position j (set J_I), the model's dependence is I_theta(a; b_j | s) = E_a KL(p(b_j | s, a) || mbar_j), mbar_j = E_a p(b_j | s, a), with a drawn from the model's own joint over A^T. This is the named split "text-first S_theta" (framework-v2 C). The **committed-set price** S^{T->I}_n = sum_{j in A^I} I_theta(a; b_j | s) is the quantity a text-first pure window removes; the **per-position statistic** S-bar of 2 averages over the receiving positions. **Two T->I sources are distinguished (R2-F1):** the plan's committed text token a (this section; the price for DA and H-C, measured on D1) and the plan-independent H-A source a* (2; the source of the H-A1 statistic, D1 at the four H-A centres and D2). S^{I->T} is symmetric (b drawn jointly, text marginals requeried); its source for H-A is the plan's committed image set. S_theta is the model's own price of co-commitment; it is not the symmetric cross term X and not a statement about the data law (red-team M10).

### 8.2 Procedure per probed pass, per direction
1. Baseline pass at s (the sampler's own pass; shared). Record the one-pass marginals p1_j.
2. **T->I, stratified (X1-M5, T1).** The source is one token with marginal p1_src: for **H-A the plan-independent source a*** (2), for **the committed-set price (DA, H-C; D1 only) the plan's committed text token a** at k0. Pad class P = ids {0, 2}; w = p1_src(P) in float64 (for a*, w < 0.5 by the source rule). Draw R = 8 values: **4 exactly from the conditional given P (pad or EOS) and 4 from the conditional given not-P (content) whenever both classes have float64 mass >= 1e-6.** If a class has mass < 1e-6, its within-class and between-class contributions are set to 0 (bounded by mass x ln 16,384 < 1e-5 nats per position, the bf16-floor scale), all 8 draws come from the other class, and the share of such probes is reported per probe pass. For a* the content class cannot be below 1e-6 (its mass exceeds 0.5); for the plan's token a it can be, and the committed-set price uses the **total** I_hat (between + within-pad + within-content), so it stays defined. One requery pass at s plus a^(r) stores the image marginals m_r(j, .) for all j in J_I (float64, renormalised). Passes added: k_T x R = 8 per source at k0. At k+ (2 tokens) the draw is sequential and iid, total only (the decomposition is not applied; k+ is descriptive).
3. I->T: for r = 1..R, draw the image set b in confidence order in **g = 4 chunks** (8 tokens each at k0): within a chunk from the product of marginals, between chunks the marginals are requeried with the earlier chunks revealed (3 intermediate passes), then one requery pass gives the text marginals for all still-masked text positions. Passes added per r: g = 4 (32 per probe). Approximate joint draw; sensitivity is gate G5.
4. Class means and the decomposition (T->I, k0): mbar_c(j) = mean of m_r(j) over the draws in class c; mbar_j = w mbar_P(j) + (1-w) mbar_C(j). With KL_r(j) = KL(m_r(j) || mbar_{class(r)}(j)): within-pad_j = w x mean_{r in P} KL_r(j); within-content_j = (1-w) x mean_{r in C} KL_r(j); between_j = w KL(mbar_P(j) || mbar_j) + (1-w) KL(mbar_C(j) || mbar_j); total I_hat_j = between_j + within-pad_j + within-content_j (an exact identity at any R). The pad/EOS indicator carries length or termination information (between), the pad-versus-EOS identity is non-semantic too (within-pad), and **the content term within-content_j, computed from the H-A source a*, is the T->I quantity H-A1 uses**. For the unstratified totals (I->T, k+) I_hat_j = (1/R) sum_r KL(m_r(j) || mbar_j), mbar_j the mean (plug-in mixture estimator; bounded above by ln R per position).
5. Naive reference for the inconsistency check: N_j = (1/R) sum_r KL(m_r(j) || p1_j) = I_hat_j + KL(mbar_j || p1_j) (exact identity); I_inc,j = KL(mbar_j || p1_j) is reported at every probe next to S_theta.
6. Committed-set sums S^{T->I}_n = sum_{j in A^I} I_hat_j (total, from the plan's committed token), its content term, and S^{I->T}_n = sum_{j in A^T} I_hat_j with state-pad recipients (p1_j mass on P >= 0.5; r1) excluded and the excluded share reported. Per-position statistics of 2 over J_d.
Probe isolation: probes run on copies of the state and never change the sampler's RNG state or the trajectory. A saturation diagnostic (share of positions with I_hat_j > 0.5 ln R) is reported. The class-conditional plug-in bound is ln 4 per class term at R = 8 (conservative for front-loading: the bias is negative and larger where dependence is larger, toy 35 percent low at R = 4, 1.4 percent at R = 64 [V]).

### 8.3 What is probed and how many trajectories
- R = 8 for D1 and D2. All R draws are stored: two disjoint halves (R = 4) give the Monte Carlo SD; the extrapolation 2 I(8) - I(4) is a sensitivity analysis only.
- **D1 (profile; DA windows, H-C predictor, E4 power projection)**: 128 trajectories at k0 with **T->I committed-set price** probes (the plan's committed token, 8 passes) at the centre pass of each window: centre(w) = (w - 1) P_w + 2, i.e. passes 2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30 of 32; **plus T->I H-A source probes (a*, 8 passes) at passes 2, 6, 26, 30 on all 128** (the four H-A centres are window centres 1, 2, 7, 8; these feed the E4 projection and the reports of mean w, source availability and H_src); **I->T** probes at passes 2, 6, 26, 30 on the first 64 of these trajectories (for the E4 projection and the H-A2 projection only); 64 trajectories at k+ with T->I probes at the first pass of each 2-pass window (centre(w) = 2(w - 1) + 1, passes 1, 3, ..., 15).
- **D2 (H-A confirmation)**: 256 trajectories at k0 with both directions at passes 2, 6, 26, 30 (T->I from the H-A source a*, I->T from the committed image set), and pass 1 on the first 128. No extension (R7). G1b is re-measured on the first 32 trajectories (8.7). D2 does not measure the committed-set price.
- **Condition R trace**: n_R trajectories at k0 under random positions within modality, probes at passes 2, 6, 26, 30 and pass 1 on n_R/2; the H-A source is the plan-independent rule of 2. **Size rule (R2-M6, T5, r3):** after D1 and before D2, for each direction the smallest true ratio resolved with power 0.8 by the condition-R rule (lower 90 percent limit > 1.0) is computed at n_R = 64 and at n_R = 128 by resampling the D1 trajectories (the actual ratio-of-sums statistic) and logged; D1 runs under confidence ranking, so this approximates the random-position precision (the I->T arm is the weaker one, few non-pad positions at the late probes). The statistician's reference values at a fixed cv are 1.20-1.25 (cv 0.5) and about 1.45-1.55 (cv 1.0) at n_R = 64 [C, round 3]. If the value at 64 exceeds 1.30 in any direction and the value at 128 is at most 1.30, the trace runs at 128 (+13,568 passes, 0.5 compute-hours, included in the sizing X of 10.4); otherwise it runs at 64 and "not distinguishable" is stated as the expected label for each direction whose value exceeds 1.30.
- Probe passes at k0: T->I R k_T = 8 per source, I->T R g = 32; D2 and condition R carry one T->I source and I->T = 40 per probed pass; D1 centres at passes 2, 6, 26, 30 carry both T->I sources = 16 (+32 on the first 64 trajectories); k+ T->I 16.

### 8.4 Cost as a formula
Extra forward passes per probed pass = R k_T (T->I) + R g (I->T). Per trajectory: passes_traj = N + sum over probes of (R k_T [+ R g]) x f_ord, f_ord in {1, 2} (2 only if G5 requires both orders). Defaults: k0 D1: 32 + 8 x 8 + 4 x 8 = 128 (+ 4 x 32 = 128 on 64 trajectories); k0 D2: 32 + 4 x 40 = 192 (+ 40 for the pass-1 probe on 128); k+ D1: 16 + 8 x 16 = 144. GPU time = passes x t_b (t_f for fp32 probe passes if G1b requires, after measuring t_f).

### 8.5 Information fraction, normalisation and the exclusion rules
Q_j = I_hat_j^c / H(mbar_j), the share of position j's mixture-marginal entropy that the revealed other modality explains (0 <= Q_j <= 1 because I <= H). Positions with H(mbar_j) < 0.05 nats are excluded from Q, R_Q, R_Q2. Q controls the bound S_j <= H_j (nouns are high-entropy slots, function words are not). R_Q2 adds the source side: I(a; b_j | s) <= min(H(a | s), H(b_j | s)); H_src as in 2. POS labels come from the final caption at word-initial subtokens, assigned after the trajectory ends; because the label is realised, a position's type is correlated with its marginal, which is why Q is the primary statistic for H-A2. Exclusions for H-A1: I->T state-pad recipients (p1_j-based, state-time, 2) and post-caption recipients (caption-end rule 4.1b, assigned after the trajectory ends); the mbar-based pad-class version of v0.3 is reported as a sensitivity (r1); T->I is handled by the source rule and the content term (a probe with no source carries no weight). Reported per probe pass and arm: the excluded share of recipients, the share of probes without a source, **the mean and the distribution of w** (not the share of draws in the pad class, which is 4/8 by design; T1), H_src, and the share of probes at which a class had mass below 1e-6. The **all-masked-position quantity is reported only as a per-position mean**; a summed version is never evidence of front-loading (X1-m2, it grows with the number masked).

### 8.6 Exploratory maps (no decision rule)
S_{T->I} per image position on the 2D token grid against a foreground map of the final image (pinned segmenter, quality on these images unverified); S_{I->T} by the spatial-word list; correlation of sample-level cumulative S with Lift within the J arm.

### 8.7 Validity checks of the estimator (gates)
- **G0 unit test on the toy**: the production estimator, run on the tables of `analysis/framework/check_crossmodal_mixture.py`, reproduces: identity naive = I + KL(mbar||p1) to 1e-12; block J gives naive 0.08109, mixture 0; finite-R estimate below I_theta in 40/40 within 3 standard errors. **Added (T2)**: the stratified estimator satisfies total = between + within-pad + within-content to 1e-12; and on a toy with a known class structure and exact I, the stratified total (128 draws per class) and the unstratified total (R = 256) each lie within 1 percent of the exact I, and at R = 8 both lie below it. Equality of the two totals in expectation is not required: their plug-in biases differ at finite R (6.6 combined standard errors apart on the statistician's toy at R = 8 [C, round 3]), so that wording could fail a correct implementation.
- **G1 noise floor, per probe and per direction (X1-M4)**. (G1a) the same state run in different batch positions and compositions; (G1b) a **no-information reveal**: R = 8 requeries, each revealing a different position among those with max marginal >= 0.999 (post-EOS or pad tail positions for T->I; for I->T the revealed positions are image positions; fewer than 6 at a probe: use positions with max marginal >= 0.99 **on the same side as the revealed source only** (T->I: text positions; I->T: image positions), and report it, because revealing different image codes changes neighbouring image marginals for real and would overstate the T->I floor, R2-m3; still fewer: that probe is flagged "floor unmeasured" and is dropped from H-A1 **before D2**), and the mixture estimator applied to the R requeries (these differ only by input-dependent rounding). It is measured **at passes 2, 6, 26 and 30 (and 1) on the same 16 pilot trajectories**, the floor being the mean over the same receiving positions as the probe's statistic; the floor-versus-entropy curve (floor per position against H(mbar_j)) is reported; the floor is **re-measured on the first 32 D2 trajectories**. **Pass rule**: for each probe and each direction the probe-level mean of the statistic is at least 20 times that probe's own mean floor. "Smallest S value" in v0.2 meant nothing definable; this replaces it. Failing that, probe passes run with fp32 weights (6.3 GB, t_f to be measured) and the gate repeats; failing that too, H-A and H-C are declared not measurable. **D2 consequence and dropped probes (T3):** the D2 re-measurement never drops, adds or replaces a probe. If an H-A1 probe fails the 20x rule on D2 in direction d, H-A1 in direction d is not reported as positive; it is labelled "floor-limited at pass p" (a floor that rises with entropy can make a floor-limited trace look front-loaded, X1-M4, so the D2 consequence can only remove a positive). A pilot-dropped probe ("floor unmeasured") is dropped before D2; if both early or both late probes of a direction are dropped, that direction is "not measurable" and H-A1 is decided by the intersection-union test over the remaining parts with that limit stated.
- **G2 null by construction (NC-1)**: the modality-blind wrapper (text marginals from a pass with all image positions masked; image marginals from a pass with all text positions masked, **prefix included**) must give |S^{T->I}|, |S^{I->T}| and I_inc below the G1 floor at all probes of 16 trajectories.
- **G3 injection test (block J on the real network)**: wrapper that returns the modality-blind image marginals mixed 70/30 with uniform over the image slice whenever any text token beyond s is revealed, unmixed otherwise. True dependence is 0. Required: mixture estimator below the G1 floor; naive estimator equals I_inc within 1e-6 and is above 10 x the floor at the first probe.
- **G3b non-semantic injection (new, X1-M5)**: wrapper that returns the modality-blind image marginals mixed 70/30 with uniform when the revealed token is in the pad class P and unmixed otherwise (a length-only dependence). Required: the total T->I statistic exceeds 10 x the floor at the first probe; the between-class term carries at least 90 percent of it; the within-content term is below the floor. This shows the decomposition routes length or termination information away from the H-A1 statistic.
- **G4 bias**: on 16 trajectories, probes at windows 1, 4, 8, compare R = 8 with R = 32 (16 pad-class and 16 content-class draws); the early/late ratio of the per-position statistic computed with R = 8 must be within 25 percent of the one with R = 32, in both directions. The pilot prints only this agreement, not the ratio itself (e1).
- **G5 draw order and granularity (E2)**: on 16 trajectories, at the probes of passes 2 and 30, the I->T estimate for g = 4, g = 8 and g = 32 (exact). Statistic per probe: ratio of means over trajectories, S(g)/S(32). Registered g = 4 if both per-probe ratios lie in [0.77, 1.30] and the early/late ratio of means with g = 4 is within 25 percent of that with g = 32; else g = 8 under the same rule (cost per probe R (k_T + 8) = 72 passes, D2 +1.5 compute-hours, stated); else I->T is "chunk-dependent": H-A1 is decided on T->I alone, H-A2 is reported descriptively, and the H-A claim is worded "T->I only". The Monte Carlo SD of each estimate (from the two R = 4 halves) is reported with G5. The confidence order is compared with its reverse on 8 of these trajectories; an order difference above 0.3 makes the S_theta used afterwards the mean over both orders (f_ord = 2).
- **G6 probe invariance**: generation with probes on equals generation with probes off (identical token sequences; if kernels are nondeterministic, the rate of differing sequences must not exceed that between two probe-off runs). I2 and I3 borrow the same rate rule.
- **Precision block (X1-m3)**: on 64 states from D0 J trajectories at 512 px, bf16 weights against the checkpoint's fp32 weights with bf16 autocast under the **current shim** (file SHA-256 and version string in the manifest): top-1 agreement, mean TV, mean entropy difference, row-sum deviation. The 256-px figures of 3 item 5b are not used for any decision.
If G1, G2, G3, G3b, G4 or PC-D' (8.8) fail, H-A and H-C are reported as "not measurable by this estimator on this checkpoint" and no inferential claim is made about S_theta; H-B still runs with DA labelled "profile-guided by an unvalidated trace".

## 9. Controls and confounds (checklist the engineer signs)

1. Matched passes, matched per-window per-modality budgets; the unmatched tokens per pass in pure windows reported per arm (5.2).

3. No time input (3 item 2); no time rule needed.
4. Tie rule: lowest position index; no random tie-breaking anywhere (`ordered_commit` commits exactly the prescribed set).
5. float64 for every renormalisation, softmax-after-logits, categorical draw and KL; common random numbers by position (4.5).

All per-sample outputs (tokens, caption, image, per-pass committed sets, probe arrays) saved under `runs/EXP-001/<stage>/` atomically per seed, resumable; free system commit logged per stage. **The manifest of every stage records the shim file SHA-256 and version string, the gpu_guard telemetry path per chunk, torch, driver and clock state.**

## 10. Statistics

### 10.1 Unit, design, interval, p-values
Unit = seed i. Every comparison is paired over seeds. Interval: percentile paired bootstrap over seeds, B = 10,000, recomputing the shuffled baseline inside each replicate (partners with the same original seed index excluded when a seed is drawn twice); BCa as a sensitivity. In a synthetic check the bootstrap SE matched the true sampling SD (0.307 against 0.306) [C]. Sensitivity for the primary (O8): an arm-swap randomization test (swap DA and J samples within seed, recompute both baselines, 10,000 draws); reported, not decisive.
**Two-sided p for a paired contrast (R5)**: p = 2 min{(1 + #{theta* <= 0})/(B + 1), (1 + #{theta* >= 0})/(B + 1)}, capped at 1. If any p lies within 2 sqrt(p(1-p)/B) of the Holm threshold it is compared with, that bootstrap is rerun once with B = 100,000 and a fixed seed recorded in the manifest, and the B = 100,000 value is used. For H-A and H-C see 10.3.
Common random numbers help for plans sharing a prefix of windows (window arms, TFJ and DA share their joint windows) and not for TF/IF. The sample-size rule uses a directly estimated paired SE and needs no rho term.

### 10.2 Primary contrast, multiplicity, outcome classes, error control
- **Fixed-sequence gatekeeping at FWER 0.05 for H-B (R3).** Step 1: H-B1 (DA - J at k0), two-sided 0.05. Step 2, only if step 1 rejects: Holm at 0.05 over the **four** secondary tests (1) Alt - J at k0, (2) DA - J at k+, (3) DA - DA-low at k0 (H-B1'), (4) DA - TFJ at k0 (H-B4). If step 1 does not reject, the four are reported with Holm-adjusted p-values and labelled exploratory. A test that is not run enters Holm with p = 1. DA - J at k- is part of H-B3 (estimation only). TFJ - J, Jd, J-global, TF, IF contrasts and H-B2 are exploratory, in no family (E6, E7a).
- **Outcome classes (R4)** for a contrast with estimate d and 95 percent interval (lo, hi), m = 1.0: *positive* = Holm-adjusted p <= 0.05 (for the primary: unadjusted p <= 0.05) and d > 0, labelled "at or above the MEI" if d >= m; *reversed* = the same with d < 0; *null* = (lo, hi) inside (-m, m); *inconclusive* = otherwise. Unadjusted 95 percent intervals are always reported. Operating characteristics [C] (n = 500, sigma_L = 4, rho 0):

| true d (points) | P(lo > 0) | P(lo > 0 and d_hat >= 1) | P("null") |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | 0.024 | 0.000 | 0.954 |
| 0.5 | 0.507 | 0.024 | 0.506 |
| 1.0 | 0.976 | 0.501 | 0.026 |
| 1.25 | 0.999 | 0.839 | 0.002 |
| 1.5 | 1.000 | 0.976 | 0.000 |

  "Power 0.977" is P(significant) at d = 1.0; the probability of being labelled "at or above the MEI" at d = 1.0 is 0.50 and at least 0.84 for d >= 1.25. sigma_L is replaced by the pilot value (10.4).
- **Reading conditions R-a, R-b, R-c (7.4)** are wording restrictions on a positive primary. R-a and R-b are 95 percent two-sided intervals on dC and the completion-increment contrast, R-c a 90 percent interval on the V-lift contrast; R-c is an intersection-union with the primary, so no alpha is spent. Their power is lower than the primary's by construction; the report states the achieved precision of each and, when a condition fails with an interval that includes the MEI-sized effect, calls it "not confirmed", not "absent".
- **Conjunctions are intersection-union tests** and need no multiplicity adjustment: the H-B null across k+ and k0 and contrasts.
- **Error control is per claim (R11).** H-B: FWER 0.05 as above. H-A: one conjunctive claim (H-A1 both directions and H-A2, intersection-union, one-sided 0.025 per ratio, i.e. the 95 percent two-sided lower limit). H-C: one-sided 0.05. No Bonferroni across EXP-001 and EXP-002 (EXP-002 is demoted, D0013). Paper 1 also reports the sensitivity with every confirmatory level halved (primary 0.025, Holm family 0.025, H-A 0.0125 per ratio, H-C 0.025) and which claims survive.
- Secondary metrics of 7.3 are descriptive with intervals, uncorrected, flagged.

### 10.3 H-A and H-C statistics
- H-A1: the composition-standardised ratio pair of section 2 (pooled and complete-case; v0.5) per direction, percentile bootstrap over trajectories, B = 10,000, with the stratum weights omega_s held fixed at their full-sample values inside every replicate; supported if the lower 95 percent limits of both members of the pair are >= 1.5 in both directions; negatives and "inconclusive (composition)" as defined in section 2 (f) and (d). Weak falsification: the **97.5 percent upper limit** of any of the three ratios (two directions and H-A2) is < 1.5; this is a disjunction, so P(false weak falsification) can reach about 0.075 when all three true ratios equal 1.5, and this is stated in the report (O6). Reversed: the upper limit of an H-A1 ratio is < 1.0.
- H-A1-Q, H-A1-Q2 and H-A2: as in 2, same bootstrap. The condition-R decision table (2) uses the same statistic on n_R trajectories (64 or 128 by the rule of 8.3).
- **No extension (R7a); power stated from D1 before D2 (E4).** The earlier figures [C, statistician, delta method] P(lower limit >= 1.5) = 0.935 at a true ratio of 1.75 hold only at cv 0.6 [A]; they are 0.74 at cv 0.8, 0.55 at 1.0 and 0.28 at 1.5, and about 0.25 with 30 percent zero values at cv 1.0 (simulated). After D1 and before D2, the cv of the window-pair averages (early, late) and their correlation are computed from D1 at k0 for each direction (T->I from the H-A source probes on the 128 trajectories, I->T from the 64 trajectories with I->T probes) and for H-A2, and the projected P(lower limit >= 1.5 | ratio 1.75) per part and for the conjunction at n = 256 is logged. The projection is made on the decision statistic itself (v0.5: the composition-standardised pair of section 2, pooled and complete-case, read as an intersection-union test, so its power is at most that of either member), the ratio of ratios of pooled sums with its per-trajectory numerator-denominator covariance (|J_T| varies for I->T), by resampling the D1 trajectories to n = 256 (r3); the mean w, the source availability and H_src per probe from D1 are logged with it (T1, R2-F1). Paper 1 quotes the logged figure, not 0.935. The per-position statistic averages over many positions and is expected to have a smaller cv than the committed-set sum [I]; D1 decides. If the projection is below 0.5 for a part the orchestrator may approve the optional extension n_D2 <= 512 (+4.2 wall-clock hours); otherwise D2 runs at 256 and "inconclusive" is the expected label for that part.
- H-C: rho_strat = mean of the within-k Spearman correlations of (M_{w,k}, Delta_{w,k}) over w = 1..8, for k in {k0, k+}; one-sided permutation test permuting windows within each k separately, with the p-value from the exact convolution of the two n = 8 null distributions (no Monte Carlo error; r5); supported if rho_strat >= 0.5 and p <= 0.05. One-sided 5 percent critical value of rho_strat = **0.452** (exact: P(rho_strat >= 0.452) = 0.048 under independent within-k permutation; the v0.3 value 0.440 has P = 0.053) [C, statistician round 3, T7]. The support rule rho_strat >= 0.5 binds (P = 0.031 under the null), so the effective level of H-C with two strata is 0.031. If only one stratum is run: exact one-sided 5 percent critical value 0.643 (P(rho >= 0.643) = 0.048 at n = 8, enumerated here). The pooled Spearman over 16 cells is not used (false support in 97 percent of cases by a between-k shift alone, statistician's simulation). The interval for rho_strat is a double bootstrap (seeds for Delta, trajectories of D1 for M). M comes from D1, which is independent of D3. **Time comparator** (X1-M6): the same statistic with E_{w,k} in place of M; the difference rho_strat(M) - rho_strat(E) is bootstrapped paired over the same resamples; the "beyond time" wording needs its lower 90 percent limit > 0.

### 10.4 Sample size and its stated assumptions [A until the pilot]
- Assumptions: sigma_L = 4 points; rho_pair = 0; effect of interest m = 1.0. Under the influence-based SD the required n rises by about 35 percent (Holm-level n about 620 with the four-member family) [C, statistician round 3].
- n for power 0.90 at two-sided alpha: n = ((z_{1-a/2} + z_{0.90}) sigma_d / m)^2 with sigma_d = 5.66: 337 at alpha = 0.05 and 457 at alpha = 0.05/4 = 0.0125 (the smallest Holm level of the four-member family) [C, round 3; the v0.3 figures 505 at 0.05/7 and 0.897 belonged to a seven-member family, T6d]. At n = 500: power 0.977 and 0.927 (sigma_L = 4), 0.885 and 0.747 (5), 0.750 and 0.555 (6) [alpha 0.05 and 0.0125]; at n = 530: 0.983 and 0.942, 0.902 and 0.776, 0.774 and 0.585.
- **Rule (R2, E5a, E7b)**: sigma_d,up = sqrt(64) x (bootstrap SE of the paired contrast Alt - J at k0 on D0, full Lift pipeline with in-replicate baseline recomputation, B = 10,000, **under the condition the gates select**) x sqrt(63 / chi2_{0.20, 63}). n_req = ceil(1.5 x ((1.96 + 1.2816) sigma_d,up / 1.0)^2). **n_conf = min(max(500, n_req), max(500, n_cap))** (T6), where **n_cap** is the largest n in [500, 1000] whose projected core-path wall-clock wall(n) = (1/duty) [(X + 192 n) t_b / 3600 + 1.0 + evaluation(n)] is at most **32 hours** (the orchestrator's authorisation of the core path under gpu_guard; v0.3 used 26). ## 11. Stop rules and falsifiers

Staging is by information per GPU-hour (section 12). Each stage ends in a written verdict file; the next runs only if its precondition holds.

1. **Stage gates.** PG-1, PG-V, PC-F, G0 to G6, G3b, PC-D' and the NC/PC checks must pass at stages 0-2 (a PC-D' failure moves H-A to "not measurable" before D1; H-B and DA continue, DA labelled "profile-guided by an unvalidated trace" as in 8.7); a failed positive control or gate stops the later stages for the measurement it protects (8.7). **A PG-V failure at rung 2 ends the experiment as a feasibility result.**
2. **H-A.** Positive: H-A1 lower limits >= 1.5 in both directions and H-A2 lower limit >= 1.5 (with the wording rules of 2). Weak falsification as in 10.3; reversed as in 10.3. Decided once at n = 256 (or at the approved n_D2), no extension. The trace shape (monotone, mid-trajectory peak) is reported whatever the verdict. A weak falsification or reversal of a T->I ratio that carries the "source-limited" label of 2 (early mean H_src below half of the late mean) is reported as such and does not count toward the Negative or Drop-P5 classes of item 7 until R_Q2 has been read.
3. **H-B, stage 3.** Run arms J, Alt, DA, DA-low, TFJ at k0 and J, DA at k+. If the contrasts Alt - J and DA - J are null (interval inside (-1, 1)) at k0 and DA - J is null at k+, H-B is reported as "null at k in {k+, k0}" (an intersection-union conclusion), the conditional stages 4, 4b and 5 do not run, and the claim is limited to those k and this value rule. If any contrast is positive, run stage 5 (k-) after the reading conditions are scored. If reversed (J better than Alt or DA with p <= 0.05 and d <= -1), run stages 4b and 4 to localise it (each needs the orchestrator's yes).
4. **H-C, stage 4.** Run the Sep arms only if (i) some stage-3 contrast is positive or reversed, and (ii) the projected SE(Delta_w) from the pilot Alt - J SE at the allowed n is at most 0.15 point (needs rho_pair >= 0.65 at n = 500 or >= 0.30 at n = 1000). Otherwise H-C is "not tested at this budget", S_theta stays descriptive; decided before any Sep run and logged.
5. **Dose.** H-B3 reported as a pattern of point estimates (k+ >= k0 >= k-); estimation only.
6. **Budget stop.** The core path (stages 0-6 of 12.1) has a planned wall-clock of **about 27 hours at n_conf = 596 and the planning t_b (28 with the 128-trajectory condition-R trace) and a ceiling of 32 hours** (the orchestrator's authorisation; the n_conf rule of 10.4 keeps the projection under it, or sends the projected overrun to the orchestrator before D1). Every conditional stage needs its precondition and the orchestrator's yes; its wall-clock is stated in 12.1 and the cumulative wall-clock may not pass 40 hours without a new yes (stage 7 alone, about 14 to 16 wall-clock hours, passes it whenever the core exceeds about 24 hours, so its yes is asked explicitly with the figure). Exceeding the core ceiling stops the work for the orchestrator. **A gpu_guard temperature stop (exit 90) is not a failure of a stage but is logged; three temperature stops in one stage stop the stage for the orchestrator.**
7. **What makes P5 leave the review's open problems or be reported as negative.**
   - Negative (on this checkpoint, resolution, prefix rung and value rule only): H-A weak falsification in any of its ratios, or H-B null at k+ and k0 with all gates passed. A negative H-A with a positive H-B is reported as such (separation helps although the trace is not front-loaded; the mechanism claim is dropped).
   - Drop P5 from the open problems (replace with "measured, small"): both of the above hold, G0 to G6 and PC-D' passed, and condition R reproduces the null for H-A, meaning that in each direction where H-A was weakly falsified the condition-R 97.5 percent upper limit of that ratio is also below 1.5 (T5).
   - Keep P5 open and say the measurement was inconclusive: any of G1 to G4 or PC-D' failed, the dynamic-range gate failed in both conditions, or an interval straddles its effect of interest at the fixed n.
   - Do not generalise to any other checkpoint, to guided sampling, to unconditional generation, or to the data law q in any of these cases.

### 12.2 GPU safety protocol (every GPU stage; new constraint)
- **Wrapper.** Every GPU process runs as `python tools/gpu_guard.py --log runs/EXP-001/<stage>/telemetry-<stage>-<chunk>.csv --max-temp 85 --max-minutes 15 -- python <stage script> --resume` (arguments as in tools/gpu_guard.py; defaults: three consecutive hot readings at 5-second sampling stop the job; the CSV is flushed per row and records temperature, power, clocks, utilisation, memory, pstate and throttle reasons). Exit 91 is a normal chunk boundary; exit 90 is a temperature stop.
- **Duty cycle about 50 percent.** A driver script runs a chunk, then idles with no GPU process for at least as long as the chunk ran (CPU-only analysis, tagging and bootstrap may run in the idle time), and starts the next chunk only when the GPU is below 65 C [A, the card's choice; the guard's stop is 85 C]. After an exit 90 the next chunks use `--max-minutes 10` and the idle period doubles; three exit-90 stops in one stage stop it (11.6).
- **Batch size at most 4** (effective batch at most 4 for CFG stages: batch 2 with the doubled guided pass). There is no batching speed-up, so batch 1-2 is the default.
- **Resumable with checkpoints.** Outputs are written atomically per (stage, arm, seed); a manifest lists completed units; a restart skips them. The counter-based generator makes a resumed seed identical to an uninterrupted one (verified by G6's rate rule). Each chunk gates on free system commit (3 item 5c) before it starts.
- **Telemetry is analysed at the end of each stage** (maximum and median temperature, throttle-reason share, mean SM clock) and written in the verdict file. The pilot re-measures t_b and the wall-to-compute ratio under the guard, and the n_cap rule (10.4) uses the measured values.
- No other GPU load runs during a stage. A crash or reboot is logged, the stage resumes from the manifest, and the orchestrator is told (failures ledger).
- The first chunk of each new stage is run at `--max-minutes 5` with the engineer watching the telemetry.

## 14. Risks the red-team should attack first

1. The prefix task may not make the tail a continuation (3 item 1c): the free tail may still be a new segment; PG-V is the only guard, and a second failure ends the experiment. Even when PG-V passes, a gate on 48 samples with thresholds chosen here (m >= 0.5, m - s >= 0.25) may admit mostly easy, generic nouns ("man", "table"). Agreement that the prefix alone produces (common cause) passes the cross-prefix criteria; the same-prefix rate s_sp is reported but is not a gate, and has low power at 24 clusters (item 15).
2. The observed prefix gives the image T->I information that no schedule changes, so cross-modal dependence between the *remaining* text and the image may be small; the all-masked per-position statistic can sit near the floor (G1b per probe decides) and H-A may be "not measurable"; PC-D' checks this at the open slot on 16 trajectories before D1.
3. The tail is 32 positions for a completion of about 8-10 tokens; the positions after it may be pad or, as the only smoke sample suggests, debris (R2-M2). Under confidence ranking the plan's committed text token is plausibly a pad or debris position early; v0.4 therefore measures H-A from a plan-independent source and keeps the committed token for the DA price only. The composition of the still-masked set still changes over time (hard positions remain), which Q and condition R only partly control.
4. The T->I statistic rests on a single source token per probe at k0 (entropy bound H(a*)); R_Q2 normalises by it but the content term within a stratified draw uses 4 content-class draws at R = 8 (plug-in cap ln 4).
5. The I->T draw is a chunked approximation; the bias direction is unknown (G5 tests it on 16 trajectories at two probes).
6. The bf16 floor under the corrected shim is not measured yet (3 item 5d); G1b may fail at early probes where the floor is largest.
7. TFJ commits 4 text tokens with 32 image tokens at pass 1, which enlarges the within-pass text-image cross term relative to J; DA - TFJ and TFJ - J are therefore not clean single-factor contrasts, and the card says so (the image-density factor remains in DA - TFJ via 42-43 against 32 image tokens, with the opposite sign to the co-commitment benefit, so a null DA - TFJ is not read as "text earlier explains", 7.4).
8. H-C has 8 cells per stratum, noisy Delta and a time comparator that may be collinear with M; it may be untestable by design.
9. The primary metric is one CLIP model whose real diversity slope is unknown; the VLM scorer has no documented minimum effect of interest and its behaviour on this checkpoint's images is unmeasured until PC-F and PG-V.
10. The VLM environment may conflict with the generation venv (transformers pin); evaluation runs separately.
11. No licence for the checkpoint limits what can be published; the release policy withholds generations.
12. Sampler-trajectory confound: the ratio the condition-R trace resolves at power 0.8 is 1.20-1.25 at cv 0.5 and about 1.45-1.55 at cv 1.0 at 64 trajectories [C, statistician round 3]; D1 logs the value for its own cv (D1 runs under confidence ranking, so this approximates the random-position precision), and the trace runs at 128 when that moves the value to 1.30 or below (8.3). A smaller gap is not resolved; "not distinguishable" is then the honest label and sampler-free wording is withheld. The I->T arm is the weaker one (few non-pad positions at the late probes).
13. Wall-clock is about 27 to 28 hours at n = 596 and the planning t_b, against a ceiling of 32: the slack is about 4 hours, which a measured t_b above about 0.15 s (throttling under the duty cycle) or the G5 and P_rand contingencies together (32.5 hours at n = 596) use up. The n_conf rule of 10.4 then lowers n (never below 500, with the achieved power reported) or sends the projected overrun to the orchestrator before D1.
14. The H-A T->I source is the most confident non-pad candidate within L_c. Its entropy may differ between early and late probes (the early candidate may be a function word, the late one a remaining hard position), so a raw ratio partly reflects H_src, in either direction. Controls: R_Q2 and R_Q, H_src reported per probe, the "source-limited" label (2, 11.2). An alternative source (the leftmost still-masked non-pad tail position) was not adopted and is untested; L_c is a design parameter set from the PG-V J samples.
15. PG-V's same-prefix comparison uses 24 prefix clusters: a non-positive m - s_sp at a lower 90 percent limit is weak evidence of "no co-generation dependence", and a positive one does not prove it. PC-D' (16 trajectories, one slot) is the operational check and is itself small.
16. The caption-end rule is a heuristic on token pieces (4.1b): an abbreviation with a full stop ends a caption early, and debris without any terminator stays inside the caption and enters CLIP and the VLM. The debris share and the untruncated-caption sensitivity bound the effect; a caption that is mostly debris is a PG-V (d) failure, not a silent pass.
