## Part 2. Findings on v0.3, ranked

### Fatal

**R2-F1. The H-A1 T->I statistic tests when this sampler commits content text, not where dependence sits. Under the
prefix task it is near zero at early probes by construction. Its likely "weak falsification" then feeds the stop rule
that removes P5 from the open problems.** Location: 2 (H-A1, per-position statistic with the within-content term);
8.1; 8.2 steps 2 and 4; 11.7.
- Chain. (1) The T->I source is the plan's committed text set A^T: one token at k0, chosen by within-modality confidence
  ranking (8.1). (2) Under the prefix task the completion is about 8-10 tokens of a 32-position tail (card 3 item 1b
  [A]). The most confident tail positions at the start are therefore the far-tail positions (pad or EOS, or a formulaic
  tail, R2-M2), not the open noun slot after "A man riding a". With one text token per pass, those positions are
  committed in windows 1-2, and the content positions remain masked until late passes [I]. (3) The registered T->I
  quantity is the within-content term, (1 - w) x mean KL over content-class draws, with w = p1_a(pad class) (8.2 step 4).
  When the committed token is a far-tail position, w is near 1 and the term is near 0, whatever the model's
  dependence. At late probes the committed token is a content word (w near 0) and the term has full weight. So the
  early/late ratio is pushed below 1 by the position rule. The per-position averaging over image receivers does not
  change this, because every receiver has the same single source.
- Failure. H-A1 T->I is "weakly falsified" or "reversed". Stop rule 11.7 counts that as "Negative (on this
  checkpoint...)". Combined with a null H-B it would "drop P5 from the open problems", even though the model's
  dependence may be strongly front-loaded at the noun slot. Condition R does not rescue the decision: it is a reading
  companion with 64 trajectories (R2-M6), not the decision statistic. A secondary effect: DA is chosen by the
  committed-set T->I price, so it will select late windows (where content text is committed). DA's window choice and
  "front-loaded" (1.1) then point in opposite directions by construction, and 1.1's sentence links them.
- What forces rejection of this finding: on the 48 PG-V J trajectories, the committed text token at passes 2 and 6 is
  pad-class (p1 mass on pad or EOS >= 0.5) or after the caption end in fewer than 30 percent of trajectories.
- Minimal fix (cost-neutral, no extra passes): define the H-A1 (and H-A1-Q, Q2) T->I source **independently of the
  plan**. At each probe, use the still-masked text position with the highest max-marginal among non-pad-class positions
  in the completion region (state-time rule, no look-ahead): R = 8 draws from its marginal, one requery each. Keep the
  committed-set price only for DA and H-C. Log the class of every committed text token on the PG-V trajectories and
  report it with the D0 outputs. Remove from 1.1 any link between "concentrated early" and the DA windows.
  (An I->T analogue is weaker but real: its source is the 32 most confident image codes, plausibly background early.
  Report the source codes' mean marginal entropy per probe beside H-A1 I->T.)

### Major

**R2-M1. PG-V cannot distinguish co-generation consistency from agreement that the prefix alone produces, so it does not
show that the task has any completion-image dependence to measure.** Location: 4.4 (statistic, m - s >= 0.25), 14
risk 2.
- [C] `redteam_exp_001_pgv_commoncause.log`, synthetic setup with a perfect checker. Completion nouns and image objects
  are drawn *independently given the prefix*. With two open slots and a prefix-conditional top-candidate probability of
  0.6-0.7, m = 0.63-0.76 and the cross-prefix shuffled rate s = 0.002-0.008, so PG-V passes with probability 0.97-1.00.
  With one slot at 0.8 it passes with probability 0.99. The same-prefix shuffle equals m in every one of these rows (no
  gap). With true co-generation dependence (dep = 0.5) the gap opens: m = 0.85-0.91 against a same-prefix rate of
  0.41-0.63. The cross-prefix shuffle breaks the shared prefix, the common cause, so m - s measures prefix specificity,
  not image-completion coupling.
- Failure: PG-V passes. S_theta between the completion and the image sits near the floor (G1b may then stop H-A late,
  after D0-D2 have run), and H-B has no lever. Or it passes on prefix-driven nouns and is read as "depictive
  co-generated captions".
- Minimal fix: make the PG pool 24 prefixes x 2 seeds (same 48 samples). Add a same-prefix rate s_sp (each sample's
  completion nouns checked against the image of the other seed with the same prefix). Report m - s_sp with an interval
  as the evidence that the completion depends on its own image beyond the prefix. Keep the cross-prefix criteria as
  the construct check. If m - s_sp is not positive, log "no measurable co-generation dependence at this rung" with the
  gate outcomes (it does not need to stop the run, but it sets the H-A and H-B expectations).

**R2-M2. Debris after the completion is not handled. The card assumes the post-completion tail is pad, while the only
evidence says a finished caption is followed by debris.** Location: 3 items 1b-1c, 4.1 ("prefix + generated tokens
before the first `</s>` or pad"), 7.1, 7.2, 2 (H-A2 typing, pad-class rule), 4.4.
- [S] setup.md 2.1 (a): after a complete observed caption, the 32 free tail tokens were "(c) photoshiko shui copyright
  (c) 1 2 3 / 02 a / 12 3 / 15 a". Under the prefix task, once the completion ends (about 8-10 tokens) the remaining
  20-plus positions are in that situation. Consequences: (i) the scored caption includes the debris, so CLIP lift
  regains the watermark and style channel of X1-F1 on the debris part. (ii) The V-lift question 'Is this figure showing
  "{caption}"?' is asked about a caption that is mostly debris. (iii) Debris tokens are not pad-class, so they enter the
  I->T receivers and the H-A2 typing; debris nouns ("copyright", "stock") are stoplisted only in PG-V. (iv) PG-V's "at
  least one noun found" passes captions that are 70 percent debris.
- Minimal fix: a caption-end rule frozen before PG-V and applied to every endpoint, H-A2 typing and the exclusion rule.
  The caption ends at the first sentence terminator after the completion's first content token, or at the first
  `</s>`/pad, whichever comes first. Positions after the caption end are treated as pad-class for H-A. PG-V reports the
  debris share (non-pad tokens after the caption end) and adds a criterion, for example a median debris share below
  0.5. CLIP lift on the untruncated caption becomes a sensitivity.

**R2-M3. The dynamic-range gate is now dominated by the prefix and can no longer detect a dead endpoint. The MEI was set
on a different range.** Location: 6 (gate statistic = Lift(J) of the full caption), 2 (MEI rationale), 7.1.
- The observed prefix gives Lift(J) a component that no schedule changes, and that component alone is likely above 2.0
  points (prefixes differ across seeds, so the shuffled baseline falls). The gate therefore passes even when the
  completion carries no signal, and its P_rand fork never fires from this trigger. A schedule can move only the
  completion and the image's agreement with it. The MEI of 1.0 point comes from UniDisc's full-caption CFG-window
  differences, so it may exceed the range a schedule can move here.
- Minimal fix: make the gate statistic J's mean completion increment (Lift(full) - Lift(prefix-only)) on D0, threshold at
  least 1.0 point, logged with the other gates. Report that increment beside the MEI. If it is below 2 MEI, state that
  the primary is sized against a large share of the movable range.

**R2-M4. No positive control shows dependence at the completion level, only at the prefix level.** Location: 8.8 PC-D,
8.7 G1b.
- PC-D compares step-1 image marginals under the matched prefix with those under another item's prefix. That is
  prefix-level sensitivity, which the redesign makes large by construction. Nothing checks, before D1 and D2 are spent,
  that revealing the *open noun slot* moves the image marginals above the per-probe floor. If it does not, H-A is "not
  measurable" only after the profile runs.
- Minimal fix: PC-D', on the 16 pilot trajectories at passes 1-2. Reveal the noun-slot position (the first completion
  position) with R = 8 draws from its content-class marginal. Require the mixture estimate per image position to be at
  least 10 times G1b's floor at that probe. Failure moves H-A to "not measurable" before D1. Cost: about 16 x 2 x 8
  passes.

**R2-M5. TFJ separates "text earlier" only partly, and section 13 words a null DA - TFJ as if it settled the mechanism.**
Location: 5.1 (TFJ), 10.2 (H-B4), 13 row "H-B1 positive and DA - TFJ not positive", 14 risk 7.
- Per window at k0, J and TFJ both co-commit 128 text-image pairs (J: 1 x 32 per pass over 4 passes; TFJ: 4 x 32 at pass
  1), and DA none. So TFJ - J is "text earlier plus within-text density" at equal co-commitment, a good design. But DA
  - TFJ = (removing 128 co-committed pairs) - (image density 42-43 against 32 per pass). The two terms have opposite
  signs. A null DA - TFJ is therefore compatible with a real co-commitment benefit cancelled by the density cost. The
  section 13 row reads it as "showing the window's text earlier explains the gain; avoided co-commitment is not
  supported", which needs TFJ - J > 0 and a bounded density cost, neither of which is required.
- Minimal fix, a wording rule. "Text earlier explains the gain" only if TFJ - J has a lower 90 percent limit > 0 and DA -
  TFJ has an interval inside (-1, 1). If DA - TFJ is not positive and TFJ - J is not positive: "mechanism not
  separable: a co-commitment benefit and the image-density cost may cancel". Report Jd - J (density alone) beside it.

**R2-M6. At 64 trajectories, condition R is unlikely to deliver the sampler-versus-model separation it was promoted to
deliver.** Location: 2 (two-way reading), 6, 8.3, 14 risk 12.
- Under random positions, the T->I source is a uniformly random still-masked tail position. With about 8 completion
  positions in 32, roughly three in four probes draw a pad or debris source, so the content term is near 0 for them
  [I]. Effective informative trajectories per probe are then about 16. Both registered readings ("lower 90 percent limit
  > 1.0" for position-free wording, "upper 90 percent limit < 1.25" for "this sampler only") need precision that 16
  noisy values per probe do not give. "Not distinguishable" is the expected label, and the coordinator's question goes
  unanswered. Agreement between two position rules would also show robustness across two samplers, not
  sampler-freedom; the card's wording ("independent of the position rule tested") is correct and should stay that
  narrow.
- Minimal fix: with R2-F1's plan-independent source, every condition-R probe has a content source, which removes the
  dilution. State the expected precision of the condition-R ratio from D1's cv before the trace runs (as E4 does for
  D2). If that projection cannot reach the 1.25 or 1.0 bounds, either raise the trace to 128 trajectories (about 13,600
  passes more, about 0.5 compute-hours) or register "not distinguishable" as the expected outcome now.

### Minor

**R2-m1. Scope from the prefix rule.** Eligibility needs a second noun within 3-6 words. That selects
subject-verb-article captions ("A man riding a"), and the prefix always ends in a determiner or preposition. Claims hold
for that caption form. Report the eligible share of COCO val2017 and word the scope accordingly.

**R2-m2. The OR rule over nouns inflates PG-V's m and s together.** "At least one completion noun found" grows with
the noun count, and small VLMs have a known tendency to answer yes to presence questions. PC-F calibrates on real
captions with a different noun count. Report the per-noun presence rate (matched and shuffled) beside the OR-rule rate.

**R2-m3. The G1b fallback is not a no-information reveal for T->I.** When fewer than 6 positions with max marginal
>= 0.999 exist, G1b reveals positions with max >= 0.99 "of any modality". Revealing different image codes changes
neighbouring image marginals for real (within-image dependence), so the T->I floor is overstated and G1b can fail
spuriously. This becomes likely if the tail is debris rather than pad (R2-M2). Restrict the fallback to text-side
positions for T->I and image-side positions for I->T. Re-revealing the same value under a changed batch composition
would also serve.

**R2-m4. The completion increment mixes "more words" with "right words".** The prefix-only lift embeds a shorter text, so
the increment includes CLIP's length response. Paired arm contrasts cancel most of it, but R-b's per-arm reading does
not. State that R-b is read only as a contrast.

## Part 3. Answers to the brief's questions (short)
- **Does the prefix leave enough dependence?** Plausibly yes at the open noun slot, which is a real choice the image must
  agree with. The card does not measure it at that slot (R2-F1), PG-V cannot show it (R2-M1), and no positive control
  checks it (R2-M4). Whether the text becomes predictable from the prefix alone is what the same-prefix shuffle and
  PC-D' would show.
- **Can the tail be a new segment or debris?** Yes. The only evidence (setup 2.1 (a)) says a finished caption is followed
  by debris, and the card's pad assumption is unverified (R2-M2).
- **Is PG-V a valid construct gate?** For "captions name what is in the image": yes, with PC-F. For "the co-generated
  text depends on the image": no; it passes under conditional independence given the prefix with probability 0.97-1.00
  in the synthetic check (R2-M1).
- **Does TFJ separate text-earlier from avoided co-commitment?** TFJ - J isolates text-earlier at equal co-commitment.
  DA - TFJ still nets co-commitment against image density, so a null DA - TFJ cannot be read as "text earlier explains"
  (R2-M5).
- **Does condition R separate "front-loaded" from "this sampler commits dependent tokens early"?** In principle it shows
  robustness across two position rules. At 64 trajectories with pad-diluted sources it will most likely return "not
  distinguishable" (R2-M6). The structural sampler effect sits in the H-A1 source definition (R2-F1), which condition R
  does not touch.

(caption-end rule) and R2-M3 (gate on the completion increment) must be fixed before PG-V runs, because they change what
the gates measure. R2-M4 to M6 must be fixed before D1. The minors can go in the same version. No finding needs a
redesign or more than about 1 compute-hour.
