# Caption drafts, Probe 1 (run P2-PROBE-01)

All numbers below come from `runs/P2-PROBE-01/summary.json` (accuracy, ci95, n_true_greater, median_diff, n_items).
Figures are generated by `analysis/paper2/fig_probe1.py`. Status of every result: exploratory feasibility probe, E4 (run here once, one seed), not preregistered inference.

## F1 (figures/paper2/F1_probe1_accuracy.pdf)

**Figure X. Matching accuracy of UniDisc 1.4B on true versus deranged-mismatched COCO captions (exploratory feasibility probe).**
For each of 300 images the model scores the true caption and a caption drawn from a different image; accuracy is the share of images for which the true caption scores higher (chance 0.5, dashed line). Markers show accuracy; bars show 95% bootstrap intervals over images (10,000 resamples). Blue: the three primary scores, text direction a_text (0.730) and image direction b_image (0.783) with K = 8 complementary masks, and their sum a_plus_b (0.733). Orange cross: caption-only control, a_text with the whole image masked, which stays at chance (0.497, interval 0.440 to 0.553), so the 0.730 of a_text is not explained by caption fluency alone. Green: exploratory variants chosen after seeing the primary results (image-masked null subtracted, single full-mask pass, K = 2, sums); the best, a_full_pmi, reaches 0.990 (interval 0.977 to 1.000). Single run, one mismatch draw; intervals do not cover variation across draws or models. Definitions of each score are in the probe report.

## F2 (figures/paper2/F2_probe1_paired_diffs.pdf)

**Figure Y. Per-image paired score differences (true minus mismatched caption) for five scores (exploratory feasibility probe).**
Each point is one of 300 images; violin, box (quartiles, median as black bar, whiskers 1.5 interquartile range) and jittered points show the same differences. The dashed line marks zero; points above it are images where the true caption scores higher, and the share above zero is the accuracy printed under each panel title (a_text 0.730, 219 of 300; a_pmi 0.940, 282; a_full_pmi 0.990, 297; b_image 0.783, 235; b_full 0.843, 253). Blue: text direction, orange: image direction. Each panel has its own vertical scale because the image-direction margins are far smaller than the text-direction ones (median difference 0.0036 for b_image and 0.166 for b_full, against 0.587 for a_text, 0.572 for a_pmi and 2.382 for a_full_pmi); magnitudes are therefore not comparable across panels, only the position relative to zero. Scores are log-likelihood differences in nats, as defined in the probe report. Single run, no inference beyond the intervals in Figure X.
