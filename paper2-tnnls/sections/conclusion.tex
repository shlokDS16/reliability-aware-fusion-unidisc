\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
% run: runs/P2-GATE-M4/analysis.json:outcome.label (= negative); A4.2.3 NEG 6.1 opening
Study~1 measured a gate that withholds the image tokens flagged by UniDisc's own masked likelihood, with the flag threshold set by
conformal risk control on clean pairs.
The preregistered test excludes an improvement or a loss of more than five percentage points in image-direction verification accuracy
from gating under JPEG quality~10, Gaussian noise with $\sigma=0.1$, and a 128-pixel splice, for this model and these severities; it does
not show that gating has no effect.
% D0097: category-disjoint negatives
The result is for one pretrained model of 1.4B parameters with category-disjoint negatives (a random caption of an image sharing no
annotated COCO category).

% run: runs/P2-LRF-L1/a6_analysis.json:family_L.L1.{acc_b (= 0.7333), acc_a (= 0.8022), net (= 0.0689), ci95 (= [0.0289, 0.1089]), p_one (= 0.000508)}; family_L.L-LEN.label (= rejected)
% run: runs/P2-LRF-L1/a6_analysis.json:family_L.L-NI.{ci90 (= [0.0089, 0.0533]), non_inferior (= true)}; runs/P2-LRF-PLAN/n_rule.json:n_L (= 450)
Study~2 tested one frozen learned reliability-aware fusion of token evidence on a fresh preregistered fold.
Under Gaussian noise with $\sigma=0.1$ it raised image-direction verification accuracy from 0.733 without reliability weighting to 0.802
(net 0.069, 95\% interval 0.029 to 0.109; exact one-sided McNemar $p=0.0005$), including with length-matched negatives, and on clean
images it lost no more than three points (90\% interval of the net change 0.009 to 0.053); the margin was fixed after an exploratory
clean interval had been seen.
This holds for one model, on 450 COCO images not used to design or fit the method, at one severity; the weighting was trained on the
tested corruption, and no preregistered test shows that its gain is specific to that corruption.
