B. Audits

described as independent. No signature says that a method works.

F. Disclosures of Addendum A4

   (1) The five Tier-3 comparators and their parameters (S-II) were fixed in Addendum A4, whose draft (40828e96) and
revision r1 (f1671c91) were committed before the headroom decision file of the main run was written; the addendum's
author states that no value of the headroom or primary stage was read when it was written, which is an assertion and
not an attestation. (2) No p-value is computed for the Tier-3 comparators; there are 72 intervals and no multiplicity
adjustment. (3) The outcome book of Addendum A4 fixed the wording of every label before the primary result existed.
On 29 October 2026 at about 00:10 IST the first author delegated the choice of how the submission condition reads
under a null primary result; under that delegation the stricter reading (no question-form framing in the title, abstract,
highlights, conclusion or the opening of the Study 1 discussion) was adopted by the agent-run study pipeline (see the
Declaration) at 00:12 IST, before any value of the main test was read (decision records D0076 and D0077; D0077
corrects D0076's wording: the author's message delegated the choice and did not itself choose a reading). (4) The
time-input statement rests on a code reading at the pinned commit (above) and on the key check. (5) Family B ran
before the re-infilling family S2, which the plan allows when a fixed time inequality holds at the end of the coherence
stage; it held. A code reading confirmed that no output of family B can reach an input of S2; the rule that B is analysed
only after the S2 table exists is procedural, and it was followed (analysis commit 10e6bc0f).

D. Pixel denoising (S-DN)

   The denoiser is OpenCV non-local means with one setting for all corruptions; on 20 images it raises the PSNR of
the noisy image from 20.41 to 25.75 dB and lowers that of JPEG quality 10 from 26.82 to 25.13 dB. Under noise
the denoised copy gave higher verification accuracy than GATE-M: 0.852 against 0.676 (GATE-M minus denoised
-0.176, 95 % interval -0.224 to -0.130; pHolm = 1.5 × 10-12). Under JPEG no difference was detected (0.844 against
0.836; -0.008, -0.042 to 0.026; pHolm = 0.74); at this setting the denoiser harms JPEG images, so this row compares
GATE-M with a defence that lowers image quality.

E. Decomposition (E-dec) and sign agreement (E-pred), descriptive

   E-dec splits the accuracy lost to corruption into the residual loss under the changed-code (oracle) gate, the recovery
by GATE-M and the gap to the oracle gate (Table S10); its parts are relative to the changed-code label, which marks
encoder instability and is not an evidence oracle. E-pred compares the committed sign of the independent-token
signal-to-noise change with the observed sign of b - c (Table S11): agreement in 2 of 2 evaluable primary cells (95 %
interval 0.158 to 1.000), the noise cell excluded for having no observed sign, and in 0 of 1 positive-control cells; the rival
rule "always +" agreed in 0 of 2. No cell had an observed interval that excludes zero, so the plan's refutation rule was
not met. On the evidence ratio, which GATE-M raised, the committed sign was negative; this disagreement is reported
and not read either way. The correlated-token proposition of the main paper is not used to interpret E-pred.

F. Coherence of the denoiser across mask patterns

   On 100 clean calibration items (800 gaps) the median absolute order gap of the joint log-likelihood was 0.071 nats,
above the numerical noise floor (95th percentile of repeat differences 0.000) and below the clean mean per-token evidence
µ = 0.199, so neither pre-stated reading applies; by pair type (descriptive) the median gap was 0.253 for horizontally
adjacent, 0.246 for vertically adjacent and 0.032 for distant tokens.

H. Re-infilling (S2): detail

   In the flagged-set arm the four draws share one position set, while in the random-set arm each draw has its own

                                          S-VII. Study 2: Exploratory Ablation (Tier 3)

   Label. Exploratory; added after the primary, family H and Study 2 results. No ablation was preregistered. The plan
(research/paper2/tnnls/ABLATION-PLAN-2026-10-30.md) was written and checked by the statistician, and the variant
list was committed before any variant was fitted or scored. Disclosure. When the plan was written, its author had seen
the fresh-fold results of the frozen LRF and of the unweighted rule, an exploratory cross-fit of four learned-weighting
families on the design items, and the frozen LRF's shape functions (main paper, Fig. 4); the variant list was fixed after
the fresh LRF and unweighted results had been seen, and no variant was fitted or scored before the list was committed.
Every listed variant is reported; no variant was selected and scored again, and the tested object of Study 2 stays the
frozen LRF.

      Variants: All fitted variants use LRF's protocol with the stated change: the same objective, optimiser, nine-
configuration search with successive halving, three inner folds over the 500 design items, selection by the inner-fold
objective, and one final seed. A-LIN is a linear logit on the 35 features (no hinge terms). A-S-BD, A-S-s3, A-S-tbv,
A-S-ltex, A-S-nbr and A-S-pos are the same additive model on the features computed from one source only: B and D
(14 features), s3 (5), tbv (5), ltex (4), nbr (4) and grid position (3). A-SF is a single-feature weight (feature rad, chosen
from 35 candidate features by the inner-fold objective on the design items, a selection budget 35 times that of the
frozen LRF; the selection used no fresh item). A-MIL is attention-weighted pooling in the style of gated attention MIL
(Ilse et al., 2018; cited in the main paper) over the same 35 features, fitted with the same objective, design items and
tuning budget; the evidence j takes the place of the instance classifier. A-UNL is an unlearned rank weight on s3,
wj = 1 - rank(s3,j); the direction and the rank transform were fixed by the plan's author, who had seen the frozen
LRF's shape functions; they were not read from an accuracy. The knockouts evaluate the frozen LRF with one group
set to its design-item mean: by source (KO-S-BD, KO-S-s3, KO-S-tbv, KO-S-ltex, KO-S-nbr, KO-S-pos) and by formula
type (KO-F-token, KO-F-position, KO-F-neighbourhood, KO-F-rank, KO-F-item). Setting a group to its mean gives
inputs that the model did not see together in fitting; an interval away from zero shows that the frozen model's decisions
depend on those inputs, not that the group alone detects anything. KO-S-pos and KO-F-position act on the same three
features, so their rows are identical.

      Intervals: Each interval is a marginal 95 % interval. 336 intervals were computed from the same images (variants ×
cells × two contrasts); they are positively dependent and no familywise or false-discovery adjustment was made. If
every true difference were zero, about 5 % of them (about 17) would still exclude zero, and the count of intervals that
exclude zero is not a calibrated tally because of the dependence. An interval that excludes zero is therefore a descriptive
observation, not a finding; patterns across rows and cells are described, single intervals are not claims. The intervals
cover item sampling only: refit-seed variation and design-item sampling are not in them. Intervals are paired percentile
bootstrap intervals with 10 000 item resamples, one shared index matrix per cell; differences are variant minus reference,
so a positive value favours the variant, and an interval that contains zero does not show that the difference is zero. The
300 images of the LG cells are the first 300 images of the L1 cells, so the eight cells are not independent samples; the
cells have 450 (L1) and 300 (LG) items.

      Controls: Recomputed from the stored arrays, the unweighted rule and the frozen LRF reproduced the decisions of
the preregistered analysis on all 3 000 item-cells (0 mismatches each), and the knockout of all 35 features (KO-ALL,
an exact-equality control with no interval) reproduced the unweighted rule on every item-cell. An LRF refit by the
ablation harness at seed 0 decided every fresh item as the frozen LRF did.

      Results: Tables S21 and S22 give every variant against the frozen LRF and Table S23 against the unweighted
rule. Under Gaussian noise (L1) the intervals against LRF contained zero for A-LIN, A-S-BD, A-S-s3 and A-MIL,
                                                                                                                                                   13

and lay below zero for A-S-tbv, A-S-ltex, A-S-nbr, A-S-pos, A-SF and A-UNL; among knockouts, KO-S-BD and
KO-F-neighbourhood lay below zero and KO-S-nbr above zero, and every other knockout interval contained zero. In
other cells, intervals against LRF that lay above zero were those of A-LIN under JPEG (0.020 [0.004, 0.038]), A-S-BD
under impulse noise (0.043 [0.003, 0.083]) and KO-S-s3 under JPEG (0.011 [0.002, 0.022]); this comparison was added
