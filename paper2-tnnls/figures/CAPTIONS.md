## Fig. 1 `figures/fig1_measurement.pdf` (two-column, 7.16 in)  -- label `fig:measurement`
**Fig. 1.** Image-text verification by per-token evidence in a masked diffusion model (conceptual drawing; no data). An image, which may be damaged by noise, JPEG compression or a spliced region before it is tokenised, is converted to image tokens (reddish purple: tokens whose code is changed by the damage). With the image tokens masked and each candidate caption visible, the model gives a per-token evidence $\Delta_j$, the log-probability ratio of the observed image token under the true and under a wrong caption (blue: $\Delta_j>0$; orange: $\Delta_j<0$). The evidence is summed without weights, $S=\sum_j\Delta_j$, and the true caption is chosen if $S>0$. The token pattern is a fixed schematic, not a measurement. Source: script `paper2-tnnls/scripts/fig1_measurement.py`; definitions in Section III.

## Fig. 2 `figures/fig2_theory.pdf` (column width, 3.5 in)  -- label `fig:frontier`
**Fig. 2.** Model figure, no data: when withholding tokens raises the signal-to-noise ratio of the evidence sum under correlated tokens (theory under stated assumptions, not validated on UniDisc). For a clean-token withholding probability $\phi_0$ and a corrupted-token withholding probability $\phi_1$, with $\mu_c=0$ and $\gamma_{01}=0$, Proposition (`prop:snrcorr`) gives the curves $\phi_1=b(\phi_0;G,\chi)$ above which withholding raises $\mathbb{E}S/\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}\,S}$ (shaded; the shade deepens where more curves lie below). Curves: $G=(\rho m-1)\gamma_1=0$ (independent tokens, Eq. (snr)), 1, 4 and 16, drawn for the display value $\chi=\rho\sigma_c^2/[(1-\rho)(\sigma^2+\mu^2)]=1$; a larger $G$ lowers the curve toward the diagonal $\phi_1=\phi_0$ (random withholding). Grey region: every rule with $\phi_1\le\phi_0$ lowers the ratio when $\gamma_{01}\ge0$. A curve ends where it reaches $\phi_1=1$ or has no root. The curves were checked against the ratio computed directly from Eqs. (mean) and (varcorr) on a grid before drawing (0 mismatches in 6 730 points; script output). The ratio is that of the sum; accuracy follows from it only for jointly Gaussian terms. Source: `research/paper2/method/THEORY-ADDENDUM-2026-10-29.md` R1.4 and R1.8; script `paper2-tnnls/scripts/fig2_theory.py`.

## NEW Fig. `figures/fig_gate_method.pdf` (two-column, 7.16 x 2.24 in)  -- label `fig:gate-method` (Study 1 Method)
**Fig. 2.** Study 1: masked-likelihood token gating with a threshold from conformal risk control. (a) Schematic (no data; the token patterns are a fixed drawing). On $n_{\rm cal}=300$ clean calibration pairs, disjoint by image from every other fold, the suspicion $u_{ij}=D_{ij}-B_{ij}$ of every image token gives the false-alarm proportion $L_i(\tau)$, the share of tokens with $u_{ij}>\tau$, and conformal risk control sets $\hat\tau$ at level $\alpha$ (\cref{eq:crc}) before any test item is seen. On a test pair, token $j$ is kept when $u_j\le\hat\tau$ ($I_j=1$); flagged tokens ($I_j=0$) are withheld by masking, which removes their evidence $\Delta_j$ (Fig. 1) from the sum $S_{\rm G}=\sum_jI_j\Delta_j$, and the true caption is chosen if $S_{\rm G}>0$. The only guarantee is that of \cref{prop:crc}: for a clean pair exchangeable with the calibration pairs, the expected flagged share is at most $\alpha$. (b) Realised flagged share of the 500 clean test pairs against the nominal level $\alpha\in\{0.01,0.05,0.10\}$ (dots) with the line share $=\alpha$ (dashed); the shaded region is share $\le\alpha$. (c) The same as share minus $\alpha$, with the 95\,\% percentile-bootstrap interval over test items (10\,000 resamples; at $\alpha=0.01$ the interval is narrower than the marker); open marker, offset to the right of $\alpha=0.05$: exploratory re-split replication (calibration and clean test pairs pooled and re-split 2\,000 times, $\tau$ refitted on each calibration part; mean and 2.5--97.5\,\% range of share minus $\alpha$ on the held-out parts; descriptive, splits overlap). At every level the calibration statistic is below the violation bound, so there is no evidence of violation of \cref{prop:crc}; the check covers clean pairs only, not the unchanged tokens of corrupted items. Source: `runs/P2-GATE-M4/analysis.json`, keys `calibration.fap_check.per_alpha.{0.01,0.05,0.1}.{F,ci_bootstrap,n_test,Z_cal,violation}`, `calibration.fap_check.critical_value`, `calibration.split_replication.*`; `runs/P2-GATE-M2/threshold.json` (`n_cal`); plotted values in `runs/P2-PAPER-FIGS/fig_gate_method.json`; script `paper2-tnnls/scripts/fig_gate_method.py`.
*Placement:* Section IV-A (Method), `figure*` at the top of the page after `prop:crc`; refer to (b)-(c) in IV-D "Calibration and the clean control". Nothing of family P, PS, detection or H is on this canvas (A4.2.5 items 3-4). Symbols: Study 1 only ($u_j$, $\hat\tau$, $\alpha$, $I_j$, $S_{\rm G}$, $\Delta_j$ from Section III).
