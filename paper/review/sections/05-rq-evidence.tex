\section{What the evidence shows, by research question}
\label{sec:rq}

This section answers the six research questions from the 615 included studies. Two kinds of number appear. Counts over all included studies come from fields the full-text screeners recorded (Section~\ref{sec:method-extraction}). Statements about how studies ran their comparisons, and the quality appraisal, come from the stratified sample of 81 studies; they are given as the number of sampled studies and as a share weighted to the size of each stratum, with a 95\% interval. Every count describes the included studies, which lean towards recent work and leave out the seed corpus (Section~\ref{sec:limits}); none describes the field as a whole. % src: analysis/rq1/rq1-counts.json; analysis/extraction/d0052/sample.json; gate-manifest.json frame sizes

\subsection{RQ1: which formulations exist}
\label{sec:rq1}

Of the 615 included studies, 559 qualify through a diffusion model whose output includes text or code (criterion I1(a)), 41 through a unified model that understands and generates across text and another modality (I1(b)), and 15 through both. In 12 of the I1(a) studies no reported result is text: the model generates only non-language strings, and these studies are counted apart. % src: analysis/rq1/rq1-counts.json i1 {"a": 559, "b": 41, "ab": 15}, nonlang_only 12
Both screeners recorded the study's modality as text only for 410 studies and as involving image, video, audio, speech or 3D for 164; 28 involve other non-text inputs or outputs and 13 were recorded differently by the two screeners. % src: rq1-counts.json mm (text 410, multimodal 164, other 28; splits 3 + 2 + 2 + 6 = 13)
The included studies are recent: 449 are dated 2026 and 111 are dated 2025, against 55 for 2021 to 2024 together. Unified I1(b) or I1(a)-and-(b) studies number 34 in 2026, 12 in 2025 and 10 earlier. % src: rq1-counts.json year {2021: 3, 2022: 5, 2023: 13, 2024: 34, 2025: 111, 2026: 449}; i1_by_year (2026 b 25 + ab 9 = 34; 2025 b 8 + ab 4 = 12; earlier b 1 + ab 2 + b 7 = 10)
The screeners also marked which research questions each study carries evidence for, by pointing to a results table or figure. Both screeners marked an autoregressive baseline on the same task in the study's own results (RQ2) for 246 studies, a within-paper contrast of a training choice (RQ3) for 237, a within-paper contrast of an inference setting (RQ4) for 394, and, among unified studies, a synergy ablation, benchmark or claim (RQ5) for 32. % src: rq1-counts.json rq_both {"RQ2": 246, "RQ3": 237, "RQ4": 394, "RQ5": 32}; criteria-v1.6-screener.md "RQ TAGS"
