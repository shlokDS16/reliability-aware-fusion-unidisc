\section{A targeted measurement on a frozen unified model}
\label{sec:exp003}

The census (Section~\ref{sec:census}) counts how often the value rule behind a headline result is not plain or not stated. It does not show that the rule changes any number. This section measures one number on one model in a preregistered run. It is a single run on one checkpoint, it serves the reading of the census in Section~\ref{sec:census-reading}, and it is not a contribution of the review. That the sampler changes joint text--image agreement in unified masked-diffusion models is already reported~\cite{arXiv:2607.13188,arXiv:2511.09611,arXiv:2503.20853}. Our scope is narrower: matched network calls on frozen released weights, guidance separated from the rest of the sampler, and a statistic that removes what the prefix alone explains. % src: C section 1.3; N sections 1, 4 and 5; decision D0019

\subsection{Question and design}
\label{sec:exp003-design}

The question is whether a 32-step variant of the official guided sampler of UniDisc 1.4B~\cite{arXiv:2503.20853} (guidance weight 3.0; the shipped default is 50 steps) gives a larger seed-specific, noun-level dependence between a generated caption tail and its image than plain sampling at the same number of network calls. A second question is how much of the dependence remains when guidance is switched off. Every statement below is scoped to UniDisc 1.4B, at 512 px, after prefixes of 5 to 9 words from COCO val2017 captions, with 32 masked tail tokens, guidance weight 3.0 (and 0) at 32 steps, and the Qwen3-VL-2B presence checker. A preregistration card (v0.3.2), reviewed by two Claude agent roles, a statistician and a red team, fixed the analysis before the run; no human review of it is recorded. % src: C sections 1.1, 3 and 11

Four arms see the same 138 fresh prefixes, two seeds per prefix: plain sampling at 64 and 32 passes (P64, P32) and the official sampler at 32 steps with guidance weight 0 and 3.0 (O0, G); G and P64 both cost 64 network calls. The statistic is a seed-balanced link $\Lambda_{\mathrm b}$: how much more often a noun that one seed's completion names, and the other's does not, is found by the checker in its own image than in the sibling image. The primary test is $\Delta_1=\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(G)-\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(P64)$ against zero, with the prefix as the cluster; the secondary contrasts separate guidance ($\Delta_{2a}$, G against O0) from the rest of the official sampler ($\Delta_{2b}$, O0 against P32). Arms, statistic, controls and secondary results are given in full in the supplement (Section~S7.5). % src: C sections 0, 2, 4 and 5; n_conf.json (138 prefixes); moved text in supplement.tex S7.5

\paragraph{Primary result.}
Under a 32-step variant of the official guided sampler (guidance weight 3.0; the shipped default is 50 steps), UniDisc 1.4B shows higher VLM noun presence in the own image than in the sibling image, not confirmed by CLIP, for nouns that one seed's completion names and the other's does not, and this difference is larger than under plain sampling at the same number of network calls (64): $\Delta_1=0.126$ (95\% interval 0.076 to 0.177; BCa interval 0.075 to 0.176; $p=0.0002$, the smallest value 10,000 replicates can return; 123 prefixes). The difference may include quality-mediated, class-level coupling. The point estimate is at or above the minimum effect of interest of 0.10; its lower limit is 0.076, so we do not state that the effect is at least 0.10. Computed after the freeze, the achieved minimum detectable effect is 0.084 at power 0.90 (0.119 was planned) and the power at 0.10 is 0.97 (Wald approximation). % src: F confirmatory.delta1 (d, pct95, bca95, p, n_prefix); A section 5 (primary sentence; W0 clause kept per decision D0025 (2)); X item2_achieved_mde_and_power (delta1)

\paragraph{Secondary results and checks.}
The official sampler's bundle of selection, pooled ordering and schedule raises the link ($\Delta_{2b}=0.077$, 95\% interval 0.024 to 0.131); a guidance effect is not detected ($\Delta_{2a}=0.023$, $-0.032$ to 0.078) and, because the positive control of the checker failed, cannot be read as a null. CLIP did not confirm the dependence. No statement is made that plain sampling shows no link or that guidance lowers diversity (supplement, Section~S7.5). % src: F confirmatory.delta2a, delta2b; runs/EXP-003/ctrl/controls_decision.json; A sections 4.1, 4.2, 5 and 6

\paragraph{Deviations.}\label{sec:exp003-deviations}
Six deviations from the preregistered plan (a raised GPU-time limit and five items computed after the freeze) are listed in the supplement (Section~S7.1); the gate audit found none that a main-stage outcome could have influenced. Its analysis outputs are archived with the review's data; generated images are withheld because UniDisc states no licence.

\subsection{What this means for the review}
\label{sec:exp003-meaning}

On this model, at equal network calls and equal weights, the link between a completion and its image depends on the sampler: 0.180 for the guided variant and 0.051 for plain sampling (each over its own eligible prefixes; on the 123 prefixes eligible in both arms the difference is 0.126), a difference that is not confirmed by CLIP and may include quality-mediated coupling. The official sampler's selection, ordering and schedule contribute a positive term; a guidance term is not detected but cannot be excluded. A joint-consistency number reported without its sampler is therefore not attributable to the model. The sampler's effect on joint agreement is known~\cite{arXiv:2607.13188,arXiv:2511.09611}; the measurement adds one controlled instance at matched calls. It does not show that the value rule matters in other models, metrics or prefixes, or that it carries joint consistency, since the positive term bundles ordering and schedule with selection. Splitting O0 into a selection-only and an ordering-only variant at matched calls would attribute the bundle term; we have not run it. The guided arms supply a regime in which the statistic is above zero, which bears on the open problem of Section~\ref{sec:open-op3}, with the caveat that the instrument's sensitivity there is untested. % src: F Table~\ref{tab:exp003-arms}; N section 4 item 6; decision D0019; A (competing_explanations, next_test); framework-v2.md section C (P5a)
