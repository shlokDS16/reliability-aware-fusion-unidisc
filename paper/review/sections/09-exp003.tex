\section{A targeted measurement on a frozen unified model}
\label{sec:exp003}

The census (Section~\ref{sec:census}) counts how often the value rule behind a headline result is not plain or not stated. It does not show that the rule changes any number. This section measures one number on one model in a preregistered run. It is a single run on one checkpoint, it serves the reading of the census in Section~\ref{sec:census-reading}, and it is not a contribution of the review. That the sampler changes joint text--image agreement in unified masked-diffusion models is already reported~\cite{arXiv:2607.13188,arXiv:2511.09611,arXiv:2503.20853}. Our scope is narrower: matched network calls on frozen released weights, guidance separated from the rest of the sampler, and a statistic that removes what the prefix alone explains. % src: C section 1.3; N sections 1, 4 and 5; decision D0019

\subsection{Question and design}
\label{sec:exp003-design}

The question is whether a 32-step variant of the official guided sampler of UniDisc 1.4B~\cite{arXiv:2503.20853} (guidance weight 3.0; the shipped default is 50 steps) gives a larger seed-specific, noun-level dependence between a generated caption tail and its image than plain sampling at the same number of network calls. A second question is how much of the dependence remains when guidance is switched off. Every statement below is scoped to UniDisc 1.4B, at 512 px, after prefixes of 5 to 9 words from COCO val2017 captions, with 32 masked tail tokens, guidance weight 3.0 (and 0) at 32 steps, and the Qwen3-VL-2B presence checker. A preregistration card (v0.3.2), reviewed by two Claude agent roles, a statistician and a red team, fixed the analysis before the run; no human review of it is recorded. % src: C sections 1.1, 3 and 11

\paragraph{Arms.}
Four arms see the same 138 fresh prefixes, two seeds per prefix. P64 and P32 are plain sampling at temperature one with within-modality confidence ranking, at 64 and 32 passes. O0 and G are the official sampler at 32 steps with guidance weight 0 and 3.0. As we read the released code, it ranks positions across both modalities by the log-probability of the drawn token plus Gumbel noise, commits by an arccos schedule and ends with an argmax pass. Guidance adds a network call per step, so G and P64 cost 64 calls, O0 33 and P32 32. The primary contrast, G against P64, is matched on calls and favours the plain sampler, which has the finer schedule. G against O0 changes guidance alone; O0 against P32 bundles selection on the drawn value, pooled ordering and schedule, and so makes no value-rule claim. % src: C sections 0 and 2; n_conf.json (138 generated prefixes)

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each prefix, two seeds give two completions and two images. The seed-balanced link $\Lambda_{\mathrm b}$ of an arm is the mean, over prefixes where each seed's completion names a noun the other's does not, of how much more often such a noun is found by the checker in its own image than in the sibling image; what the prefix explains, and an additive yes-bias of either image, cancel. The contrasts are $\Delta_1=\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(G)-\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(P64)$, $\Delta_{2a}=\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(G)-\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(O0)$ and $\Delta_{2b}=\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(O0)-\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(P32)$; the formulas, the telescoping identity that links them and the prior work the baseline follows are in the supplement (Section~S7.4). % src: C sections 4.1 and 4.2 (moved text in supplement.tex S7.4)

\paragraph{Inference and controls.}
The prefix is the cluster (percentile bootstrap, 10,000 replicates). The one primary test is $\Delta_1$ against zero, two-sided at 0.05, with Holm's procedure for $\Delta_{2a}$ and $\Delta_{2b}$. Four controls fix the wording; if the positive control PC-1 fails, every null label becomes ``not measurable with this instrument'' (rule W2) and a positive stays readable (supplement, Section~S7.4). % src: C sections 4.5, 5, 6 and 7

\paragraph{Primary result.}
Under a 32-step variant of the official guided sampler (guidance weight 3.0; the shipped default is 50 steps), UniDisc 1.4B shows higher VLM noun presence in the own image than in the sibling image, not confirmed by CLIP, for nouns that one seed's completion names and the other's does not, and this difference is larger than under plain sampling at the same number of network calls (64): $\Delta_1=0.126$ (95\% interval 0.076 to 0.177; BCa interval 0.075 to 0.176; $p=0.0002$, the smallest value 10,000 replicates can return; 123 prefixes). The difference may include quality-mediated, class-level coupling. The point estimate is at or above the minimum effect of interest of 0.10; its lower limit is 0.076, so we do not state that the effect is at least 0.10. Computed after the freeze, the achieved minimum detectable effect is 0.084 at power 0.90 (0.119 was planned) and the power at 0.10 is 0.97 (Wald approximation). % src: F confirmatory.delta1 (d, pct95, bca95, p, n_prefix); A section 5 (primary sentence; W0 clause kept per decision D0025 (2)); X item2_achieved_mde_and_power (delta1)

\paragraph{Second result.}
The official sampler without guidance minus plain sampling at 32 steps is positive: $\Delta_{2b}=0.077$ (95\% interval 0.024 to 0.131; Holm-adjusted $p=0.012$; 127 prefixes). The official sampler's bundle of selection, pooled ordering and schedule raises the link. A guidance effect is not detected: $\Delta_{2a}=0.023$ (95\% interval $-0.032$ to 0.078; Holm-adjusted $p=0.43$; 122 prefixes). Because PC-1 failed, this interval is not read as a null: it is not measurable with this instrument. On the 118 prefixes eligible in all four arms, the identity $\Delta_1=\Delta_{2a}+\Delta_{2b}+[\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(P32)-\Lambda_{\mathrm b}(P64)]$ reads $0.126 = 0.026 + 0.072 + 0.029$ (rounded to three decimals; the unrounded terms sum exactly), and we state no share of the effect. Descriptive and uncorrected, G minus P32 is 0.094 (0.042 to 0.146; 125 prefixes) and P64 minus P32 is $-0.033$ ($-0.076$ to 0.009; 132 prefixes); we draw no conclusion from the second. % src: F confirmatory.delta2a, delta2b, identity, steps_matched_G_minus_P32, plain_doubling_P64_minus_P32; A sections 4.2 and 5

\paragraph{Checks and controls.}
CLIP did not confirm the dependence: the sibling-link contrast of G against P64 on the completion text alone has a 90\% interval of $-0.085$ to 0.861, where the rule requires a lower limit above zero. The preregistered flag for quality-mediated coupling is not triggered, which does not exclude it, and the composition sensitivity cannot be computed. NC-1, NC-2 and PC-2 passed; PC-1 failed (0.031, 95\% interval $-0.042$ to 0.100, where 0.10 was required), so we cannot say that the checker registers a link even when the image sees the whole completion. We make no statement that plain sampling shows no link. The full paragraph, with every value, is in the supplement (Section~S7.4). % src: F clip_sibling_link_G_minus_P64_completion_only, W1_concordant; runs/EXP-003/ctrl/controls_decision.json; A sections 4.1 and 6 item 9; decision D0025 (5)

\paragraph{Diversity.}
The hypothesis that the guided arm lowers diversity (E3-H4) was not tested by the frozen analysis. An exploratory computation made after the freeze is in the supplement (Section~S7.2); we make no statement that guidance lowers diversity.

\subsection{What this means for the review}
\label{sec:exp003-meaning}

On this model, at equal network calls and equal weights, the link between a completion and its image depends on the sampler: 0.180 for the guided variant and 0.051 for plain sampling (each over its own eligible prefixes; on the 123 prefixes eligible in both arms the difference is 0.126), a difference that is not confirmed by CLIP and may include quality-mediated coupling. The official sampler's selection, ordering and schedule contribute a positive term; a guidance term is not detected but cannot be excluded. A joint-consistency number reported without its sampler is therefore not attributable to the model. Other metrics and model families depend on sampling or evaluation settings~\cite{openreview:Gq4yt8pxRo,arXiv:2606.29228,arXiv:2606.20536,arXiv:2607.24763}, and the sampler's effect on joint agreement is known~\cite{arXiv:2607.13188,arXiv:2511.09611}; the measurement adds one controlled instance at matched calls. It does not show that the value rule matters in other models, metrics or prefixes, or that it carries joint consistency, since the positive term bundles ordering and schedule with selection. Splitting O0 into a selection-only and an ordering-only variant at matched calls would attribute the bundle term; we have not run it. The guided arms supply a regime in which the statistic is above zero, which bears on the open problem of Section~\ref{sec:open-op3}, with the caveat that the instrument's sensitivity there is untested. % src: F Table~\ref{tab:exp003-arms}; N section 4 item 6; decision D0019; A (competing_explanations, next_test); framework-v2.md section C (P5a)
