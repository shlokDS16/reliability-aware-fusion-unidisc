\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We reviewed diffusion language models and unified multimodal diffusion models under PRISMA 2020, with the departures and limits stated in Sections~\ref{sec:method-deviations} and~\ref{sec:limits}. Besides the evidence by research question, the review makes four contributions. One notation, a commit kernel with five design choices, places 47 models, with 11 fitting only partly (Section~\ref{sec:design}). A census of a random sample finds that the value rule behind the headline result is either not plain sampling or not stated for 147 of 153 codable papers\aud (Section~\ref{sec:census}). The error budget gives a place to put the value rule among the other terms and is bookkeeping, not a measurement (Section~\ref{sec:budget}). The open problems each name the evidence that would settle them (Section~\ref{sec:open}). % src: design-space-v1.csv; census-result-v2.md ("Result"); framework-v2.md sections B and D

The evidence by research question is thin where the field's strongest claims are made (Section~\ref{sec:rq}). In the stratified sample, 35 of 79 studies compare a diffusion model with an autoregressive one, none at a budget matched in every comparison, and the direction is split, so whether diffusion language models are better or worse than autoregressive ones is untested at matched compute. Gains credited to training recipes point the proposers' way but are rarely isolated or repeated. More inference compute usually improves quality, yet in 10 of the 19 studies with a sweep at least one curve stops improving or falls inside the tested range. Synergy between understanding and generation is claimed by 3 of 20 unified studies and measured on no benchmark built for it. The main comparison is repeated over runs in 11 of 74 studies and given an interval or test in 10. % src: sections/05-rq-evidence.tex (rq-results.json RQ2-RQ5; tables/qa-summary.tex QA4, QA5)

The most practical result is about reporting. Value rules are rarely plain and often unstated, and one preregistered run on a frozen unified model shows that the choice can matter: at equal network calls and equal weights, the guided variant of the official sampler gives a seed-balanced text--image link of 0.180 and plain sampling 0.051, a difference of 0.126 on the prefixes eligible in both arms (Section~\ref{sec:exp003}). That difference is not confirmed by CLIP and may include quality-mediated coupling. The part due to the official sampler's selection, ordering and schedule is positive, the part due to guidance is not detected, and the control that tests the instrument failed, so the run supports neither a null nor a claim about the value rule alone. % src: runs/EXP-003/analysis/confirmatory.json (arms.G, arms.P64, delta2a, delta2b); gate audit 2026-10-15-gate-EXP-003.md section 5

Three pieces of work would change these conclusions. A human-coded replicate of the census, extended from the sample to all included records, would test the reporting count. A repeat of the measurement on other prefixes, another checker and another model would test whether it generalises, and a split of the official sampler into its selection and ordering parts would say where the bundle term comes from. Until then, a number from a diffusion generator is best read together with the position rule, the value rule and the guidance level that produced it. % src: Section~\ref{sec:open-reporting}; Section~\ref{sec:exp003-meaning}
