\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We reviewed diffusion language models and unified multimodal diffusion models under PRISMA 2020, with the departures and limits stated in Sections~\ref{sec:method-deviations} and~\ref{sec:limits}. Besides the evidence by research question, the review makes four contributions. One notation, a commit kernel with five design choices, places 47 models, with 11 fitting only partly (Section~\ref{sec:design}). A census of a random sample finds that the value rule behind the headline result is either not plain sampling or not stated for 147 of 153 codable papers (Section~\ref{sec:census}). The error budget gives a place to put the value rule among the other terms and is bookkeeping, not a measurement (Section~\ref{sec:budget}). The open problems each name the evidence that would settle them (Section~\ref{sec:open}). % src: design-space-v1.csv; census-result-v2.md ("Result"); framework-v2.md sections B and D

The evidence for the research questions is weakest where the field's strongest claims are made (Section~\ref{sec:rq}). Of 59 sampled included studies, 25 compare diffusion with autoregressive models, one at a matched budget in at least one comparison, with split results (mixed). Anchor training configurations win most contrasts that few isolate (weakly supported); more inference compute improves most curves in 9 of 15 sweeping studies, while 7 show one that peaks or reverses (mixed); and the joint model wins in two of five signed synergy ablations, with no purpose-built benchmark (mixed). The main comparison is repeated over runs in 10 of 54 included studies and given an interval or test in 9. % src: sections/05-rq-evidence.tex (rq-results.json D0060 included: RQ2_has_ar 25/59, RQ2_matched_any 1/25; RQ4_sweep_up_majority 9/15, RQ4_any_nonmonotone 7/15; RQ5_joint_better_majority 2/5, worse 3/5; tables/qa-summary.tex QA4 10, QA5 9 of 54)

The most practical result is about reporting. Value rules are rarely plain and often unstated. One preregistered run on a frozen unified model shows that the choice can matter. At equal network calls and equal weights, the guided variant of the official sampler gives a seed-balanced text--image link of 0.180 and plain sampling 0.051, a difference of 0.126 on the prefixes eligible in both arms (Section~\ref{sec:exp003}). That difference is not confirmed by CLIP, may include quality-mediated coupling, and, with guidance not detected and the instrument's control failed, supports no claim about the value rule alone. % src: runs/EXP-003/analysis/confirmatory.json (arms.G, arms.P64, delta2a, delta2b); gate audit 2026-10-15-gate-EXP-003.md section 5

A human-coded census of all included records, a repeat of the measurement on other prefixes, checkers and models, and a split of the official sampler into its selection and ordering parts would each test these conclusions. Until then, a number from a diffusion generator is best read together with the position rule, the value rule and the guidance level that produced it. % src: Section~\ref{sec:open-reporting}; Section~\ref{sec:exp003} (full reading in supplement S7.6)
