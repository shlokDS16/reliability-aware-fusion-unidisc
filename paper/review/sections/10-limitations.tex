Preprints are rated in their latest arXiv version; differences from published versions are not measured. % src: corpus/search-log.jsonl; protocol.md section 3 "Version rated"; screening/dedup-report.json

  \item Protocol: not frozen or registered before screening (Section~\ref{sec:method-design}).
  \item Census: limits listed in Section~\ref{sec:census-limits}; text only, no code inspection.
  \item Design space: inclusive vocabulary, 29 of 47 rows not re-read (Section~\ref{sec:design}). % src: design-space-v1.md
  \item Theory: the budget is bookkeeping, not a measured quantity; several statements are checked by exact enumeration on toy models only. % src: framework-v2.md sections B and D
  \item Closed models and non-English work are outside the evidence; positive-result bias in what papers report is not corrected.
  \item AI assistance: all research steps listed in Table~\ref{tab:ai} used language models; models of one family did most of the coding, adjudication and drafting.
\end{itemize}
