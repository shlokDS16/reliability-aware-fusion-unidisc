\subsection{The problems in brief}
\label{sec:open-op1}\label{sec:open-op2}\label{sec:open-op3}\label{sec:open-op4}\label{sec:open-v}
OP1 asks what a step sweep identifies: at one token per step the gain of a position rule over its base is a difference of learning terms, but at more tokens per step it also moves the factorisation term. OP2 asks whether a revision sampler's gain over its base survives when the value rule is matched on both arms. OP3 asks whether a unified model's own cross-modal dependence is concentrated in early steps, and whether schedules that avoid committing dependent text and image positions together score better at matched cost. OP4 is a four-arm measurement on one frozen checkpoint that separates sample-then-filter from its resampled and tilted twins, for which a preregistrable design is given. OP5 brackets the effect of the value term on one model by comparing plain sampling, rank-then-sample, sample-then-filter and best-of-$N$ at matched network calls and matched entropy. The Supplement sets out each problem in full, with the evidence now, the measurement that would settle it and what would refute it.

\subsection{A preregistered check on one frozen unified model}
\label{sec:exp003}

One preregistered run checks, at matched network calls on the released UniDisc 1.4B weights~\cite{arXiv:2503.20853}, what earlier work reports for unified masked-diffusion models~\cite{arXiv:2607.13188,arXiv:2511.09611}: that the sampler changes joint text--image agreement. On 138 prefixes from COCO val2017 captions with two seeds each, a 32-step variant of the official guided sampler (guidance weight 3.0) gives a larger seed-specific, noun-level dependence between a generated caption tail and its image than plain sampling at the same 64 network calls ($\Delta_1=0.126$, 95\% interval 0.076 to 0.177, on the 123 prefixes eligible in both arms). The dependence is measured by a vision-language checker and is not confirmed by CLIP; the checker's positive control failed, so no null can be read, and the positive term bundles selection with ordering and schedule. The run therefore shows only that, on this model, a joint-consistency number reported without its sampler is not attributable to the model, which is how the census is read (Section~\ref{sec:census-reading}). It is not a contribution of the review; the arms, statistic, controls, deviations and secondary results are in the Supplement (Section~S7).
