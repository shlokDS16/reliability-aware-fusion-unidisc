\section{Practical guidance for building, decoding and evaluating diffusion language models}
\label{sec:practice}

This section condenses Sections~\ref{sec:design} to~\ref{sec:budget} for readers who build, decode or evaluate these models. Each recommendation names the evidence it rests on, and none is a measured optimum. Some rest on single studies outside the review's sample; these are suggestions for practice, not findings of the review.

\subsection{How widely used models differ in the five design choices}
\label{sec:practice-exemplars}

Table~\ref{tab:exemplars} in Section~\ref{sec:design} places thirteen widely used models in the five design choices C1 to C5. Most recent large models share the erasure (masking) channel and differ mainly in the commitment policy, choice C4, which fixes which positions are committed (the position rule) and how their values are chosen (the value rule). LLaDA ranks positions by confidence and takes the argmax; MMaDA and UniDisc add guidance; Fast-dLLM commits by an entropy or threshold rule; BD3-LM decodes in blocks with truncated sampling; ReMDM adds remasking. Continuous models (Plaid) and uniform-state models (Duo) update every position at each step and revise implicitly. For Dream~7B, the parts read state neither the position rule nor the value rule of the headline results.

\subsection{Decoding defaults and the evidence behind them}
\label{sec:practice-defaults}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Fix and state the value rule before tuning anything else.} In the census, 147 of 153 headline results use a rule other than plain sampling or leave it unstated (Section~\ref{sec:census}); Section~\ref{sec:budget-value} shows where the rule enters the error budget.
  \item \emph{Do not assume that more parallelism or more steps is free.} In 7 of the 15 sampled studies that sweep steps or tokens per step, at least one curve peaks inside the range or moves both ways (Section~\ref{sec:rq4}). The tokens per step should therefore be swept on the target task with the value rule held fixed. Dependency-aware selection~\cite{arXiv:2604.02560} and learned commitment policies~\cite{arXiv:2605.24697} are two recent alternatives to a single confidence threshold.
  \item \emph{Test remasking rather than assume that it helps.} A re-evaluation of one post-hoc confidence-based remasking method found little or no benefit at short block lengths and only partial help under more stochastic decoding~\cite{arXiv:2606.12232}. Other remaskers have not been re-evaluated this way, so any of them should be compared with confidence-based unmasking at matched network calls.
  \item \emph{Judge a sampler by quality and diversity together.} Low negative log-likelihood or generative perplexity can coexist with an incorrect sampling distribution~\cite{arXiv:2602.19619}, and output entropy can fall far below the data entropy from factorisation error alone, the error of treating positions committed together as independent (Section~\ref{sec:budget}).
  \item \emph{Choose a diffusion model for the constraint it relaxes.} In compute-matched studies, masked diffusion shows a compute gap to autoregressive models~\cite{arXiv:2410.18514} but overtakes autoregressive training when unique data is scarce~\cite{arXiv:2507.15857,arXiv:2511.03276}. Among diffusion families, faster sampling can outweigh worse likelihood on the speed--quality frontier~\cite{arXiv:2602.15014}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{A reporting checklist for diffusion-model results}
\label{sec:practice-checklist}

The census and the extraction sample give the checklist its content and, where they measure it, how often each item is missing.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The value rule of every headline result: temperature, truncation, guidance weight, and any selection among drawn values. The rule is unstated for 50 of the 153 census headlines.
  \item The position rule, the tokens per step or number of steps, and the number of network calls.
  \item For any comparison with an autoregressive model, the parameter count, training tokens and inference budget of both arms, and whether the baseline was run or copied. In the sample, 23 of the 25 studies with such a comparison leave one arm's budget unreported in at least one comparison, and 5 state that they copied the baseline numbers.
  \item Run-to-run variation or seeds for every contrast the paper interprets. In the sample, 3 of the 25 studies report it for the autoregressive comparison.
  \item A quality--diversity frontier, or at least a diversity or entropy measure, beside any single quality number.
  \item For any sweep, which settings were held fixed. Of the 55 census papers that report the headline at two or more step settings, 20 state that the value rule was held fixed.
  \item Code and checkpoint at a stated commit, so that a reader can rerun the headline under another value rule.
\end{enumerate}
