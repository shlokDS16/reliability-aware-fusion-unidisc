Electronic supplement to "Diffusion Language Models and Unified Multimodal Generation: A Critical Systematic Review
of Discrete and Continuous Approaches" (Goenka, Vairavasundaram, G N; ACM Computing Surveys).

The main paper is the 35 pages under review, references included. This supplement (supplement.pdf) holds material
that does not fit in that limit:

S1  PRISMA 2020 checklist and every logged search string with its date and record count.
S2  Screening confusion table of the two model screeners.
S3  Census method in full, gap-filling rulings, coding-choice sensitivity and the full list of census limits.
S4  Agreement of the two extractors on the eight double-read studies.
S5  The 47-model design-space table (Figure S1 and the condensed table) and the limits of the design-space test.
S6  Quality appraisal of each sampled study and the sensitivity of the sample estimates.
S7  Detail of the preregistered measurement on UniDisc (deviations, diversity, controls, statistics).
S8  Text moved from Sections 3, 4, 7 and 8 of the main paper (sampler algorithm, error-budget terms, estimator).

Row-level data (screening rows, census table, extraction records), the analysis scripts and the LaTeX sources are in
the archived repository named in the main paper's data and code availability statement.
